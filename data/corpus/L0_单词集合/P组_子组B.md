## philosopher

### Identity
- lemma: philosopher
- title: philosopher
- pos: n
- ipa: /fəˈlɒsəfə/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: philosophers

### Short Definition
哲学家；思想深邃或冷静思考的人

### Core Definitions
1. 哲学研究者
   - en: a person who studies or develops philosophical ideas
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 寻求真理和智慧的人
   - en: a person devoted to the search for wisdom and universal truth
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 冷静而有原则的思考者
   - en: a person regarded as thoughtful, rational, or detached in outlook
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B

### Prototype
philosopher = 不是自称“智者”，而是持续热爱并追求智慧的人。

### Morphology
- prefix: philo- (loving)
- root: soph- / Greek *sophos* (wise)
- suffix: -er (agent noun ending)
- family: philosopher, philosophy, philosophical, philosophize

### Etymology Narrative

约14世纪初作 philosophre，取代了古英语的 philosophe。经拉丁语 philosophus 溯至希腊语 philosophos（爱智慧者），由 philos（爱……的）加 sophos（智慧的）构成。画面是一个人倾心追求智慧，而不自以为已经拥有智慧。英语的 -er 词尾反映盎格鲁-法语或古法语的施事名词词尾。中世纪时该词亦指炼金术士或术士，后来“有学问的人”这一通义成为主流。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: philo（爱）+ sophia（智慧）→ 打心眼里爱慕智慧的人，一辈子追着道理问下去。

### Semantic Chain
爱 -> 智慧 -> 爱智之人 -> 探理之思 -> 哲学研究者

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/philosopher；early 14c. *philosophre* < Latin *philosophus* < Greek *philosophos* = *philos* + *sophos*.
- The *-er* is an Anglo-French/Old French agent ending; do not treat the form as a regular modern English derivation from *philosophy*.
- No aliases: philosophy and philosophical are family derivatives, not aliases.
- confidence: source-backed
