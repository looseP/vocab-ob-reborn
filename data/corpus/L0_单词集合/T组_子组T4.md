## thirst
### Identity
- lemma: thirst
- title: thirst
- pos: n/v
- ipa: /θɜːrst/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: thirsty
### Short Definition
口渴；渴望
### Core Definitions
1. 需要饮水的感觉
   - en: the need or desire to drink
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 对某事的强烈渴望
   - en: a strong desire for something
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
### Prototype
thirst = 走了半天沙子路，嗓子眼里像着了火

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: thirst-/thurst- / Old English *thyrstan* (be thirsty)
- suffix: none
- family: thirst, thirsty, thirstily, thirstiness
### Etymology Narrative

本词是日耳曼语中指身体对饮水之需要的词。画面是喉咙干得冒烟、只想喝水。身体上缺水的状态，比喻为对知识、体验、权力或别的事物的强烈渴求。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 日耳曼语里指身子要水的那个词 → 喉咙干得冒烟；后来也说对学问、对权位的那股急切。
### Semantic Chain
身子缺水 -> 急欲饮水 -> 渴意难耐 -> 口渴

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/thirst；Old English/Germanic thirst line.
- Physical and figurative desires are retained as related branches.
- `thirsty` is a lookup derivative, not a self-reference.
- confidence: uncertain

## though
### Identity
- lemma: though
- title: though
- pos: conj/adv
- ipa: /ðəʊ/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: although
### Short Definition
虽然；尽管；不过
### Core Definitions
1. 引入让步从句
   - en: introducing a contrast or concession
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 句末表示转折
   - en: used at the end of a sentence to add a contrasting qualification
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
### Prototype
though = 承认一个事实存在，同时把注意力转向与之相反或限制性的事实。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: though-/þēah / Old English *þēah* (though, nevertheless)
- suffix: none
- family: though, although
### Etymology Narrative

源自古英语 þēah（虽然、然而）。画面是一边让一步、一边仍把话顶回去。本词标示让步：承认一种说法，同时仍提出另一种说法作为限定或对照。
### Mnemonic
- type: contrast
- text: 为的是让一句话说出“这固然不假，可另一桩事照样要紧”→ 前后两层摆在一处，各让一步。
### Semantic Chain
纵然 -> 认下实情 -> 转而相抵 -> 虽然

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/though；Old English *þēah*.
- Conjunction and sentence-final adverb uses are grammatical branches of concession.
- `although` is a related conjunction lookup form, not a self-reference.
- confidence: uncertain

## threaten
### Identity
- lemma: threaten
- title: threaten
- pos: v
- ipa: /ˈθretən/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: threat
### Short Definition
威胁；可能造成危险；预示
### Core Definitions
1. 表示要造成伤害
   - en: to state an intention to cause harm
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 构成即将发生的危险
   - en: to present a danger or risk to someone or something
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 预示不利结果
   - en: to seem likely to cause an unwanted result
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
threaten = 把可能的伤害或危险摆到对方面前，使其感到未来被逼近。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: threat-/þreat- / Old English *þreat* (threat, pressure)
- suffix: -en (recognition layer; inherited Old English formation)
- family: threat, threaten, threatening, threatened
### Etymology Narrative

承自古英语 þreatnian，出自 þreat（威胁、逼压）。画面是被人逼到墙角、胸口顶着一只手。口头的恫吓转为对某人呈现出的危险；同一警告框架也推及风暴、损失或其他即将临头的结果。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 古英语 þreatnian，出自 þreat（逼压）→ 被人逼到墙角、胸口顶着一只手；风暴要来也这么说。
### Semantic Chain
逼迫 -> 扬言加害 -> 示以险境 -> 威胁

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/threaten；Old English *þreat* threat/pressure line.
- Speech, danger, and predictive uses are kept as branches of impending harm.
- `threat` is the base noun relation, not a self-reference alias.
- confidence: uncertain

## thrift
### Identity
- lemma: thrift
- title: thrift
- pos: n
- ipa: /θrɪft/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: thrifty
### Short Definition
节俭；节约；繁荣；海石竹
### Core Definitions
1. 节约使用资源
   - en: careful and economical use of money or resources
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 节俭带来的良好经营或繁荣
   - en: prosperity or successful growth through careful management
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 一种植物
   - en: a small flowering plant of coastal habitats
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
thrift = 一件棉袄穿了八年，补丁摞补丁还舍不得扔

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: thrift-/þrīfan- / Old Norse or Germanic thrive family
- suffix: none
- family: thrift, thrifty, thrive, thriving, thriftless
### Etymology Narrative

本词与 thrive 一族相关，本义含“兴旺生长”之意。画面是草木长得茂盛、家业蒸蒸日上。由于细心经营、善于积蓄能致兴旺，遂有现代“节俭”一义；指植物的那个名字则是另一条词汇分支。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 与 thrive 同族，本义是“兴旺、长得茂盛”→ 会经营、善积蓄，家业才一天天往上走。
### Semantic Chain
兴盛 -> 理材有方 -> 省用其资 -> 节约

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/thrift；Germanic/Norse thrive and prosperity line.
- Frugality and prosperity follow the thrive line; the plant sense is retained as a separate modern branch without claiming an unaudited naming origin.
- `thrifty` is a lookup derivative, not a self-reference.
- confidence: uncertain
