## preface

### Identity
- lemma: preface
- title: preface
- pos: n/v
- ipa: /ˈprefəs/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: prefaces, prefaces, prefaced, prefacing

### Short Definition
序言；前言；为文本作序或引介

### Core Definitions
1. 书或演讲的引言
   - en: an introductory section of a book, speech, or discourse
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 序言中的说明
   - en: preliminary remarks that introduce what follows
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 为作品作序
   - en: to introduce a work with preliminary remarks
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C

### Prototype
preface = 先把背景、目的或阅读路线说在前面，为后文搭桥。

### Morphology
- prefix: prae-/pre- (before)
- root: fat- / Latin *fari* (to speak)
- suffix: -ion (action/result noun layer)
- family: preface, prefatory, prefatorily

### Etymology Narrative

名词约14世纪末出现，经古法语 preface 溯自中世纪拉丁语 praefatia 及拉丁语 praefationem，意为“预先的言说、引言”，出自动词 praefari（事先说），历史上可析为 prae-（在前）加 fari（说）。画面是在正题之前先说几句。动词“以开场白引入”一义始于1610年代。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: prae-（在前）+ fari（说）→ 正文开张之前先说的那几句话，替全书起个头。

### Semantic Chain
事前言说 -> 引出 -> 为书立框 -> 引言

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/preface；late 14c. < Old French *preface* < Medieval Latin *praefatia* < Latin *praefari*.
- *prae-* + *fari* is a historical Latin analysis, not a claim about a productive modern English split.
- No aliases; prefatory is a related derivative, not an alias.
- confidence: source-backed

### Reviewer Notes
