## opponent

### Identity
- lemma: opponent
- title: opponent
- pos: n
- ipa: /əˈpəʊnənt/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: opponents

### Short Definition
对手；反对者

### Core Definitions
1. 竞争或对抗中的对手
   - en: a person or team that competes against another
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 持相反意见或立场的人
   - en: a person who opposes an idea, argument, or action
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
opponent = 站在另一方、与某人或某个主张相对的人或队伍。

### Morphology
- prefix: ob- / assimilated op- (against, in the way of)
- root: pon- / Latin *ponere* (put, set, place)
- suffix: -ent (present-participial layer)
- family: oppose, opposition, position, opponent

### Etymology Narrative

名词约1580年代出现，源自拉丁语 opponentem（opponere 的现在分词），意为“反对、抗辩”，字面为“置于对面、放在对立处”。拉丁语 opponere 由同化了的 ob- 加 ponere（放置）构成。画面是把棋子摆在对面与你对弈。通义由较早的“辩论中持相反论点者”一义发展而来。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: ob-（相对）+ ponere（放置）→ 把棋子摆到你正对面，隔着棋盘与你对坐的那个人。

### Semantic Chain
摆在对面 -> 使其相争 -> 对抗之人 -> 对手

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/opponent；1580s，Latin *opponentem* < *opponere* “set against” < assimilated *ob-* + *ponere*.
- The final `-nt` is the historical participial layer, not a freely productive English suffix analysis.
- `opposition` and `position` are family members, not aliases; aliases is `none`.
- confidence: source-backed

### Prototype

- root: optim- / Latin *optimus* (best, very good)
- suffix: -um (Latin neuter singular ending)
- family: optimum, optimal, optimally, optimization

### Etymology Narrative

名词始见于1879年，直接取自拉丁语 optimum，是 optimus（最好、极好）的中性单数形式，用作 bonus（好）的最高级。画面是“好到了顶”。其更深的关联有争议：或关 ops（力量、资财），或关 ob-（在前）。英语最初用于生物学，指最有利于生长或代谢的条件；形容词用法1885年跟进。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 拉丁语 optimus 是 bonus（好）的最高级 → 好到了顶上，再往上也没有了的那个点。

### Semantic Chain
良善 -> 至善至好 -> 最利之况 -> 最适宜

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/optimum；1879，Latin neuter *optimum* < *optimus* “best.”
- The deeper relation of *optimus* is presented as perhaps *ops* or perhaps *ob-*; no single deeper root is asserted.
- `-um` is a Latin inflection, not a productive English suffix; optimal and optimization are later family formations.
- confidence: uncertain

### Core Definitions
1. 最初存在或发生的
   - en: existing or occurring first in time
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 直接由创作者产生的
   - en: produced directly by an author, artist, or creator
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 新颖而非模仿的
   - en: new and not copied or derived from another work
   - priority: 3

4. 最初文本、物件或版本
   - en: the first or authentic text, object, or version
   - priority: 4
   - tags: B

### Prototype

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: origin- / Latin *origo* (beginning, source, birth)
- suffix: -al (adjectival layer)
- family: origin, original, originally, originate

### Etymology Narrative

形容词约14世纪初出现，源自古法语 original（最初的），并直接出自拉丁语 originalis，溯至 originem（起始、源头、出生），来自动词 oriri（升起）。画面是一事物从源头涌出之处，故为“原本、最初”。“由作者或艺术家直接创作的”一义始见于1630年代，“新颖、别出心裁”一义约1782年。名词指源头或原文，发展于14世纪末。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: oriri（升起）→ 离泉眼最近的那捧水：时辰上最靠前，也是头一个做出来的。

### Semantic Chain
源起 -> 时序最先 -> 首制之作 -> 最初

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/original；early 14c. < Old French *original* and Latin *originalis* < *originem* < *oriri*.
- “Original” develops from source/firstness to creator-made and then novel; these are related semantic extensions.
- `-al` is a historical adjectival layer; original and origin are family members, not aliases.
- confidence: source-backed
