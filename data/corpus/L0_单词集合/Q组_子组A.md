## qualification
### Identity
- lemma: qualification
- title: qualification
- pos: n
- ipa: /kwɒlɪfɪˈkeɪʃən/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: qualifications
### Short Definition
资格；资历；限定条件；合格
### Core Definitions
1. 资格；资历
   - en: an ability, certificate, or experience that makes someone suitable
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 限定条件；修正说明
   - en: a condition or statement that limits or modifies a claim
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
### Prototype
qualification = 把一个人或一个说法限定到“符合什么条件”的范围内。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: qual / Latin *qualis* (of what kind)
- suffix: -ification (causative/action noun layer)
- family: qualify, qualification, qualified, quality
### Etymology Narrative

本词由 qualify 加 -ation 构成。qualify 经法语溯自中世纪拉丁语 qualificare（赋予某种性质），出自 qualis（何种性质的）。画面是给一样东西定下它属于哪一类。其义由“赋予性质”，转为使某人合格胜任，再转为一种限制条件或资格凭证。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: qualis（哪一类的）+ -fy + -ation → 先定下它算哪一类、成色如何，再据此发给能胜任的凭据。
### Semantic Chain
哪一类 -> 赋以资质 -> 使之合格 -> 资格

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/qualification；qualification < qualify + -ation；qualify < Medieval Latin *qualificare* < *qualis*。
- 区分个人资历与对命题的限定说明；`qualifications` 为复数形式。
- confidence: source-backed

## qualitative
### Identity
- lemma: qualitative
- title: qualitative
- pos: adj
- ipa: /ˈkwɒlɪtətɪv/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: qualitatively
### Short Definition
定性的；质量方面的；性质的
### Core Definitions
1. 定性的；描述性质的
   - en: relating to qualities or characteristics rather than quantities
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 质量的；性质上的
   - en: relating to the nature or character of something
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
### Prototype
qualitative = 关注“是什么样”而不是只数“有多少”。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: quality / Latin *qualitas* (quality, nature)
- suffix: -ative (adjectival layer)
- family: quality, qualify, qualitative, qualitatively, qualification
### Etymology Narrative

本词由 quality 加形容词层次构成。quality 经古法语溯自拉丁语 qualitas（性质、品质），出自 qualis（何种性质的）。画面是掂量一样东西属于哪一类、成色如何。故 qualitative 与对数量的计量相对，重在描述性质特征。
### Mnemonic
- type: contrast
- text: qualis（哪一类的）→ 问的是“什么样”，而非“有多少”；与 quantitative 对举着记最省事。
### Semantic Chain
哪一类 -> 关乎性质 -> 描其特质 -> 定性

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/qualitative；qualitative < quality + -ative；quality < Latin *qualitas* < *qualis*。
- 不把 `-tative` 当作现代自由后缀；保留 quality 的历史借入链。
- confidence: source-backed

## quest
### Identity
- lemma: quest
- title: quest
- pos: n/v
- ipa: /kwest/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: quests, quested, questing
### Short Definition
寻找；探索；任务；追寻
### Core Definitions
1. 寻找；探索
   - en: a search for something valuable or important
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 任务；追寻目标
   - en: a long or difficult search undertaken as a goal
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 探索；寻找
   - en: to search for or pursue something
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
quest = 他背着干粮往山里走，说要找到那眼泉

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: quest / Latin *quaerere* (seek, ask)
- suffix: none
- family: quest, question, query, request, conquest
### Etymology Narrative

经古法语 queste 溯自拉丁语 quaerere（寻求、取得、询问）。画面是回头再去找、非要找到不可。名词最初指一次寻求的行为或一次查问，后指艰苦的搜寻或使命；动词沿用同一“搜寻”意象。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: quaerere（寻求、询问）→ 把心里那个问号，走成了一条非找到不可的长路。
### Semantic Chain
追问 -> 多方探问 -> 苦苦搜寻 -> 寻找

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/quest；Old French *queste* < Latin *quaerere*。
- `question/query/request` 是相关词族，不放入 aliases；保留 search 与 mission 两条义项。
- confidence: source-backed

## quick
### Identity
- lemma: quick
- title: quick
- pos: adj/adv/n
- ipa: /kwɪk/
- cefr: A2
- aliases: quickly, quicker, quickest
### Short Definition
快的；迅速地；活着的；敏捷的
### Core Definitions
1. 快的；迅速的
   - en: moving or happening at a high speed
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 活着的；有生命的
   - en: alive or living
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 敏捷的；反应快的
   - en: able to understand, react, or act rapidly
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
quick = 生命在动、反应在发生，于是“活着”逐渐连接到动作迅速和反应敏捷。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: quick / Old English *cwic* (alive, living) < Proto-Germanic *kwikwaz*
- suffix: none
- family: quick, quickly, quicken, quickness, alive
### Etymology Narrative

源自古英语 cwic（活的、有生命的），出自原始日耳曼语 *kwikwaz，关涉一个表示“生命”的词根。较古的意义保留在 the quick and the dead（生者与死者）与 quick flesh（嫩肉）中；画面是活物会动、会挣扎。“迅速、反应快”一义，正由活着、能动的生命力发展而来。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 古英语 cwic 本义是“活的”（the quick and the dead）→ 活物会动、会挣扎，那份灵动后来就成了快。
### Semantic Chain
活的 -> 活肉跳动 -> 反应敏捷 -> 快的、迅速的

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/quick；Old English *cwic* < Proto-Germanic *kwikwaz*；speed sense developed from living vitality。
- 不把 `qu-` 当作英语前缀；“the quick and the dead”保留旧的 alive 义。
- confidence: source-backed
