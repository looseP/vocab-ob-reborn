## tend
### Identity
- lemma: tend
- title: tend
- pos: v
- ipa: /tend/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: tendency
### Short Definition
倾向；趋向；照料
### Core Definitions
1. 有某种倾向
   - en: to be likely to behave or develop in a particular way
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 朝某方向发展
   - en: to move or develop toward a direction or result
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 照料或看护
   - en: to care for or attend to someone or something
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
tend = 他每天早晚两遍，给那几畦菜浇水

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: tend- / Latin *tendere* (stretch, aim)
- suffix: none
- family: tend, tendency, tending, attendant
### Etymology Narrative

表“趋向”的 tend 与拉丁语 tendere（伸展、指向）相关，经法语与拉丁语形式而来。画面是枝条朝着有光的方向伸展过去。“倾向”即某物所朝之方向。而表“照料、看护”的 tend 是另一条历史分支，关涉专心照管某人或某事。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 拉丁语 tendere（伸展、指向）→ 枝条朝着有光的一边伸过去；照看病人那一支，另出一源。
### Semantic Chain
拉向 -> 指向目标 -> 渐成其势 -> 倾向

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/tend；Latin *tendere* direction line and a separate care branch.
- Direction and care meanings are retained as branches; they are not forced into one modern suffix analysis.
- `tendency` is used as a lookup relation, not a self-reference alias.
- confidence: uncertain

   - priority: 1

2. 用来试探的
   - en: intended as a trial or test
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
### Prototype
tentative = 先伸手试一下而不完全承诺，因此方案仍可修改或撤回。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: tent-/tempt- / Latin *tentare* (try, test)
- suffix: -ive (adjectival layer)
- family: tentative, tentatively, tempt, temptation, attempt
### Etymology Narrative

本词建立在拉丁语一条关涉“尝试、试验”的线索上，与 tentare（试、检验）相关。画面是先伸出手探一探、摸着石头过河。tentative 的行为是一次试探，而非最终的承诺；现代该形容词描述的是仍可更改的提议、结论或安排。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 与 tentare（试、检验）相关 → 先伸手探一探，摸着石头过河：这一步还改得动。
### Semantic Chain

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/tentative；Latin *tentare* try/test line.
- The exact relation among *tentare*, *tempt*, and *attempt* is left cautious; confidence remains uncertain.
- `tentatively` is a derivative lookup form, not a self-reference.

### Short Definition
终止；结束；使停止
### Core Definitions
1. 使某事结束
   - en: to bring something to an end
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 到达终点
   - en: to end or come to a final limit
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 终止合同或关系
   - en: to end an agreement, employment, or relationship
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
terminate = 把运动或过程带到边界处，使它不能继续前进。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: termin- / Latin *terminus* (boundary, limit)
- suffix: -ate (recognition layer; whole Latin borrowing)
- family: term, terminate, termination, terminal, terminology
### Etymology Narrative

源自拉丁语 terminare（划定界限、限定），出自 terminus（边界、界限）。画面是田边立着的那块界石——走到此处为止。有界限的过程会走到它的尽头，遂生出现代终止某项行动、合同、雇佣或关系诸义。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: 拉丁语 terminus 本是田边那块界石 → 划下界线，走到此处为止，往后再没有了。
### Semantic Chain
界限 -> 定下尽头 -> 行至末端 -> 终止

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/terminate；Latin *terminare* < *terminus*.
- Physical boundary and administrative ending are retained as one limit-based semantic line.
- `termination` is a derivational lookup relation, not a self-reference alias.
- confidence: uncertain

## theoretical
### Identity
- lemma: theoretical
- title: theoretical
- pos: adj
- ipa: /θɪəˈretɪkəl/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: theoretically
### Short Definition
理论的；推测的；非实践的
### Core Definitions
1. 与理论或原理有关的
   - en: relating to ideas, principles, or theories
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 可能成立但尚未实践验证的
   - en: possible in principle but not yet tested in practice
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 偏抽象而非实际操作的
   - en: concerned with abstract explanation rather than practical action
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
theoretical = 先从观察中退一步进行思考，用一套解释原则组织现象，而不立即操作。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: theoretic- / Late Latin *theoreticus* < Greek *theoretikos* (contemplative)
- suffix: -al (English adjectival formation)
- family: theory, theoretical, theoretically, theorize, theorist
### Etymology Narrative

由晚期拉丁语 theoreticus 加英语后缀 -al 构成，最终溯自希腊语 theōrein（观看、沉思）。画面是站在一旁静静端详、把道理看出来。本词描述的是解释性的原理，后来又用来标示理论上可行与经实践检验过者之间的对照。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: 希腊语 theōrein 本义是“观看、沉思”→ 退开一步静静端详，把背后的道理看明白。
### Semantic Chain
静观玄思 -> 说理释原 -> 成其理论 -> 理论

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/theoretical；Late Latin *theoreticus* + English *-al*, ultimately Greek *theōrein*.
- Abstract explanation and the theory/practice contrast are retained as related branches.
- `theoretically` is a derivative lookup form, not a self-reference.
- confidence: uncertain
