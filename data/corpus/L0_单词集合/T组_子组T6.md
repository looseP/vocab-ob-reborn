# T组 · 子组T6（L1 生产词条）

## token
### Identity
- lemma: token
- title: token
- pos: n/adj
- ipa: /ˈtəʊkən/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: tokens, tokenized, tokenization
### Short Definition
标志；象征；代币；令牌；象征性的
### Core Definitions
1. 代表某种意义、关系或事实的标志或信物
   - en: a sign, object, or act that represents something else
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 可兑换、计数或取得权限的代币、筹码或数字令牌
   - en: a physical or digital unit used for exchange, counting, or access
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 仅具象征意义或数量很少的
   - en: done only as a symbol or involving a very small representation
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
token = 一个可见或可处理的记号，代表某种身份、价值、承诺或信息单位。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: token-/tacen / Old English *tacen* (also written *tācen*; sign, symbol, evidence, portent)
- suffix: none
- family: token, betoken, tokenize, tokenization
### Etymology Narrative

本词是日耳曼语本族词，出自古英语 tacen（亦作 tācen），意为“记号、象征、证据、征兆”，源自原始日耳曼语 *taikna-。画面是举在手里作为凭据的一件东西——见物即知有其事。由具体的记号或代表物，发展出像钱币那样的代用券，再按同样的“代表一个单位”的逻辑，引出语言学与计算机上的“词元、令牌”。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 古英语 tacen，本义是“记号、证据、征兆”→ 举在手里作凭据的那一小件：见物便知有其事。
### Semantic Chain
记号凭证 -> 代言之物 -> 币状之代 -> 信物

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/token；Old English *tacen* (also written *tācen*) < Proto-Germanic *\*taikna-*.
- The false `to + ken` split and Tolkien/password folk associations are rejected; computing uses are later semantic extensions, not a separate ancient origin.
- Aliases contain inflected or derived lookup forms and do not repeat the lemma.
- confidence: uncertain

## tolerate
### Identity
- lemma: tolerate
- title: tolerate
- pos: v
- ipa: /ˈtɒləreɪt/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: tolerates, tolerated, tolerating
### Short Definition
容忍；忍受；耐受
### Core Definitions
1. 容许不喜欢或不赞成的人、行为或观点存在
   - en: to allow something disliked or disapproved of without interference
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 忍受不适、痛苦或困难
   - en: to endure discomfort, pain, or difficulty
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 能承受药物、环境或其他条件而无严重不良影响
   - en: to withstand a drug, condition, or environment without harmful effect
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
tolerate = 承受一个不理想的对象或条件，但不因此制止、崩溃或受到严重损害。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: toler-/tolerare / Latin *tolerare* (bear, endure, sustain)
- suffix: -ate (recognition layer; whole Latin borrowing)
- family: tolerance, tolerant, tolerable, intolerable
### Etymology Narrative

源自拉丁语 toleratus（tolerare“忍受、支撑、承托、受苦”的过去分词），字面即“承载”。画面是肩头扛着重物、咬牙撑住。由承受艰难，发展出容许自己不喜之物存在，以及医学或生物学上抵御某种状况的能力。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: 拉丁语 tolerare 字面是“承载”→ 肩头扛着重物、咬牙撑住；也就容得下自己不喜欢的那些。
### Semantic Chain
承负 -> 耐其苦 -> 容而弗禁 -> 容忍

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/tolerate；Latin *toleratus* < *tolerare* “endure, sustain, support, suffer.”
- `-ate` is a modern recognition layer on a whole Latin borrowing; no English `to-` prefix is claimed.
- `toll` is not placed in this family: its fee and bell histories follow separate lines despite a possible remote PIE comparison for the fee theory.
- confidence: source-backed

## toll
### Identity
- lemma: toll
- title: toll
- pos: n/v
- ipa: /təʊl/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: tolls, tolled, tolling
### Short Definition
通行费；损失或伤亡；缓慢鸣钟
### Core Definitions
1. 使用道路、桥梁或其他设施所付的费用
   - en: a charge paid for using a road, bridge, or other facility
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 某事造成的损失、伤害或死亡人数
   - en: the damage, suffering, or number of deaths caused by something
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 缓慢而逐下地敲钟；钟的一下声响
   - en: to sound a bell in slow single strokes, or one such stroke
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
toll = 过桥要交钱，挑担的也得交

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: toll / separate fee and bell lines; ultimate origins disputed or obscure
- suffix: none
- family: tollbooth, tollgate (fee line); tolled, tolling (bell line)
### Etymology Narrative

指费用的名词经中古英语 tol 溯自古英语 toll（税、贡赋、过路钱、租金）；其更深的来源有争议，或说是日耳曼语早期借自晚期拉丁语 tolonium，或说是日耳曼语本有之词。敲钟义的动词中古英语作 tollen，大概是一个意为“拉、引诱、招引”的冷僻动词的专用化用法；take its toll 一语则是把“费用”这一线引申为“索取去的代价”。
### Mnemonic
- type: contrast
- text: 两支要分开：过路钱溯自古英语 toll（税、贡赋），敲钟一支中古英语作 tollen，是“拉、引”→ 关卡上抽的那笔。
### Semantic Chain
贡赋 -> 过路抽费 -> 所耗之数 -> 通行费

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/toll；fee noun and bell verb are separate entries with disputed or obscure deeper origins.
- The loss/death sense is tied conservatively to figurative `take its toll` “exact a cost” (by 1910), not to the bell line.
- No bridge-rope collector story, `tell` certainty, or false link to `tolerate` is used.
- confidence: uncertain

## topic
### Identity
- lemma: topic
- title: topic
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈtɒpɪk/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: topics, topical, topically
### Short Definition
话题；主题；论题
### Core Definitions
1. 谈话、讨论或写作所围绕的主题
   - en: a subject discussed, written about, or studied
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 逻辑或修辞中可据以展开论证的论题或范畴
   - en: a heading or class of considerations used in logic or rhetoric
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
### Prototype
topic = 一晚上大家都在说粮价，谁也没提别的事

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: top-/topos / Greek *topos* (place)
- suffix: -ic (recognition layer; whole Greek-Latin borrowing)
- family: topical, topically, topos, topography
### Etymology Narrative

经拉丁语 Topica 与法语的学术形式 topique，溯自希腊语 Ta Topika（通用论题），基于 topikos（属于地方的、本地的）与 topos（地方）。画面是修辞学上存放论点的那些“位子”——按题头一处一处去找话讲。由这种修辞上的“处所”或题头，到1720年转为言说或写作中所论的主题。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: 希腊语 topos（地方）→ 修辞学上存放论点的那些“位子”，按题头一处一处找话讲。
### Semantic Chain
安放处 -> 常谈之料 -> 立题以论 -> 话题

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/topic；Latin *Topica* < Greek *Ta Topika*, *topikos* < *topos* “place.”
- The French transmission form *topique* is retained conservatively; sources differ on whether English borrowed immediately from French or Latin.
- `top-` here means Greek “place,” not English `top` “highest part”; `-ic` is a recognition layer, and `topical` is topic + `-al`, not the source structure of `topic`.
- confidence: uncertain

## toward
### Identity
- lemma: toward
- title: toward
- pos: prep/adj
- ipa: /təˈwɔːd/
- cefr: A2
- aliases: towards, towardly, towardness
### Short Definition
向；朝；接近；对于；有利的
### Core Definitions
1. 朝某人、某物或某个方向
   - en: in the direction of someone or something
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 接近某个时间、数量、目标或结果
   - en: near or moving closer to a time, amount, goal, or result
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 对于；关于、有利或顺遂的
   - en: in relation to, or rarely favorable and promising
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
toward = 把空间箭头指向目标，再把同一方向感扩展到时间、结果和态度。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: to / Old English *to* (in the direction of)
- suffix: -ward (directional element; Old English *-weard*)
- family: towards, untoward, towardly, towardness
### Etymology Narrative

源自古英语 toweard（即将到来的、朝向的、临近的），字面为 to 加 -ward。画面是面朝着某处、一步步走近。空间上的方向约1500年起推及时间上的临近；towards 在古英语中作 toweards，只是加了一个副词的属格词尾，并非新的词根。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: 古英语 toweard = to + -ward → 面朝着某处一步步走近；挪到时辰上说，就是临近了。
### Semantic Chain
指其方向 -> 面向趋近 -> 临于其境 -> 朝向

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/toward and https://www.etymonline.com/word/towards；Old English *toweard* = *to* + *-weard*; *toweards* adds an adverbial genitive ending.
- The historical structure is `to + -ward`, never `to + w + ard`; `towards` is listed as a variant relation without repeating the lemma in aliases.
- Rare adjective senses are retained behind the common prepositional direction senses.
- confidence: source-backed
