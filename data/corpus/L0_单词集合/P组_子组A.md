## panel

### Identity
- lemma: panel
- title: panel
- pos: n/v
- ipa: /ˈpænəl/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: panels, panels, panelled, panelling

### Short Definition
面板；专家小组；评审组；镶板

### Core Definitions
1. 平面的一块面板
   - en: a distinct section of a wall, door, or surface
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 专家或评审小组
   - en: a group of people assembled to advise, judge, or discuss something
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 作为小组成员参加讨论或评审
   - en: to take part in a panel discussion or serve on a panel
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C

### Prototype
panel = 把一块独立的布料或表面区域看作一个可分辨的单元，后来扩展为由人组成的评审单元。

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: pann- / Latin *pannus* (piece of cloth)
- suffix: -el (Latin diminutive layer in *pannellus*)
- family: panel, paneling, panelist, empanel

### Etymology Narrative

名词约14世纪初出现，原指“一块布”，源自古法语 panel，出自俗拉丁语 pannellus，是拉丁语 pannus（一块布）的指小词。画面是一小块布幅，后指镶在框架里的一块板。陪审团一义出自被召集服役者的名单；“顾问小组”一义始于1570年代，墙壁或门上的“镶板”义约1600年。动词约15世纪中叶作“遴选陪审员”，后亦指“装上镶板”。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 拉丁语 pannus 本义是一块布（指小词 pannellus）→ 一小幅布片，后来成了镶在框子里的一块板。

### Semantic Chain
布片 -> 分明一块 -> 陪审之列 -> 板面之片 -> 面板

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/panel；early 14c. < Old French *panel* < Vulgar Latin *pannellus* < Latin *pannus*.
- Jury and wall senses are historical semantic branches from the idea of a distinct piece or listed unit.
- No aliases; paneling and panelist are derivatives, not aliases.
- confidence: source-backed

## panorama

- title: panorama

- cefr: B2

### Short Definition
全景；全貌；概览

### Core Definitions
1. 全景画或全景图
   - en: a wide picture or display showing an extensive view
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 全景景象
   - en: a complete or comprehensive view of a place or scene
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 全面概览
   - en: a comprehensive survey of a subject or situation
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C

### Prototype

### Morphology
- prefix: pan- (all)
- root: horam / Greek *horama* (sight, spectacle)
- suffix: none (Greek compound)
- family: panorama, panoramic, diorama

### Etymology Narrative

1796年已有记载，约1789年由罗伯特·巴克（Robert Barker）创制，字面意为“完整的视野”。由 pan-（全）加希腊语 horama（景象、奇观）构成，后者出自动词 horan（看、视）。画面是环顾一周、尽收眼底。“综合概览”这一现代义1801年已有记录。horan 更深的 PIE 关联只是可能之说，故不作定论。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: pan-（全）+ 希腊语 horama（景象）→ 站定了转一整圈，把四面八方的景致一次收进眼里。

### Semantic Chain
尽收眼底 -> 完整景象 -> 环转宽画 -> 全景

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/panorama；1796 < coined c.1789, *pan-* + Greek *horama*.
- Direct Greek formation is clear; the possible PIE link of *horan* is limited and remains uncertain.
- No aliases; panoramic and diorama are related forms, not aliases.
- confidence: uncertain

### Mnemonic

- No aliases; paragraphic is a derivative.

- type: image

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/parallel；1540s < French < Latin *parallelus* < Greek *parallēlos*.
- The Greek compound explains the historical form; modern English does not freely build the word from these pieces.
- No aliases; parallelism and parallelize are derivatives.
- confidence: source-backed
