# T组 · 子组T7（L1 生产词条）

## towards
### Identity
- lemma: towards
- title: towards
- pos: prep
- ipa: /təˈwɔːdz/
- cefr: A2
- aliases: toward
### Short Definition
向；朝；接近；对于
### Core Definitions
1. 朝某人或某物的方向
   - en: in the direction of someone or something
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 接近某个时间、数量或状态
   - en: near or approaching a time, amount, or state
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 对于；关于、以……为目标
   - en: in relation to or in support of a person, subject, or goal
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
towards = 一条路径朝目标延伸，也可表示时间、数量、态度或行动正向某个点靠近。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: toward-/Old English *toweard* (coming, facing, approaching)
- suffix: -s (historical adverbial genitive ending)
- family: toward, towards
### Etymology Narrative

源自古英语 toweards，建立在 toweard（即将到来的、朝向的、临近的）加一个副词属格词尾之上。画面是面朝着某处、一步步走近。空间上朝向某地，推及时间或数量上的接近，以及朝向某一主题或目标的取向。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 古英语 toweards，比 toward 多一个副词属格词尾 → 箭尖指着靶子，一路都往那个点去。
### Semantic Chain
面向其向 -> 趋近标的 -> 临于一点 -> 朝向

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/towards；Old English *toweards* from *toweard* plus an adverbial genitive ending.
- `toward` is the cognate spelling without this historical `-s` layer; regional BrE/AmE preference is usage, not the source of the ending.
- `toward` is a lookup spelling, not a self-reference.
- confidence: source-backed

## transfer
### Identity
- lemma: transfer
- title: transfer
- pos: v/n
- ipa: /trænsˈfɜːr/; /ˈtrænsfɜːr/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: transference, transferable
### Short Definition
转移；调动；转让；换乘
### Core Definitions
1. 把人或物从一处转到另一处
   - en: to move someone or something from one place or position to another
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 转让权利、财产、资金或控制权
   - en: to pass rights, property, money, or control to another person or account
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 换乘；转车、调动
   - en: a change from one vehicle, route, school, or job position to another
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
transfer = 把某人、某物或某种权利“带过边界”，交到另一个地点、载体或持有者手中。
### Morphology
- prefix: trans- (across, beyond)
- root: fer- / Latin *ferre* (carry)
- suffix: none
- family: transfer, transference, transferable, transferee, translation (same Latin *transferre*, suppletive *lat-* stem)
### Etymology Narrative

经古法语 transferer，或直接出自拉丁语 transferre（运过去、携往对岸），由 trans-（跨越）加 ferre（携带）构成。画面是把东西扛过河、交到对岸。物理上的搬运过去，扩大为移交财产、信息、人员，以及交通上的换乘转乘。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: trans-（跨越）+ ferre（携带）→ 把东西扛过河、交到对岸；换个手、改个主、转笔账，都是这个动作。
### Semantic Chain
搬运而过 -> 易地而移 -> 转交其主 -> 转移

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/transfer；Old French *transferer* / Latin *transferre* from *trans* + *ferre*.
- `translation` is included only because both descend from Latin *transferre*; its *lat-* is the verb's suppletive past-participle stem, not the surface *fer-* stem.
- Words sharing only `trans-`, including `transient` and `transmission`, are not treated as family.
- confidence: source-backed

## transient
### Identity
- lemma: transient
- title: transient
- pos: adj/n
- ipa: /ˈtrænziənt/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: transience, transiently
### Short Definition
短暂的；过往的；暂住者
### Core Definitions
1. 持续时间很短的
   - en: lasting for only a short time
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 经过某地但不久留的
   - en: passing through a place without staying long
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 暂住者；短暂停留的人
   - en: a person who stays in a place only briefly
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
transient = 某物像过客一样经过，很快便离开，因此既可指短暂现象，也可指短暂停留的人。
### Morphology
- prefix: trans- (across, beyond)
- root: i- / Latin *ire* (go)
- suffix: -ent (present-participle/adjectival layer via Latin *transientem*)
- family: transient, transience, transiently, transition, transitory
### Etymology Narrative

源自拉丁语 transientem（主格 transiens，意为“越过的、逝去的”），是 transire 的现在分词，由 trans-（跨越）加 ire（走）构成。画面是一阵风从这边吹到那边、片刻不停留。凡越过去、走掉的东西都不会留下，故有“短暂的、转瞬即逝的”一义。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: trans-（穿过）+ ire（走）→ 旅人穿过驿站，脚不停留：来了就走，不肯久住。
### Semantic Chain
越而过 -> 行经即走 -> 片刻不留 -> 短暂

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/transient；Latin *transientem/transiens* from *transire* = *trans-* + *ire*.
- The direct root here is Latin `i-` from *ire*; related family words may show the past-participle stem `it-`, but it is not assigned to this word’s surface root.
- Shared `trans-` does not make `transfer`, `translation`, or `transmission` members of this family.
- confidence: source-backed

## translation
### Identity
- lemma: translation
- title: translation
- pos: n
- ipa: /trænsˈleɪʃən/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: translate, translational
### Short Definition
翻译；译文；转化；平移
### Core Definitions
1. 把文字或话语译成另一种语言的行为或结果
   - en: the process or result of rendering words in another language
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 把思想、发现或事物转化到另一种形式或领域
   - en: the conversion of an idea, discovery, or thing into another form or context
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 不旋转地移动图形或物体
   - en: movement of a figure or object without rotation
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
translation = 他把那页英文一句句换成中文，前后改了三稿

### Morphology
- prefix: trans- (across, beyond)
- root: lat- / Latin *latus* (borne, carried; suppletive past participle of *ferre*)
- suffix: -ion (recognition layer; whole borrowing via Old French *translacion* / Latin *translationem*)
- family: translation, translate, translational, mistranslation, transfer (same Latin *transferre*, surface *fer-* stem)
### Etymology Narrative

经古法语 translacion 或拉丁语 translationem 而来，意为“运送过去、移走、意义的转移”，由拉丁语 transferre 的过去分词词干构成。词中的 lat- 反映 latus（被承载的），那是 ferre 的异干过去分词。画面是把一件东西从这边搬到那边。故其历史不只是现代英语的 translate 加 -tion。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: trans-（跨越）+ lat-（latus，被承载的）→ 把意思扛过语言的边界，在另一头重新说出来。
### Semantic Chain
搬运而过 -> 转其含义 -> 另语出之 -> 翻译

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/translation and https://www.etymonline.com/word/translate；Old French *translacion*, Latin *translationem*, and *translatus* from *transferre*.
- `transfer` and `translation` are cautiously cross-listed because they continue different stems of the same Latin verb: *fer-* versus suppletive *lat-*.
- `transmission` is excluded: its root is Latin *mittere*, not *ferre*.
- confidence: source-backed

## transmission
### Identity
- lemma: transmission
- title: transmission
- pos: n
- ipa: /trænzˈmɪʃən/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: transmit, transmitter
### Short Definition
传送；传播；播送；变速器
### Core Definitions
1. 信息、信号、能量或疾病的传送与传播
   - en: the sending or spreading of information, signals, energy, or disease
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 广播或播送的内容或过程
   - en: a broadcast or the process of broadcasting
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 把发动机动力传给车轴的变速器
   - en: the vehicle system that passes power from the engine to the axle
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
transmission = 把某物发送过去，并让信息、能量、疾病或动力从一端传到另一端。
### Morphology
- prefix: trans- (across, beyond)
- root: miss-/mit- / Latin *mittere* (send, let go; *miss-* past-participle stem)
- suffix: -ion (recognition layer; whole borrowing from Latin *transmissionem*)
- family: transmission, transmit, transmitter, retransmission
### Etymology Narrative

源自拉丁语 transmissionem（送过去、越过、通过），来自 transmittere 的过去分词词干，由 trans-（跨越）加 mittere（送、放行）构成。画面是把东西送到河对岸、交到另一头。“送过去”这一意象，推及资讯传达、信号发送、疾病传播，以及传递动力的机械装置。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: trans-（跨越）+ mittere（送、放行）→ 把东西送到河对岸、交到另一头；信号、病症也这么递过去。
### Semantic Chain
发送过境 -> 递相传去 -> 传讯传能 -> 传播

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/transmission and https://www.etymonline.com/word/transmit；Latin *transmissionem/transmissio* from *transmittere*.
- `miss-/mit-` records the past-participle and verb stems of Latin *mittere*; it is unrelated to the *fer-/lat-* stems in `transfer` and `translation`.
- Shared `trans-` alone does not justify adding those words to this family.
- confidence: source-backed
