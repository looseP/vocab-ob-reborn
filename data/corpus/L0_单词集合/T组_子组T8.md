## transmit
### Identity
- lemma: transmit
- title: transmit
- pos: v
- ipa: /trænzˈmɪt/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: transmitter, retransmit
### Short Definition
传送；传输；传播；播送
### Core Definitions
1. 把信息、信号或能量传到另一处
   - en: to send information, signals, or energy to another person or place
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 传播疾病、特征或影响
   - en: to pass a disease, characteristic, or influence from one person or thing to another
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 广播或播送节目
   - en: to broadcast a programme or signal
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
transmit = 把某物发送越过距离或媒介，使它从一端到达另一端。
### Morphology
- prefix: trans- (across, beyond)
- root: mit-/miss- / Latin *mittere* (send, let go; *miss-* past-participle stem)
- suffix: none
- family: transmit, transmission, transmitter, retransmit
### Etymology Narrative

源自拉丁语 transmittere（送过去、使越过、传递），由 trans-（跨越）加 mittere（释放、放行；派遣）构成。画面是把信交出去、让它渡到对岸。物理上的向前传送，扩大为传达资讯，以及传播信号、疾病或其他作用。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: trans-（跨越）+ mittere（放行、派遣）→ 把信交出去，让它渡到对岸；消息、能量都这么走。
### Semantic Chain
发出 -> 越而送之 -> 传于彼端 -> 传送

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/transmit；c. 1400 *transmitten* from Latin *transmittere*.
- `mit-` is the verb stem and `miss-` the past-participle stem of Latin *mittere*; `transmission` is therefore a genuine family member.
- `transfer` and `transport` share only `trans-` here and are excluded from this family.
- confidence: source-backed

## transport
### Identity
- lemma: transport
- title: transport
- pos: v/n
- ipa: /trænˈspɔːt/; /ˈtrænspɔːt/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: transportation, transporter
### Short Definition
运输；运送；交通运输；使强烈激动
### Core Definitions
1. 把人或货物从一处运到另一处
   - en: to carry people or goods from one place to another
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 运输系统、运输方式或运输行为
   - en: a system, means, or act of carrying people or goods
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 使人因强烈情感而仿佛被带离当下
   - en: to carry someone away with strong emotion
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
transport = 把人、货物或人的心神从一处“携带越过”到另一处。
### Morphology
- prefix: trans- (across, beyond)
- root: port- / Latin *portare* (carry)
- suffix: none
- family: transport, transportation, transporter, transportable, portable, import, export
### Etymology Narrative

经古法语 transporter，并直接出自拉丁语 transportare（运送过去、携往对岸、搬运），由 trans-（跨越）加 portare（携带）构成。画面是把货物扛过河去。“搬运过处”这一意象生出交通运输诸义；而“带走、使离去”一义又发展为被强烈情感所淹没、神魂飘然。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: trans-（跨越）+ portare（携带）→ 把货物扛过河去；人也被这么载着走。
### Semantic Chain
载运 -> 越地而移 -> 转运人货 -> 运输

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/transport；late 14c. *transporten* via Old French *transporter* and Latin *transportare*.
- The emotional “carry away” sense is recorded by c. 1500 and remains a branch of the carrying image.
- `transmit` and `transfer` are excluded: they share `trans-` but use Latin *mittere* and *ferre*, not *portare*.
- confidence: source-backed

## trivial
### Identity
- lemma: trivial
- title: trivial
- pos: adj
- ipa: /ˈtrɪviəl/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: trivially, triviality
### Short Definition
琐碎的；微不足道的；不重要的
### Core Definitions
1. 不重要或价值很小的
   - en: having little importance or value
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 很容易解决或处理的
   - en: simple and requiring little effort to solve or deal with
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
### Prototype
trivial = 原本属于公共路口、随处可见的事物，因普通而被看作不值得重视。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: trivi- / Latin *trivium* (place where three roads meet; public place)
- suffix: -al (recognition layer; whole borrowing from Latin *trivialis*)
- family: trivial, trivially, triviality, trivialize, trivia
### Etymology Narrative

源自拉丁语 trivialis（平常的、普通的、粗俗的），字面为“属于三岔路口的”，出自 trivium（三条路交会之处），由 tri-（三）加 via（路）构成。画面是村口三路交会的空地，人人往来、谁都可以站着闲聊。由“公共场所”变为“司空见惯”，再变为“无足轻重、琐屑”。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 拉丁语 trivialis 字面是“三岔路口的”（tri- 三 + via 路）→ 村口那块空地，人人往来闲聊，也就最不打紧。
### Semantic Chain
三岔 -> 十字街头 -> 人尽可至 -> 琐碎

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/trivial；Latin *trivialis* from *trivium* = *tri-* + *via*.
- The entry preserves the whole historical path through *trivium*; it is not a modern `try + vial` split, and `tri-` is not treated as a free English prefix here.
- The mnemonic uses the sourced public/commonplace connection and does not add the unsupported crossroads-gossip story.
- confidence: source-backed

   - en: to attempt to do something or make an effort to achieve it
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 试用、检验或体验某物
   - en: to test, use, or experience something to discover whether it works or suits you
   - priority: 2

3. 审理案件或审判某人
   - en: to examine a case or person judicially and reach a decision
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
try = 他把那把钥匙插进去，转了一下

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: try-/Anglo-French and Old French *trier* (pick out, cull)
- suffix: none
- family: try, trial, triable, triage
### Etymology Narrative

源自盎格鲁-法语与古法语 trier（拣出、挑选），经高卢-罗曼语 *triare，其更深的来源不明。画面是把好坏分开、拣出所要的那一堆。由“经检视而分出所要的部分”，引出“试验、评估”，再转为司法上的审讯，以及日常“尝试”一义。
### Mnemonic
- type: story
- text: 古法语 trier 本义是“拣出、分开”→ 把好坏分开、挑出合用的；挑出来试一试，也就有了这一说。
### Semantic Chain
挑出 -> 考校 -> 试上一试 -> 尝试

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/try；c. 1300 *trien* from Anglo-French and Old French *trier*.
- Gallo-Roman *\*triare* is explicitly of unknown origin, so no deeper root is asserted.
- `trial` is an Anglo-French noun formed from Old French *trier*; it is a cognate formation, not analyzed here as modern English `try + -al`.
- confidence: uncertain

- title: type
- pos: n/v

- cefr: A2

### Short Definition
类型；种类；字体；打字
### Core Definitions
1. 具有共同特征的一类人或事物
   - en: a group of people or things sharing particular characteristics
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 印刷字体、铅字或字样
   - en: printing blocks, printed characters, or a style of printed text
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 用键盘输入文字
   - en: to write words using a keyboard or typewriter
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
type = 铅字一个一个排在槽里，印出来字迹一模一样

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: type / Greek *typos* (blow, impression, figure)
- suffix: none
- family: type, typical, typify, prototype, stereotype, typography
### Etymology Narrative

源自拉丁语 typus，出自希腊语 typos（一击、凹痕、印痕、记号，亦指“图形”或“一般的形式”）。画面是把印章按下去留下的那个印子。印痕转为印刷用的铅字，又转为代表性的形式或类别；印刷一义后来生出“用打字机书写”的动词用法。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 一个模子反复敲出同样的印记 → 凡是带着这个模样的，都归作同一路。
### Semantic Chain
一击 -> 留下印模 -> 成其范式 -> 类型

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/type；Latin *typus* from Greek *typos*.
- `type` is treated as a whole borrowing with no English prefix or suffix.
- The typing verb is recorded by 1888 and grows from the printing-type noun, not directly from an invented modern split.
- confidence: source-backed

## typical
### Identity
- lemma: typical
- title: typical
- pos: adj
- ipa: /ˈtɪpɪkəl/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: typically, atypical
### Short Definition
典型的；有代表性的；一贯的
### Core Definitions
1. 具有某类事物代表性特征的
   - en: having the usual or representative qualities of a particular kind
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 某人或某物一贯会有的
   - en: showing the behaviour or qualities usually associated with someone or something
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
### Prototype
typical = 某个对象清楚呈现其所属类型的共同特征，因此可以代表这一类。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: typ-/type / Greek *typos* (impression, type)
- suffix: -ical (recognition layer; whole borrowing from Medieval Latin *typicalis*)
- family: typical, type, typically, atypical, typicality
### Etymology Narrative

源自中世纪拉丁语 typicalis（象征性的），经晚期拉丁语 typicus 与希腊语 typikos，最终溯自 typos（压出的印痕）。画面是把印章按下去、留下一个模子。其较古的“作为典型、作为预表”一义，发展为“合乎某一类型”，故即“有代表性的、典型的”。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: 溯至希腊语 typos（压出的印痕）→ 印章按下去留下的那个模样；带着这一类模样的，就是它的样本。
### Semantic Chain
印记 -> 成其类型 -> 足为样本 -> 典型

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/typical；c. 1600 from Medieval Latin *typicalis*, Late Latin *typicus*, and Greek *typikos* from *typos*.
- `-ical` is retained as one modern recognition layer; it is not overlapped or split into `-ic + -al`, and the whole word was historically borrowed.
- `type` is a genuine family member through Greek *typos*.
- confidence: source-backed
