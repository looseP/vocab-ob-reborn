## region
### Identity
- lemma: region
- title: region
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈriːdʒən/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: regions
### Short Definition
地区；区域；范围
### Core Definitions
1. 地区；区域
   - en: a particular area or part of a country, world, or body
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 范围；领域
   - en: a range or part of a larger system or field
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
### Prototype
region = 过了那条河，口音就变了，吃的东西也不一样

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: reg- / Latin *regio* (district, direction)
- suffix: -ion (Latin noun layer)
- family: region, regional, regionally
### Etymology Narrative

约1300年出现，源自盎格鲁-法语与古法语 region，出自拉丁语 regionem（地区、国度、方位），来自动词 regere（统治、引导）。画面是划出墨线、由权力所及的那片地方。由被治理或被划定的区域，转为地理上的区，再转为一般的领域或范围。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: regere 本义是“统治、划界”→ 墨线一划，权力所及的那片地方。
### Semantic Chain
依规划定 -> 受辖之区 -> 一方之地 -> 地区

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/region；c. 1300 < Anglo-French/Old French *region* < Latin *regionem* < *regere*.
- The geographical and abstract-range senses are kept as extensions of an area bounded or directed within a larger whole.
- confidence: source-backed
