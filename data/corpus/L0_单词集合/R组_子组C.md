## resent

### Identity
- lemma: resent
- title: resent
- pos: v
- ipa: /rɪˈzent/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: resents, resented, resenting

### Short Definition
愤恨；因受冒犯而心生怨气

### Core Definitions
1. 因不公、轻视或冒犯而怨恨
   - en: to feel bitterness or indignation at an insult, injury, or unfairness
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 对被迫接受之事感到不满
   - en: to dislike or begrudge something that is imposed or unwelcome
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
resent = 某件事再次刺痛主观感受，于是内心反复“感觉到”那份被冒犯或被亏待的不快。

### Morphology
- prefix: re- (again; perhaps intensive in the source note)
- root: sent- / Latin *sentire* (feel, perceive)
- suffix: none
- family: resent, resentment, resentful, resentfully

### Etymology Narrative

约1610年代出现，源自法语 ressentir（深切感受、意识到、懊悔），由 re- 加 sentir（感觉）构成，而 sentir 出自拉丁语 sentire（感觉、察觉）。画面是旧事重上心头、再感受一遍。英语把词义收窄为因受到伤害或侮辱而持续怀有的不快与愤慨。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: 法语 ressentir = re-（再）+ sentir（感觉）→ 旧辱翻上来再受一遍，日子久了就结成了疙瘩。

### Semantic Chain
有所感 -> 深衔其辱 -> 久怀不平 -> 怨恨

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/resent；1610s < French *ressentir* < *sentir* < Latin *sentire*.
- The source note says *re-* is “perhaps” intensive here, so the prefix value is recorded cautiously rather than as a fixed fact.
- No aliases; *resentment* and *resentful* remain family members, not aliases.
- confidence: uncertain
