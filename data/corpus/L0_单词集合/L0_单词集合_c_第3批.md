## commodity

### Identity
- lemma: commodity
- title: commodity
- pos: n
- ipa: /kəˈmɒdəti/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: commodities

### Short Definition
商品；货物；有价值且可利用的资源

### Core Definitions
1. 商品；货物
   - en: a product that can be bought and sold, especially a raw material or basic good
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 有价值且可利用的东西
   - en: something useful or valuable that people need or want
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
commodity = 粮、盐、布，一样样都能在市场上换成钱

### Morphology
- prefix: com- (perhaps intensive; historical layer inside whole borrowing)
- root: mod (< Latin commodus “suitable, convenient”)
- suffix: -ity (recognition layer inside whole borrowing)
- family: commodities, commodify, commodification

### Etymology Narrative

经古法语 commodit 溯自拉丁语 commoditatem（主格 commoditas），意为“合宜、便利、好处”，由 commodus（合适的、方便的）派生。历史上的 com- 或为强化成分。画面是正合用、省事又得力。由“合宜、有用”发展出“有用的物品”，再转为可交易的商品。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 把 commodity 想成市场上“有用、适合交换”的货物；这是意义锚点，不把拼写硬拆成伪词源。

### Semantic Chain
合用便利 -> 有用有利 -> 顶用之物 -> 可贸之货 -> 商品；货物

### Reviewer Notes
- whole borrowing；不连 common 或 communicate。
- confidence: source-backed

### Short Definition
交流；沟通；通信

### Core Definitions
1. 交流；沟通
   - en: the sharing or exchange of information, ideas, or feelings
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 通信；通讯系统
   - en: the sending and receiving of information over distance
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 交通联系（常用复数）
   - en: systems or routes used for travel and connection
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C

### Prototype

### Morphology
- prefix: com- (historical layer inside whole borrowing)
- root: mun (< Latin communis “common, shared”)
- suffix: -ication (recognition layer inside whole borrowing)
- family: communicate, communicative, communications

### Etymology Narrative

### Mnemonic

### Reviewer Notes
