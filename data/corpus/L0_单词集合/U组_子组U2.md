# U组 · 子组U2（L1 生产词条）

## universal
### Identity
- lemma: universal
- title: universal
- pos: adj/n
- ipa: /juːnɪˈvɜːsl/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: universally, universality, universalism
### Short Definition
普遍的；全体的；通用的；普遍概念
### Core Definitions
1. 适用于所有人或事物的
   - en: applying to everyone or everything without exception
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 全世界的或广泛存在的
   - en: occurring everywhere or affecting the whole world
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 适用于多种情况的；通用的
   - en: suitable for or usable in many situations
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
universal = 一个整体中的每个成员都被覆盖，没有例外。
### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: univers- / Latin *universus* (all together, whole, entire; from *unus* “one” + *versus* “turned”)
- suffix: -al (recognition layer; whole borrowing through Latin *universalis* and Old French *universel*)
- family: universal, universe, universality, universalism
### Etymology Narrative

经古法语 universel 溯自拉丁语 universalis（属于全体的），由 universus（合在一起的、整个的、全部的）派生。底层的意象是“诸多事物被转成一体”。画面是把零散的东西收拢成一个整体。其义由整体或全体，推及适用于该整体中每一个成员的。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 拉丁语 universus 的意象是“诸多转成一体”→ 把零散的收拢成一整块，一条规矩罩住里头的每一个。
### Semantic Chain
全体 -> 归于一总 -> 人人有份 -> 普遍

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/universal；late 14c. from Old French *universel* and Latin *universalis* < *universus*.
- The page links *universe* and records *universality*; *universalism* is also a linked derivative. `uni-` is not treated as a modern English prefix in this entry; *unus* “one” remains a historical component of *universus*.
- `university` and `universally` are not added here because this audit does not rely on an independent direct form from the main page.
- confidence: source-backed

## unlike
### Identity
- lemma: unlike
- title: unlike
- pos: prep/adj
- ipa: /ʌnˈlaɪk/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: unlikeness, unliked, unliking
### Short Definition
不像；与……不同
### Core Definitions
1. 与某人或某物不同；不像
   - en: not resembling someone or something
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 不同的；不相似的
   - en: different in character or qualities
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
### Prototype
unlike = 把两个对象放在比较中，明确指出它们不具有相似性。
### Morphology
- prefix: un- (not; English negation prefix)
- root: like / Old English *gelic* (like, similar)
- suffix:
- family: unlike, unlikeness, like
### Etymology Narrative

在英语中由 un-（不）加 like（相像的）构成。画面是两样东西放在一起、对不上号。否定前缀把相似关系转为直接的对照，遂有“不相像的”一义，后有介词用法“与……不同”。
### Mnemonic
- type: contrast
- text: like means “像”; add English un- “不” and unlike means “不像、与之不同.”
### Semantic Chain
相像 -> 不再相似 -> 与之为异 -> 不同

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/unlike；c. 1200 *unlike* “not resembling” from *un-* “not” + *like*.
- This is an English-layer negative formation. It is kept separate from `unlikely`, whose second element is *likely* and whose core meaning is low probability; `unusual` is likewise a separate *un-* + *usual* formation.
- The verb sense “cease to like” is not used as the primary L1 definition here.
- confidence: source-backed

## unlikely
### Identity
- lemma: unlikely
- title: unlikely
- pos: adj/adv
- ipa: /ʌnˈlaɪkli/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: unlikeliness, unlikelihood, likely
### Short Definition
不太可能的；未必的；不大可能地
### Core Definitions
1. 不太可能发生或为真的
   - en: not likely to happen or be true
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 不像会做某事的
   - en: not expected or considered likely to do something
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
### Prototype
unlikely = 某个事件与合理预期不相符，因此发生概率很低。
### Morphology
- prefix: un- (not; English negation prefix)
- root: likely / *like* (having the appearance of truth or fact)
- suffix: -ly (historical adjectival or adverbial layer in *likely*)
- family: unlikely, unlikeliness, unlikelihood, likely
### Etymology Narrative

由英语的 un-（不）加 likely 构成。likely 由“看来真实或合乎事实的”转为“很可能的”；画面是看来像那么回事。故这一否定构词由“看来不会发生的”，发展出一般性的低概率判断。
### Mnemonic
- type: contrast
- text: If likely means “像是会发生,” un- + likely means “不像会发生”: unlikely.
### Semantic Chain
看似成真 -> 或有可能 -> 几无可能 -> 不太可能

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/unlikely；late 14c. *unlikli* “probably not going to come to pass” from *un-* “not” + *likely*.
- *Likely* is separately described as “having the appearance of truth or fact,” with “probable” recorded later; this supports the probability path without merging the entry with `unlike`.
- `likely` is retained in family as the base of this formation; `like` is not repeated in the family because the direct family evidence is represented through *likely*.
- confidence: source-backed

## unusual
### Identity
- lemma: unusual
- title: unusual
- pos: adj
- ipa: /ʌnˈjuːʒuəl/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: unusually, unusualness, unusuality
### Short Definition
不寻常的；罕见的；与众不同的
### Core Definitions
1. 不常见或超出平常的
   - en: not usual, common, or expected
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 与同类不同而显得特别的
   - en: different from what is typical or familiar
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
### Prototype
unusual = 偏离通常模式，因而显得少见或特别。
### Morphology
- prefix: un- (not; English negation prefix)
- root: usual / Old French *usuel* < Latin *usus* (custom, use)
- suffix: -al (recognition layer; whole borrowing in *usual* from Latin *usualis*)
- family: unusual, unusually, unusualness, unusuality, usual
### Etymology Narrative

在英语中由 un-（不）加 usual（常见的、惯常的）构成。usual 经古法语 usuel 溯自晚期拉丁语 usualis，出自拉丁语 usus（习惯、惯例）。画面是众人天天走的那条老路。故这一否定构词指的是偏离了惯常的、稀见的。
### Mnemonic
- type: contrast
- text: un-（不）+ usual，后者溯至拉丁语 usus（习惯、惯例）→ 偏离了众人天天走的那个老路。
### Semantic Chain
常习 -> 惯见之态 -> 异于常情 -> 不寻常

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/unusual；1580s “not usual, uncommon” from *un-* “not” + *usual*.
- The linked *usual* entry gives Old French *usuel* < Late Latin *usualis* < Latin *usus* “custom.” This is not analyzed as a modern English `use + -ual` split.
- `unlike` and `unlikely` remain separate formations: this entry negates *usual*, not *like* or *likely*.
- confidence: source-backed

## upgrade
### Identity
- lemma: upgrade
- title: upgrade
- pos: v/n
- ipa: /ʌpˈɡreɪd/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: upgraded, upgrading, up-grade
### Short Definition
升级；提升；升级版
### Core Definitions
1. 提升到更高的等级或质量
   - en: to increase something to a higher grade, rank, or quality
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 升级后的版本或待遇
   - en: a higher-level version or an improvement in status or quality
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
### Prototype
upgrade = 沿等级阶梯向上走一步，从低级别进入更高一级。
### Morphology
- prefix: up (English adverb or direction word, upward)
- root: grade / Latin *gradus* (step, degree)
- suffix:
- family: upgrade, upgraded, upgrading, grade
### Etymology Narrative

本词由英语的 up 与 grade 复合而成，1847年最初指一段向上的斜坡。grade 出自拉丁语 gradus（台阶、等级），画面是一级一级的台阶。沿斜坡或标尺向上移动，遂发展为提升等级、品质或版本。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: up + grade，后者出自拉丁语 gradus（台阶、等级）→ 一级一级往上抬，抬到更高一层去。
### Semantic Chain
台阶 -> 拾级而上 -> 更高等第 -> 升级

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/upgrade；1847 *up-grade* “upward slope” from *up* + *grade*; verb “increase to a higher grade or rank” by 1904.
- `up` is an English direction word, not a Latin prefix. The grade element is linked to Latin *gradus* “a step, a pace, a degree.”
- The modern “upgrade” sense is a semantic development from upward movement or position to higher rank, quality, version, or treatment.
- confidence: source-backed

## urgent
### Identity
- lemma: urgent
- title: urgent
- pos: adj
- ipa: /ˈɜːrdʒənt/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: urgently, urgency, urged, urging
### Short Definition
紧急的；急迫的；迫切需要处理的
### Core Definitions
1. 需要立即处理或行动的
   - en: requiring immediate attention or action
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 催促的；极力推动某事的
   - en: pressing or strongly encouraging something to happen
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
### Prototype
urgent = 某种力量正在向前推压，事情因此不能再等待。
### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: urg- / Latin *urgere* (press hard, push forward, drive)
- suffix: -ent (present-participle or adjectival recognition layer in the Latin-to-French formation)
- family: urgent, urgently, urgency, urge
### Etymology Narrative

经古法语溯自拉丁语 urgentem（urgere“用力推、催促”的现在分词）。画面是有只手在背后推着你走。物理上“推挤、驱迫”一义，发展为时间上“需要立即处理的”一义；相关的动词 urge 仍保留着推挤之意。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: 拉丁语 urgere（用力推、催促）的现在分词 → 有只手在背后推着你走，一刻也耽搁不得。
### Semantic Chain
狠压 -> 驱之向前 -> 燃眉之急 -> 紧急

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/urgent；mid-15c. from Old French *urgent* < Latin *urgentem*, present participle of *urgere* “to press hard, urge.”
- `urgent` is related to `urge`; it is not formed with English `un-` and has no negative-prefix meaning. `-ent` is retained only as a recognition layer for the historical participial/adjectival form.
- Family is limited to forms directly supported by the urgent/urge entries; unrelated words with similar spelling are excluded.
- confidence: source-backed

## utilize
### Identity
- lemma: utilize
- title: utilize
- pos: v
- ipa: /ˈjuːtəlaɪz/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: utilization, underutilize, underutilized
### Short Definition
利用；使用；使某物发挥效用
### Core Definitions
1. 使用某物以达到目的
   - en: to make use of something for a purpose
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 使资源或对象变得有用并加以利用
   - en: to make something useful or turn it to account
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
### Prototype
utilize = 把现有对象投入用途，使它发挥可用的价值。
### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: utilis- / Latin *utilis* (usable, useful) < *uti* (make use of)
- suffix: -ize (English and French verb-forming layer)
- family: utilize, utilization, underutilize, use
### Etymology Narrative

经法语 utiliser 进入英语，出自意大利语 utilizzare，最终溯自拉丁语 utilis（可用的）与 uti（使用）。画面是把一件合用的东西拿在手里派上用场。故其历史走向是由“有用、可用”，转为主动地加以利用，尤指把某项资源用出效益来。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: 拉丁语 utilis（可用的）与 uti（使用）→ 把合用的东西拿在手里，派上实在的用场。
### Semantic Chain
使用 -> 堪可受用 -> 取其效用 -> 利用

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/utilize；1794 from French *utiliser* < Italian *utilizzare* < Latin *utilis* < *uti*.
- `-ize` is treated as the English/French verb-forming layer. `util-` is not treated as an English free root, and the word is not rewritten as a folk `use + -ize` formation.
- The main page directly links `use`, `underutilize`, and `utilization`; `utility` and `usable` are not added without a corresponding direct audit form.
- confidence: source-backed

## utter
### Identity
- lemma: utter
- title: utter
- pos: v/adj
- ipa: /ˈʌtər/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: uttered, uttering, utterance, utterly
### Short Definition
说出；发出声音；完全的；彻底的
### Core Definitions
1. 说出或发出声音
   - en: to speak, say, or give public expression to something
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 完全的；彻底的、十足的
   - en: complete, total, or extreme
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
### Prototype
utter = 动词把声音向外发出；形容词把程度推到最外、最极端的位置。
### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: ut-/out- / Old English *ut* “out”; the two historical verb lines are noted separately
- suffix:
- family: utter, utterance, uttered, uttering, utterly
### Etymology Narrative

形容词 utter 是与 out 相关的比较级形式，由“更外的、最外的”转为“极端的”，再转为“完全的”。动词约1400年已见，是中古荷兰语或中古低地德语 uteren（显示、说出）与中古英语 outen（披露、公开）的合流；两者都表示把某物“放出去”，但历史上是两条线，不可并作一个简单的现代派生关系。
### Mnemonic
- type: contrast
- text: 动词与形容词两源：动词出自中古低地德语 uteren（说出），形容词是 out 的比较级 → 把话从嘴里吐出来，说给在场的人听。
### Semantic Chain
向外 -> 倾吐而出 -> 言或泄之 -> 发出

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/utter；the adjective is a comparative of *out*; the verb “speak, say, give public expression” is c. 1400 and reflects two merged historical lines.
- The adjective sense “complete, total” comes through “outermost, furthest, extreme”; the verb sense “say or give expression” follows the notion “to put forth.” These are related but not treated as one undifferentiated source path.
- `outer` is not listed as a family member here merely because the adjective has a comparative relation to *out*; no unsupported blanket `utter = outer` analysis is made. Prefix and suffix remain blank.
- confidence: uncertain
