## vain
### Identity
- lemma: vain
- title: vain
- pos: adj
- ipa: /veɪn/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: vainly, vanity, vainglory
### Short Definition
徒劳的；虚荣的；空虚无意义的
### Core Definitions
1. 徒劳的；无结果的
   - en: producing no useful result or effect
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 自负的；虚荣的
   - en: too proud of one's appearance, abilities, or achievements
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 空虚的；无价值或无意义的
   - en: lacking real value, importance, or substance
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
vain = 一个容器空无一物，努力之后仍然什么也没有留下。
### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: van- / Latin *vanus* (empty, void)
- suffix:
- family: vanity, vainglory, vainly
### Etymology Narrative

经古法语 vain、vein 溯自拉丁语 vanus（空的、空虚的）。画面是一只空罐子、里头什么都没有。物理上“空”的意象，转为“徒劳无功的”努力，再转为把自尊建立在琐屑空虚之物上的人。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 想象一个空盒子：努力塞东西却没有结果；再想象一个内在空虚却自我欣赏的人，都是 vain。
### Semantic Chain
空空 -> 有名无实 -> 落了空 -> 徒劳

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/vain；c. 1300 *vain/vein* “having no value or importance,” from Old French *vain/vein* < Latin *vanus* “empty, void,” figuratively “idle, fruitless.”
- `vanity` may be listed as a cautious related derivative, but `vain` is not modern `van- + -ish`; prefix and suffix remain blank.
- Etymonline links `vain` and `vanish` through the reconstructed PIE root *eue-*, but spelling resemblance alone is not used to merge them; the direct Latin source for `vain` is *vanus*.
- confidence: source-backed

- `ex-/es-` is recorded as a historical source layer only; `vanish` is not modern `van + -ish`, and `-ish` is blank.
- The deeper reconstruction is kept conservative: the entry records the page’s chain without claiming a simple modern decomposition or collapsing it with `vain` by spelling.

## variance
### Identity
- lemma: variance
- title: variance
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈveəriəns/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: variances, variant, variation
### Short Definition
差异；不一致；统计方差
### Core Definitions
1. 差异；变化幅度
   - en: a difference or degree of change between things
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 分歧；不一致
   - en: a state of disagreement or inconsistency
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 方差
   - en: a statistical measure of how far values vary from the average
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
variance = 两份清单摆在一起，三处对不上

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: vari- / Latin *variantia* from *variare* (change)
- suffix: -ance (historical nominal layer; whole borrowing through French/Latin)
- family: variance, vary, variant, variation
### Etymology Narrative

约14世纪末作 variaunce，源自古法语 variance，并直接出自拉丁语 variantia，由 variare（变化）的词干派生。画面是同一件东西一次次地变样。由“经历变化”之意，发展出差异与前后不一致，再转为意见不合，以及统计学上度量变异的量。
### Mnemonic
- type: contrast
- text: vary 是“变化”，variance 是变化留下的“差异”：同一标准下不再一致，就是 variance。
### Semantic Chain
变化 -> 变异之状 -> 互有出入 -> 差异

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/variance；late 14c. *variaunce* “fact of undergoing change of state or condition,” from Old French *variance* and directly from Latin *variantia*, from *variare* “to change.”
- `-ance` is treated as the historical nominal layer of the borrowed form, not as proof of a simple productive English `vary + -ance` formation.
- `variance` may share a real derivative family with `vary`, `variant`, and `variation`, but its sense path is kept separate: change → discrepancy/disagreement → statistical spread.
- confidence: source-backed

## ventilate
### Identity
- lemma: ventilate
- title: ventilate

### Short Definition
使通风；使空气流通；公开表达
### Core Definitions
1. 使通风；给……换气
   - en: to supply a room or building with fresh air
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 使肺部通气
   - en: to supply the lungs with air, especially artificially
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 公开表达（意见或不满）
   - en: to express an opinion or grievance openly

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: vent- / Latin *ventus* (wind), via *ventilare* (fan, blow, winnow)
- suffix: -ate (historical verb-forming/recognition layer in the Latin borrowing)

### Etymology Narrative

### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 想象风把谷壳吹走、把房间浊气换掉：ventilate 就是让空气流通，也可把想法“放出来”。
### Semantic Chain
风 -> 簸去谷壳 -> 扇风送气 -> 通风

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/ventilate；early 15c. *ventilaten* “to scatter, disperse,” from Latin *ventilatus*, past participle of *ventilare*, from *ventulus* “a breeze,” diminutive of *ventus* “wind.”
- `vent-` is the root from the wind line, not a modern English prefix; `-ate` is only a historical/verb recognition layer for the borrowed form.
- The page directly supports the winnowing image, the later fresh-air sense, and artificial lung ventilation; `vent` is not used to force a family link to venture or venue.
- confidence: source-backed
