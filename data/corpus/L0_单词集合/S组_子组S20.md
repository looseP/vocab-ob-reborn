## survival
### Identity
- lemma: survival
- title: survival
- pos: n
- ipa: /sərˈvaɪvəl/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: survivals

### Short Definition
生存；幸存；继续存在

### Core Definitions
1. 继续活着
   - en: the state of continuing to live
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 在危险或困难后幸存
   - en: the state of remaining alive after danger or difficulty
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 某物继续存在
   - en: the continuation of an institution, practice, or thing
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C

### Prototype
survival = 同批下地的种子，只有这几棵熬过了霜

### Morphology
- prefix: super-/sur- (over, beyond)
- root: viv-/vivere (live)
- suffix: -al (state or act noun layer)
- family: survive, survival, survivor, survivable, surviving

### Etymology Narrative

本词由 survive 加 -al 构成。survive 经法语溯自拉丁语 supervivere（活得比……长久），由 super（超过）加 vivere（活）构成。画面是别人都倒下了，他还活着走出来。本词由“仍然活着”，扩大为制度、做法或系统的继续存在。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: supervivere = super（越过）+ vivere（活）→ 别人都倒下了，他还活着走了出来。

### Semantic Chain
活着 -> 脱险余生 -> 续存不去 -> 存活

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/survival；*survive* < Latin *supervivere* < *super* + *vivere*.
- The noun is treated as an English derivative of survive; precise French mediation is not freshly verified.
- Biological, personal, and institutional continuity are branches of remaining.
- confidence: uncertain

### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: system（成体系的）+ -atic → 照着一套排好的次第走，不东一榔头西一棒槌。

### Semantic Chain

- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/systematic；Greek *systēma* / Latin *systema* with an adjectival formation.
- The Greek internal analysis is kept as a cautious historical anchor; the English `-atic` layer is instructional.
- Method, process, regularity, and deliberate repetition are branches of system-based order.
- confidence: uncertain
