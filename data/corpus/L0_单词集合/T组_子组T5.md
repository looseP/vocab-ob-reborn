## through
### Identity
- lemma: through
- title: through
- pos: prep/adv/adj
- ipa: /θruː/
- cefr: A2
- aliases: thru
### Short Definition
穿过；通过；贯穿；完成的
### Core Definitions
1. 从一边或一端穿到另一边或另一端
   - en: from one side or end of something to the other
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 从开始一直到结束；凭借某种过程或手段
   - en: from beginning to end, or by means of a process or method
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 完成的；直达的、接通的
   - en: finished, direct without interruption, or connected
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
### Prototype
through = 他从这头进去，从那头出来，中间没停

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: through-/þurh / Old English *þurh* (through)
- suffix: none
- family: through, thorough, breakthrough, throughout
### Etymology Narrative

本词是日耳曼语本族词，中古英语作 thurgh（由音位换序而来），古英语作 þurh（穿过）。画面是从一头进去、由另一头出来。其路径义自然延伸到时间的持续、凭藉的手段、事情的完成、直达的行程与连络；它与 thorough 直到近代英语初期才清楚分开。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 古英语 þurh（穿过）→ 从这一头进去，由那一头出来；一件事走完一整段，也这么说。
### Semantic Chain
过一路 -> 穿行其间 -> 达于对岸 -> 穿过

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/through；Middle English *thurgh* < Old English *þurh* < Proto-Germanic *\*thurx*.
- `th + rough` is rejected as etymology; the word is Germanic and historically related to, but now distinct from, `thorough`.
- `thru` is a spelling variant, not a self-reference.
- confidence: source-backed

## throughout
### Identity
- lemma: throughout
- title: throughout
- pos: prep/adv
- ipa: /θruːˈaʊt/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: thruout
### Short Definition
遍及；贯穿……始终；自始至终
### Core Definitions
1. 在某个地方的每一部分
   - en: in every part of a place or thing
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 在整个时间段或过程之中
   - en: during the whole of a period or process
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
### Prototype
throughout = 一条路径不仅穿过对象，还把其中每个部分或每个时刻都覆盖到。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: through + out / English compound, Old English *þurhut*
- suffix: none
- family: through, throughout, thruout
### Etymology Narrative

本词是英语中 through 与 out 的复合词，古英语晚期作 þurhut，意为“完全穿过、从一头或一侧进、由另一头出”。画面是一根线从头穿到尾、贯通无阻。由这一完整的路径，发展出覆盖某处的每一部分、以及某段时间中的每一刻。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: through + out 的复合，古英语晚期作 þurhut → 一根线从头穿到尾，一处也不落。
### Semantic Chain
穿行而过 -> 透达彼方 -> 无所不到 -> 遍及

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/throughout；late Old English *þurhut*, Middle English *thurgh-out*.
- `out` is the English adverbial compound element reinforcing complete extent, not an independent Latin prefix.
- `thruout` is a spelling contraction, not a self-reference.
- confidence: source-backed

## tip
### Identity
- lemma: tip
- title: tip
- pos: n/v
- ipa: /tɪp/
- cefr: A2
- aliases: tips, tipped, tipping
### Short Definition
尖端；提示；小费；倾斜或翻倒
### Core Definitions
1. 细长物体的尖端或末端
   - en: the pointed or extreme end of something slender
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 有用的建议或私下提供的信息
   - en: a useful piece of advice or confidential information
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 因服务而额外给的一小笔钱
   - en: a small extra payment given for service
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
4. 使倾斜、翻倒或倾倒
   - en: to lean, overturn, or cause something to pour out
   - priority: 4
   - tags: C
### Prototype
tip = 他放下筷子，又多搁了两枚铜子在碟子边

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: tip / multiple historical lines of uncertain origin
- suffix: none
- family: tip, tipped, tipping, tiptoe, fingertip, tip-off
### Etymology Narrative

本词有数条历史线索，不可强并为一源。“尖端”义的名词大概出自中古低地德语或中古荷兰语 tip，或古 Norse 语 typpi 之类的斯堪的纳维亚语源；“倾倒、翻倒”义的动词来源不明，可能出自斯堪的纳维亚语；而“小费”与“内部消息”两义，则上承一个来源不明的黑话动词，本义为“给、递、传”。
### Mnemonic
- type: contrast
- text: 三条线索不可强并：末梢一说出自低地德语或斯堪的纳维亚语，倾倒与小费又各出一源 → 细长东西的那一头。
### Semantic Chain
诸源交汇 -> 收束成尖 -> 末端之点 -> 尖端

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/tip；separate entries for *tip* n.1, v.1, v.2, v.3, n.3, and v.4.
- The pointed-end, gratuity/information, light-touch, and overturning lines are not collapsed into a false single origin; the “To Insure Promptitude” acronym tale is rejected.
- Inflected lookup forms in aliases do not repeat the lemma.
- confidence: uncertain

## title
### Identity
- lemma: title
- title: title
- pos: n/v
- ipa: /ˈtaɪtl/
- cefr: A2
- aliases: titles, titled, titling
### Short Definition
标题；头衔；所有权；授予标题
### Core Definitions
1. 书籍、文章、电影等的名称
   - en: the name of a book, article, film, or other work
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 表示身份、职位、等级或冠军地位的称号
   - en: a name showing a person's position, rank, or championship status
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 财产的合法所有权；给作品加标题
   - en: legal ownership of property, or to give a work its name
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
title = 放在某物之上的识别性铭文，后来成为作品名称、人的称号和权利凭证。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: title-/titulus / Latin *titulus* via Old French *title* and Old English *titul*
- suffix: none
- family: title, titled, titular, entitle, subtitle
### Etymology Narrative

本词整体借自古法语 title，部分与古英语 titul 相合；二者都出自拉丁语 titulus（题铭、标签、票签、告示；荣衔）。画面是贴在物品上的小牌子。由这种标识性的题铭，发展为著作的名称、身份等级的称号、对财产的法律权利，以及冠军头衔。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 拉丁语 titulus 本是贴在物件上的小牌子 → 著作顶上那个题名，也指爵位与对产业的权利。
### Semantic Chain
题刻 -> 标名之头 -> 书名或爵 -> 名称

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/title；Old French *title* and Old English *titul* < Latin *titulus*.
- The word is treated as a whole borrowing; final `-tle` is not an English suffix.
- Inflected lookup forms in aliases do not repeat the lemma.
- confidence: source-backed

## together
### Identity
- lemma: together
- title: together
- pos: adv/adj
- ipa: /təˈɡeðər/
- cefr: A1
- aliases: togetherness, get-together
### Short Definition
一起；共同；同时；协调完整的
### Core Definitions
1. 与其他人或事物在同一处或共同做事
   - en: with other people or things in one place or joint activity
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 在同一时间；结合成一个整体
   - en: at the same time or so as to form a unified whole
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 镇定自信、生活有条理的
   - en: self-assured and emotionally well organized
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
together = 八个人抬着一根梁，脚步得踩在同一个点上

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: together-/togædere / Old English *togædere* (in one place or group)
- suffix: none
- family: together, togetherness, get-together, gather
### Etymology Narrative

经中古英语 togeder 溯自古英语 togædere（聚集于一处、成群、聚成一堆）。历史上含古英语 to 加 gædere，后者与 geador 相关，可与 gather 相比照；画面是把分散的东西聚拢到一处。但现代这个词是早已熔合的整体，不可拆成 to ＋ get ＋ her。
### Mnemonic
- type: story
- text: 古英语 togædere，画面本就是一群人聚在一处 → 拢到一块儿，不分你我。
### Semantic Chain
同一地 -> 聚成一伙 -> 协同而动 -> 一起

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/together；Middle English *togeder* < Old English *togædere*.
- Historical composition is retained without treating modern `to + get + her` as morphology or etymology.
- `togetherness` and `get-together` are lookup derivatives, not self-references.
- confidence: source-backed
