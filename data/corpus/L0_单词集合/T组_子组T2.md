# T组 · 子组T2（L1 生产词条）

## technician
### Identity
- lemma: technician
- title: technician
- pos: n
- ipa: /tekˈnɪʃən/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: technicians
### Short Definition
技术员；技师
### Core Definitions
1. 具有专业技术的工作人员
   - en: a person trained in a practical technical skill
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 负责操作、维护或修理设备的人
   - en: a worker who operates, maintains, or repairs equipment
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
### Prototype
technician = 掌握某种具体技术并把它用于设备、实验或生产现场的人。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: technic- / English *technic* < Greek *tekhnē* (skill, craft)
- suffix: -ian (person-forming suffix)
- family: tech, technique, technical, technician, technology
### Etymology Narrative

1833年在英语中由 technic 加 -ian 构成。technic 最终上溯希腊语 tekhnē（技艺、技能、手艺）。画面是匠人手上吃饭的本事。故本词指运用专门的实用知识做事的人。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: technic + -ian（做这行的人），溯至希腊语 tekhnē（手艺）→ 凭手上那套实用本事干活的人。
### Semantic Chain
工艺 -> 技术本领 -> 专司其业 -> 技术员

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/technician；technical family from Greek *tekhnē*.
- Direct formation is `technic + -ian`; `-ician` is not treated as the historical suffix.
- No aliases; related forms remain in family.
- confidence: uncertain

## temperature
### Identity
- lemma: temperature
- title: temperature
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈtemprətʃər/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: temperatures
### Short Definition
温度；体温；气温
### Core Definitions
1. 冷热程度的测量
   - en: the degree of heat or cold measured in a body or environment
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 人体体温
   - en: the degree of heat in a person's body
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 某地或某物的冷热状态
   - en: the heat condition of a place, substance, or system
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
temperature = 把身体、空气或物体的冷热状态量化为一个可比较的数值。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: temper- / Latin *temperare* (mix, moderate)
- suffix: -ature (recognition layer; whole Latin borrowing)
- family: temper, temperature, temperate, temperament, temperately
### Etymology Narrative

经法语溯自拉丁语 temperatura（恰当的调配、适中），出自动词 temperare（调和、调节、使适度）。画面是把冷热两种东西兑到刚刚好。由“处于均衡状态”这一观念，发展出物体与环境可测量的冷热状况。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 拉丁语 temperatura 本义是“兑得刚刚好”→ 冷热两样调到适中；后来成了这份冷热能量出的度数。
### Semantic Chain
调匀 -> 适中状态 -> 冷热之度 -> 温度

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/temperature；Latin *temperatura* < *temperare*.
- The modern physical sense is retained as the main branch; the older moderation frame is kept in the narrative.
- No aliases; body and environmental uses are semantic branches.
- confidence: uncertain

## temporal
### Identity
- lemma: temporal
- title: temporal
- pos: adj
- ipa: /ˈtempərəl/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: temporally
### Short Definition
时间的；世俗的；短暂的
### Core Definitions
1. 与时间有关的
   - en: relating to time or the passage of time
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 世俗的而非宗教的
   - en: relating to worldly rather than spiritual affairs
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 暂时的
   - en: lasting for only a limited period
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
### Prototype
temporal = 处在时间流逝和现实世界之中，而不是永恒或精神领域之外。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: tempor- / Latin *tempus* (time)
- suffix: -al (adjectival layer)
- family: temporal, temporally, temporary, contemporary
### Etymology Narrative

源自拉丁语 temporalis，出自 tempus（时间）。画面是沙漏里不断漏下的沙。与时间相关一义，推及在时间中展开的现世生活，以及被视为暂时而非永恒的状况。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: 拉丁语 tempus（时间），画面是沙漏里不断漏下的沙 → 属乎光阴的、在岁月里流过去的一切。
### Semantic Chain
时 -> 处于时间中 -> 世间生涯 -> 时间

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/temporal；Latin *temporalis* < *tempus*.
- The time and worldly senses are separate historical branches; neither is forced into the other.
- `temporally` is a derivative lookup form, not a self-reference.
- confidence: uncertain

## temporary
### Identity
- lemma: temporary
- title: temporary
- pos: adj/n
- ipa: /ˈtempəreri/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: temporarily
### Short Definition
暂时的；临时的；临时工作人员
### Core Definitions
1. 只持续有限时间的
   - en: lasting for a limited period
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 为短期使用或任职的
   - en: intended for short-term use, work, or employment
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
### Prototype
temporary = 被放进时间流中但只占据一个短暂区段，之后会被替换或结束。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: tempor- / Latin *tempus* (time)
- suffix: -ary (recognition layer; whole Latin borrowing)
- family: temporal, temporary, temporarily, temporariness
### Etymology Narrative

经拉丁语与法语溯自 tempus（时间）一族。画面是一段有限的时间、有始有终。本词描述限于某一时段之内的事物，后来也作名词，指短期的工作人员或安排。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 同出自 tempus（时间）一支 → 只占着一段时日，等长久的安排一到就腾出位子。
### Semantic Chain
时 -> 限于一时 -> 暂为之备 -> 临时

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/temporary；Latin *temporarius* / *tempus* line.
- The noun use is a later contextual specialization of the time-limited adjective.
- `temporarily` is a derivative lookup form, not a self-reference.
- confidence: uncertain

## temptation
### Identity
- lemma: temptation
- title: temptation
- pos: n
- ipa: /tempˈteɪʃən/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: temptations
### Short Definition
诱惑；诱因；引诱
### Core Definitions
1. 想做不应做之事的欲望
   - en: a strong desire to do something one should not do
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 引诱人行动的事物
   - en: something that attracts a person toward an action
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
### Prototype
temptation = 某种吸引力把人拉向一个当下有吸引力但可能不合适的选择。
### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: tempt- / Latin *temptare* (test, try, handle)
- suffix: -ation (action or result noun layer)
- family: tempt, temptation, tempting, tempter, tempted
### Etymology Narrative

经法语溯自拉丁语 temptationem，出自动词 temptare（试验、试探）。画面是把人放到试金石上掂一掂。由对一个人决心的考验，转为考验自制力的那种吸引力，以及由此而生的欲望。
### Mnemonic
- type: story
- text: 拉丁语 temptare 本义是“试验、试探”→ 把人放上试金石掂一掂，掂的就是他守不守得住。
### Semantic Chain
试探 -> 考验心志 -> 诱人动手 -> 诱惑

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/temptation；Latin *temptatio/temptationem* < *temptare*.
- Desire and tempting object are retained as two branches of the same testing frame.
- No aliases; `tempting` and `tempter` remain family derivatives.
- confidence: uncertain
