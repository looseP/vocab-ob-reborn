## narrative

### Identity
- lemma: narrative
- title: narrative
- pos: n/adj
- ipa: /ˈnærətɪv/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: narratives

### Short Definition
叙述；故事；叙事的

### Core Definitions
1. 叙述或讲述
   - en: a spoken or written account of connected events
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 故事或记叙文
   - en: a tale or story, especially an account of events
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 叙述的
   - en: relating to or characterized by narration
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B

### Prototype
narrative = 他把几年里的事按先后串成了一条线

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: narrat- / Latin *narrare* (tell, relate) < *gnarus* (knowing)
- suffix: -ive (adjectival layer, from Latin *-ivus*)
- family: narrate, narration, narrative, narrator

### Etymology Narrative

形容词约15世纪中叶出现，源自中古法语 narratif 及晚期拉丁语 narrativus（适于叙述的），出自拉丁语 narrat-（narrare 的词干）“讲述、叙述、陈说、解说”。名词“故事、连贯的记述”始于1560年代，由法语 narrative 及形容词转来。更深的关联把 narrare 与 gnarus（知晓的）相连，画面是把所知道的事说给人听；这是拉丁语内部的历史链条，不可按现代英语的前缀来拆。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: narrare（讲述、陈说）→ 把知晓的事一件件连贯讲出来，前后相接，成一整段有头有尾的话。

### Semantic Chain
知晓 -> 讲说转述 -> 连贯讲出 -> 叙述

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/narrative；adjective mid-15c. < Late Latin *narrativus* < Latin *narrare*；noun 1560s.
- `narr-` is a historical root from *narrare*; `-ive` is a historical adjectival layer, not a freely productive decomposition of every modern form.
- No aliases: narrator, narration, and narrate are related family members, not spelling variants or inflections of *narrative*.
- confidence: source-backed

## narrow

### Identity
- lemma: narrow
- title: narrow
- pos: adj/v
- ipa: /ˈnærəʊ/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: narrows, narrowed, narrowing

### Short Definition
狭窄的；有限的；勉强的；使变窄

### Core Definitions
1. 宽度小的
   - en: having little width or breadth
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 有限或狭隘的
   - en: limited in range, scope, or outlook
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 勉强的或差距很小的
   - en: involving only a small margin or distance
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
4. 变窄或使变窄
   - en: to become or make narrower
   - priority: 4
   - tags: B

### Prototype
narrow = 空间宽度受到压缩，继而表示范围、观点或差距受到限制。

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: narrow / Old English *nearu* (of little width, constricted, limited) < West Germanic *narwaz*
- suffix: none
- family: narrow, narrowly, narrowness, narrow-minded

### Etymology Narrative

经中古英语 narwe 溯自古英语 nearu（宽度小的、不宽不阔的；受束缚的、有限的），出自西日耳曼语 *narwaz。该词在其他日耳曼语中未见对应，来源不明。画面是两壁夹峙、只留一线缝隙。动词由形容词发展而来，古英语 nearwian 意为“挤入、束缚、限制；变小、收缩”。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 古英语 nearu → 两壁夹峙，中间只留一线缝；地方一局促，回旋的余地也就跟着少了。

### Semantic Chain
宽窄不足 -> 空间局促 -> 范围受限 -> 宽度小的

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/narrow；Middle English *narwe* < Old English *nearu* < West Germanic *narwaz*；origin stated as unknown.
- Do not connect *narrow* to *narrative* or *narr-*; the apparent resemblance is not evidence of shared etymology.
- The verb is historically derived from the adjective and Old English *nearwian*; narrowly and narrowness are derivatives, not aliases.
- confidence: source-backed

## national

### Identity
- lemma: national
- title: national
- pos: adj/n
- ipa: /ˈnæʃənəl/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: nationals

### Short Definition
国家的；全国的；民族的；国民

### Core Definitions
1. 国家或全国的
   - en: relating to a nation or a country as a whole
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 民族的或全体国民共有的
   - en: common to the people of a country or relating to a nation
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 国有或由国家维持的
   - en: established and maintained by the nation or its laws
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
4. 国民或某国公民（名词）
   - en: a citizen of a particular nation
   - priority: 4
   - tags: B

### Prototype
national = 把事物放在整个国家及其人民的范围内，而不是地方或个人范围内。

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: nation- / Latin *natio* (birth, people, nation) < *nasci* (be born)
- suffix: -al (adjectival layer, relating to)
- family: nation, national, nationality, nationally

### Etymology Narrative

约1590年代出现，源自法语 national，亦可视作 nation 加 -al。形容词义为“属于国家或视作整体之国家的”，后亦指由国家或其法律设立、维持的事物。名词指“某国国民”，始见于1887年。nation 的更早历史经古法语 nacion 溯至拉丁语 natio（出生、出身；族类），出自 nasci（出生），画面是“同出一源的一群人”。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: nation 本义是“同出生的一族人”→ 属于这一族、由这一族全体共同撑起来的那些事物。

### Semantic Chain
同出生的族类 -> 形成民族 -> 属全国共有 -> 国家

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/national；1590s < French *national* and *nation* + *-al*；noun “citizen” 1887.
- `-al` is the historical adjective-forming layer; the deeper *nat-* history belongs to *nation* and Latin *nasci*, not to an English prefix `nat-`.
- No aliases: nation, nationality, and nationally are related forms, not spelling variants or inflections of *national*.
- confidence: source-backed

## nationality

### Identity
- lemma: nationality
- title: nationality
- pos: n
- ipa: /næʃəˈnæləti/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: nationalities

### Short Definition
国籍；民族；种族群体

### Core Definitions
1. 国籍
   - en: the status of belonging to or being a citizen of a particular state
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 民族或种族群体
   - en: a racial or ethnic group or identity
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 国家作为共同体的独立性或统一性
   - en: the separate existence, unity, or integrity of a nation
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C

### Prototype
nationality = 那本深蓝色封皮的证件，决定了他过海关走哪条通道

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: national- / nation- / Latin *natio* (birth, people, nation)
- suffix: -ity (abstract-state layer, from Latin *-itas* through French *-ité*)
- family: nation, national, nationality, nationalities

### Etymology Narrative

约1690年代出现，由 national 加 -ity 构成，某些用法或许受法语 nationalité 影响。早期义为“作为国家的独立存在、国家的统一与完整”。“属于某国或其公民的身份”一义始于1828年，后逐渐偏向种族或族裔；“某个种族或族群”一义始见于1832年。后缀 -ity 表示状态或性质。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: national（属于某国的）+ -ity → 你归在哪一国名下的那个身份，白纸黑字印在护照上。

### Semantic Chain
民族 -> 国民身份 -> 公民资格 -> 国籍

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/nationality；1690s < *national* + *-ity*；citizenship sense 1828；racial/ethnic-group sense 1832.
- `-ity` is a historical abstract-noun layer from Latin *-itas* through French *-ité*; do not treat the whole word as a direct modern `nat- + -ity` formation.
- No aliases: national and nation are family members; nationalities is the regular plural, not an alias of the singular.
- confidence: source-backed

## necessary

### Identity
- lemma: necessary
- title: necessary
- pos: adj/n
- ipa: /ˈnesəsəri/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: necessaries

### Short Definition
必要的；必需的；必然的；必需品

### Core Definitions
1. 必要或必需的
   - en: needed, required, essential, or indispensable
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 必然或不可避免的
   - en: such as must be or cannot be otherwise
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 必需品（名词）
   - en: an indispensable or useful thing, especially a necessity of life
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B

### Prototype
necessary = 没有这枚螺丝，整台机器就转不起来

### Morphology
- prefix: ne- (not, no)
- root: cess- / Latin *necesse* < *cedere* (withdraw, go away, yield)
- suffix: -ary (adjectival layer, relating to)
- family: necessary, necessarily, necessity, necessitate

### Etymology Narrative

约14世纪末出现，源自古法语 necessaire 并直接出自拉丁语 necessarius（不可避免的、必需的），由 necesse（不可避免，本义"无可退避"）而来；拉丁语可拆为 ne-（不）＋ cedere（退去、避让、屈服）。亦作名词表"必需品"，复数为 necessaries。此属拉丁语历史分析，非谓现代英语由能产的 ne- 与 cess 自由构词。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: ne-（不）+ cedere（退避）= 退无可退 → 背后就是墙，躲不开也省不掉的那一样。

### Semantic Chain
无从退避 -> 避无可避 -> 缺它不行 -> 必要

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/necessary；late 14c. < Old French *necessaire* < Latin *necessarius* < *necesse*；*necesse* explained as *ne-* + *cedere*.
- Correct the legacy `ced-cess` note at the historical-source level, but do not present `ne- + cess + -ary` as a transparent modern English construction.
- Necessaries is the noun plural; necessity, necessarily, and necessitate are related derivatives, not aliases.
- confidence: source-backed

## necessitate

### Identity
- lemma: necessitate
- title: necessitate
- pos: v
- ipa: /nəˈsesɪteɪt/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: necessitates, necessitated, necessitating

### Short Definition
使成为必需；使不可避免；迫使

### Core Definitions
1. 使成为必需
   - en: to make something necessary or render it unavoidable
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 迫使或要求
   - en: to compel or oblige someone or something
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
necessitate = 某种情况使另一项行动或条件不可回避，从而客观上要求它发生。

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: necessitat- / Medieval Latin *necessitatus* < Latin *necessitas* (compulsion, necessity)
- suffix: -ate (historical verb-forming layer)
- family: necessitate, necessity, necessary, necessitation

### Etymology Narrative

约1620年代出现，意为“不可抗地迫使、强制”，亦作“使成为必要、使不可避免”。出自中世纪拉丁语 necessitatus（necessitare“使成为必要”的过去分词），溯至拉丁语 necessitas（强制；命运）。故本词在历史上与 necessity、necessary 相连，不宜简单按现代英语拆成 necessary ＋ -ate 的拼写操作。更早的英语动词 necessen 见于14世纪末。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 同根于 necesse（无可退避）→ 把事情逼到那个份上，叫人非如此做不可。

### Semantic Chain
必然情势 -> 使之难免 -> 强加于身 -> 使成为必需

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/necessitate；1620s < Medieval Latin *necessitatus* < *necessitare* “render necessary” < Latin *necessitas*.
- `-ate` is retained as a historical verb-forming layer, but the entry must be traced through *necessitas/necessitare* rather than mechanically derived from the modern adjective.
- No aliases: necessity, necessary, and necessitation are related family members; necessitates, necessitated, and necessitating are inflectional forms excluded from aliases.
- confidence: source-backed
