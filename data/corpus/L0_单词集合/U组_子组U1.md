# U组 · 子组U1（L1 生产词条）

## ultimate
### Identity
- lemma: ultimate
- title: ultimate
- pos: adj/n
- ipa: /ˈʌltɪmət/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: ultimately, ultimacy, ultimateness

### Short Definition
最终的；根本的；极端的；终极事物

### Core Definitions
1. 最终的、最后的
   - en: forming the final aim, object, or point in a series
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 根本的、首要的
   - en: basic or fundamental, beyond which no further cause or analysis is possible
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 极端的、达到最高程度的
   - en: reaching the greatest or most extreme degree
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B

### Prototype
ultimate = 到达最远、无法再超越的尽头。

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: ultim- / Latin *ultimus* (last, final, farthest)
- suffix: -ate (historical derivational/recognition layer; whole borrowing from Late Latin *ultimatus*)
- family: ultimately, ultimacy, ultimateness

### Etymology Narrative

源自晚期拉丁语 ultimatus（ultimare“成为最终、走到尽头”的过去分词），出自拉丁语 ultimus（最后的、最终的、最远的、最偏远的、极度的）。画面是走到了路的尽头、再无去处。由这一“最远端点”的意象，转出最终目的、极致程度，以及分析所无法再追的终极原因。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 拉丁语 ultimus（最远的、最后的）→ 一条路走到了尽头，再没有更远的地方可去。

### Semantic Chain
最远 -> 归到尽头 -> 极处之度 -> 最终

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline gives *ultimatus/ultimus*; `-ate` is retained only as a historical recognition layer, not a modern `ult + imate` split.
- In some contexts ultimate overlaps with final, but its “ultimate cause” sense points backward to the deepest origin rather than simply the last event; family is kept cautious and does not assert loose links such as *last* or *outmost*.
- confidence: source-backed

## underlie
### Identity
- lemma: underlie
- title: underlie
- pos: v
- ipa: /ʌndəˈlaɪ/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: underlay, underlain, underlying

### Short Definition
位于……之下；构成……的基础

### Core Definitions
1. 构成……的基础或根源
   - en: to form the basis or fundamental cause of something
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 位于……之下
   - en: to be situated under or beneath something
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
underlie = 躺在某物下面，成为其下方的支撑或基础。

### Morphology
- prefix: under- (beneath; historical English compound element)
- root: lie / Old English *licgan* (be situated, lie down)
- suffix:
- family: underlie, underlay, underlain, underlying

### Etymology Narrative

中古英语作 underlien，出自古英语 under licgan（居于其下、服从于），由 under 加 lie 构成。画面是垫在底下、托着上头的东西。“位于……之下”这一具体的空间义约1600年出现，比喻义“作为……的基础”随之而来，即由下方支撑的位置推及抽象的根基。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: 古英语 under licgan = under（在下）+ lie（位于）→ 垫在底下托着上头的；也指藏在表面之下的缘由。

### Semantic Chain
在底 -> 潜伏其下 -> 自下承托 -> 构成基础

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline records Middle English *underlien* from Old English *under licgan*; `under-` is an inherited English compound element here, not an abstract Latin prefix.
- `lie` is the root line; related forms are limited to the actual underlie paradigm and its present-participle adjective. Do not merge this family with *understand* merely because both begin with `under-`.
- confidence: source-backed

## underline
### Identity
- lemma: underline
- title: underline
- pos: v/n
- ipa: /ʌndəˈlaɪn/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: underlined, underlining

### Short Definition
在……下面画线；强调；突出

### Core Definitions
1. 在文字下面画线
   - en: to draw a line underneath written or printed words
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 强调或突出
   - en: to emphasize the importance or seriousness of something
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 下划线
   - en: a line drawn below written or printed matter
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C

### Prototype
underline = 在文字下方画线，使被标记内容醒目。

### Morphology
- prefix: under- (beneath; English compound element)
- root: line / Latin *linea* (linen thread, string, line)
- suffix:
- family: underline, underlined, underlining

### Etymology Narrative

1721年在英语中由 under 与 line 构成，最初意为“在下面画一道线作标记”。画面是在字底下划一道横线，使之醒目。这一具体的标记动作，引申为比喻上的“强调”。line 一词则经古法语与古英语上溯拉丁语 linea（亚麻线、细绳）。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: under + line（1721 年构成）→ 在字底下划一道横线，叫它跳出来；后来也指“把这一点看重”。

### Semantic Chain
字下添线 -> 标而记之 -> 引人注目 -> 画线

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline supports the English compound `under + line` and records the “mark underneath” sense from 1721; the emphasis sense is treated as a meaning extension of marking, not as a separate root.
- Keep this line-based family separate from *underlie* and *underlying*: the shared `under-` does not make `line` and `lie` the same root.
- confidence: source-backed

## underlying
### Identity
- lemma: underlying
- title: underlying
- pos: adj/v
- ipa: /ʌndəˈlaɪɪŋ/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: underlie, underlay, underlain

### Short Definition
根本的；潜在的；位于表面下的

### Core Definitions
1. 根本的、基础的
   - en: forming the fundamental basis or cause of something
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 潜在的、隐含的
   - en: existing beneath the surface and not immediately obvious
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 位于表面下的
   - en: lying beneath or supporting something physically
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B

### Prototype
underlying = 掀开表面那层，底下这根柱子才是撑着整座屋的

### Morphology
- prefix: under- (beneath; inherited English compound element)
- root: lie / Old English *licgan* (be situated, lie down)
- suffix: -ing (English present-participle/adjectival layer)
- family: underlie, underlay, underlain, underlying

### Etymology Narrative

本词是 underlie 的现在分词形容词，1610年代已见，意为“位于下方的、支撑着的”。画面是垫在底下、托着上层之物。其比喻义“根本的、基本的”约1852年出现，故构词方向是由 underlie 到 underlying，而非另有一个 under 加 lying 的独立词源故事。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: underlie 的现在分词形容词 → 冰山露在水面上的那一角，底下还垫着大得多的一块，那才是根子。

### Semantic Chain
藏于下 -> 在下支撑 -> 表面之下的 -> 根本

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline explicitly identifies *underlying* as a present-participle adjective from *underlie*; do not present `under + lying` as an independent etymological narrative.
- It is distinct from *underline*: *underlying* follows the `lie` root and the `-ing` formation, while *underline* follows the `line` root and has a separate “draw a line” history.
- confidence: source-backed

## understanding
### Identity
- lemma: understanding
- title: understanding
- pos: n/adj
- ipa: /ʌndəˈstændɪŋ/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: understand, understood, understandingly

### Short Definition
理解；理解力；谅解；共识

### Core Definitions
1. 理解或理解力
   - en: comprehension or the ability to comprehend something
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 谅解或共识
   - en: mutual agreement reached through shared comprehension
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 善解人意的、体谅的
   - en: showing sympathetic tolerance and forgiveness
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C

### Prototype
understanding = 他听完，点了头，接下来说到了点子上

### Morphology
- prefix: under- (historical “among/between” sense in *understand*; not simply “beneath”)
- root: stand- / Old English *standan* (stand)
- suffix: -ing (verbal-noun/adjectival layer)
- family: understand, understood, understanding, understandingly

### Etymology Narrative

本是古英语的动词性名词，意为“理解、领悟”，由 understand 构成，其古英语形式 understandan 即“理解、把握意思”。这一复合词大概本作“站在中间”解，其中 under 取“在……之间、在其中”之义，而非简单的“在下面”。画面是置身于事物之间、置身其中方能领会。后来“理解”一义引申为彼此的共识与同情包容。
### Mnemonic
- type: story
- text: 古英语 understandan，其中 under 取“在其间”，而非“在下面”→ 站到事情中间，看各部分怎么合得上。

### Semantic Chain
置身其间 -> 领会其意 -> 彼此通晓 -> 理解

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline presents the *understandan* analysis as probably “stand in the midst” and notes that sources disagree on whether this *under* is a distinct “among” sense; the entry does not use the folk “stand beneath” explanation.
- The root is `stand`, and the direct formation is `understand + -ing`; family includes *understand* and *understood*, but not unrelated `under-` compounds such as *underlie*.
- confidence: source-backed

## unemployment
### Identity
- lemma: unemployment
- title: unemployment
- pos: n
- ipa: /ʌnɪmˈplɔɪmənt/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: unemployed, employment, joblessness

### Short Definition
失业；失业状态；失业人数

### Core Definitions
1. 失业状态
   - en: the condition of being out of work
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 失业现象或失业人数
   - en: the level or condition of people who have no work
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
unemployment = 没有被雇用、因而处于无工作的状态。

### Morphology
- prefix: un- (not; English negating prefix)
- root: employ- / Old French *emploiier* < Latin *implicare* (enfold, involve)
- suffix: -ment (noun-forming layer in *employment*)
- family: unemployment, unemployed, employment, employ, employable, employer, employee

### Etymology Narrative

1887年构成，由英语前缀 un-（不）加 employment 而成。employment 原指“金钱的支出”，后指“一个人的固定职业或营生”，由 employ 加 -ment 构成；employ 经古法语上溯拉丁语 implicare（包入、卷入），画面是把人卷进某件事里、使其有事可做。故现代的社会义是“就业状态的否定”，不可拆成 em- 前缀来分析。
### Mnemonic
- type: contrast
- text: un-（不）+ employment，后者上溯拉丁语 implicare（卷入其中）→ 前头加个“不”，便是手里没了活计。

### Semantic Chain
雇用 -> 有业在身 -> 不再受雇 -> 失业

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline gives the direct formation as `un- + employment` (1887); *employment* is `employ + -ment`, and *employ* ultimately follows *implicare* “enfold, involve.”
- Do not parse `em-` as an English prefix in this word. `un-` is the productive English negation prefix, while `-ment` belongs to the employment formation.
- confidence: source-backed

## unexpected
### Identity
- lemma: unexpected
- title: unexpected
- pos: adj
- ipa: /ʌnɪkˈspektɪd/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: expected, unexpectedly, unexpectedness

### Short Definition
意外的；想不到的；未预料到的

### Core Definitions
1. 意料之外的
   - en: not looked for, expected, or foreseen
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 突如其来的
   - en: happening in a way that was not anticipated
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
unexpected = 事件越过预期位置，突然出现在视线之外。

### Morphology
- prefix: un- (not; English negating prefix)
- root: spect- / Latin *exspectare* (look out for, await)
- suffix: -ed (English past-participial/adjectival layer)
- family: expect, expected, expecting, unexpectedly, expectation

### Etymology Narrative

1580年代由 un-（不）加 expect 的过去分词构成，意为“未曾料到、未预见到的”。expect 源自拉丁语 exspectare（等候、向外张望），出自动词 spectare（看）。画面是伸长脖子往前看。故本词的语义走向是：由向前张望，转为缺少一个预见中的事件。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: 字根 spect 是“看”→ 预先没望见它的影儿，它就来了。

### Semantic Chain
张望以待 -> 心中期待 -> 未曾料到 -> 意外

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline supports `un- + expected`; `-ed` is the English past-participial/adjectival layer, and `spect-` is retained as the root recognition line.
- Do not treat `ex-` as the prefix of *unexpected*: it belongs inside the history of *expect/exspectare*, while the word-level negating prefix is only `un-`.
- confidence: source-backed

## unify
### Identity
- lemma: unify
- title: unify
- pos: v
- ipa: /ˈjuːnɪfaɪ/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: unified, unifying, unifier, unification

### Short Definition
统一；使成为一体；使一致

### Core Definitions
1. 使成为一体、统一
   - en: to make separate parts or groups into one whole
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 使一致或协调
   - en: to make ideas, systems, or standards uniform or consistent
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 统一、联合（不及物）
   - en: to become uniform or unified
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B

### Prototype
unify = 把分散的部分合成一个整体。

### Morphology
- prefix: uni- (Latin “one”; not the English negative *un-*)
- root: fac-/fic- / Latin *facere* (make)
- suffix: -ify (verb-forming “make” layer)
- family: unify, unified, unifying, unifier, unification, reunify

### Etymology Narrative

约1500年出现，源自古法语 unifier，或直接出自晚期拉丁语 unificare（使成为一），由拉丁语 uni-（一）加 facere（做、使）的组合形式构成。画面是把散落的几件东西合成一件。这一“使成为一”的意象，发展为现代把群体、系统或观念合为统一、齐整之整体的诸义。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: uni-（一）+ -fy（使），别误看成 un- → 把散落的几件并成一件。

### Semantic Chain
单一 -> 合而为一 -> 并其碎部 -> 统一

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline supports Old French *unifier* / Late Latin *unificare*, from Latin *uni-* “one” plus a combining form of *facere* “make”; `uni-` is not the English negative prefix `un-`.
- Family is limited to forms directly linked from the *unify* entry; *union*, *unity*, and *unite* are not added as automatic family members without separate evidence for the intended relation.
- confidence: source-backed
