## other

### Identity
- lemma: other
- title: other
- pos: adj/pron
- ipa: /ˈʌðə/
- cefr: A2
- aliases: 

### Short Definition
其他的；另一个；不同的；另外

### Core Definitions
1. 另一个或其他的
   - en: a different person or thing from the one already mentioned or in view
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 额外的或其余的
   - en: additional, further, or remaining
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 另一方或另一组中的成员
   - en: one of two or more people or things considered in relation to one another
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
4. 另外地（副词）
   - en: in another way or additionally
   - priority: 4
   - tags: B

### Prototype
other = 这只不合适，他伸手拿了旁边那只

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: *anthera- / Old English *oþer* (second, other)
- suffix: -tero- (historical comparative element in the deeper PIE analysis)
- family: other, another, otherwise, otherness

### Etymology Narrative

源自古英语 oþer（二者中之第二；另外的、进一步的），出自原始日耳曼语 *anthera-；与 PIE *an-tero- 相系，或为 *al-tero-（两者中的另一个）的变体，但该页亦记有涉及 eno-/ono- 的另析。古义"第二"因易歧义而从 other 分出，现代英语改用 second 表此义。此属历史比较分析，非现代能产的后缀规则。
### Mnemonic
- type: contrast
- text: 古英语 oþer 本指“二者中的第二个”→ 把目光从这一个挪开，落到了剩下的那一个上。

### Semantic Chain
两者中另一 -> 不同的一者 -> 余下的一群 -> 另一个

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/other；Old English *oþer* < Proto-Germanic *anthera-*；deeper PIE analysis recorded as possible, not certain.
- Do not treat the visible `oth-` as a modern English root plus a productive suffix; the form is inherited through Germanic sound change.
- *another*, *otherwise*, and *otherness* are related derivatives or compounds, not aliases of *other*.
- confidence: source-backed

## otherwise

### Identity
- lemma: otherwise
- title: otherwise
- pos: adv/adj
- ipa: /ˈʌðəwaɪz/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: 

### Short Definition
否则；在其他方面；以不同方式

### Core Definitions
1. 否则
   - en: in circumstances different from those just stated or implied
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 以另一种方式
   - en: in a different manner or by different means
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 在其他方面
   - en: apart from the matter under discussion
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B
4. 不同的（形容词）
   - en: different from what has been mentioned
   - priority: 4
   - tags: B

### Prototype
otherwise = 把同一情况换成另一种方式、条件或方面来说明。

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: other / Old English *oþer* + wise / Old English *wise* (manner, way)
- suffix: -wise (historical adverbial word-forming element)
- family: other, wise, likewise, otherwise

### Etymology Narrative

由古英语短语 on oðre wisan（以另一种方式）缩合而成，中经中古英语 oþre wise、oþerwise。副词形式约1200年已见，形容词用法约1400年起。后一半是名词 wise（方式、样子、做法），而不是现代意为“聪明”的形容词 wise；画面是“走另一条路子”。故本词历史上结构透明，但并非简单的否定前缀构词。
### Mnemonic
- type: contrast
- text: other（别的）+ wise（方式，不是“聪明”）→ 换个样子的情形：若是不照前面说的那样办。

### Semantic Chain
其他 -> 别样情状 -> 换种方式 -> 否则

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/otherwise；Old English *on oðre wisan*；Middle English *oþre wise*；adverb c. 1200, adjective c. 1400.
- Here *-wise* means “in the manner of,” historically from the noun *wise* “way,” not an English suffix meaning intelligence.
- *other* and *wise* are source components; *likewise* is a parallel compound, not an alias.
- confidence: source-backed

- type: image

### Semantic Chain
向外 -> 更远在外 -> 临外之面 -> 外部

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/outer；late 14c. comparative of *out*, by analogy with *inner*；compare Old English *uttera*.
- Do not merge *outer* with the later adjective *utter* merely because both are historically related to older comparative forms.
- *outward* and *outside* are derivatives or compounds, not aliases.

## outlet

### Identity
- lemma: outlet
- title: outlet
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈaʊtlet/

### Short Definition
出口；排出口；发泄途径；销售店

### Core Definitions
1. 让事物流出或逃出的开口
   - en: a place or opening through which something is let out or escapes
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 河口或排出口
   - en: a point where water or another substance flows out
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 情绪或能量的释放途径
   - en: a means of relief, expression, or discharge
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C
4. 电源插座或零售店
   - en: a socket for electrical supply or a store selling products
   - priority: 4
   - tags: C

### Prototype
outlet = 某物从封闭空间、压力状态或生产系统中向外释放的通道。

### Morphology
- prefix: out- (outward, away)
- root: let / Old English *lǣtan* (allow, let go)
- suffix: none
- family: let, outlet, out, let out

### Etymology Narrative

名词约13世纪中叶已见，原指“某物得以泄出或逸出的口子”，亦指“河口”，由 out- 加 let 构成，画面是“放它出去的口子”。“排解、宣泄的途径”这一比喻义始于1620年代。电学上指“把装置接上电源的插座”，始见于1892年；商业上的“销路、门店”等义后起。本复合词历史上是基于动词 let（让……走）构成的。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: out + let（放）→ 放它出去的那个口子：水、电流、憋着的一口气，都从这儿泄。

### Semantic Chain
放走 -> 放将出去 -> 逸出之口 -> 出口

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/outlet；mid-13c. “place or opening by which anything is let out or escapes; a river mouth” < *out-* + *let*；electrical sense 1892; commercial sense later.
- The noun is a compound of historical *out* and *let*; do not treat `-let` as a diminutive suffix here.
- *let* and *let out* are source-related forms, not aliases of *outlet*.
- confidence: source-backed

### Core Definitions

   - en: the quantity produced by a person, machine, system, or industry

- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/output；1839 < *out-* + *put*；energy/system sense by 1884; verb “produce” 1858.
- Do not analyze the final `-put` as a suffix; *put* is the lexical verb in the compound.
- *input* and *outcome* are parallel compounds, not aliases; inflections are excluded from aliases.

1. 彻底淹没

   - priority: 1

   - en: to affect someone with more force or emotion than they can manage

### Morphology
- prefix: over- (above, across, excessively)
- root: whelm / Middle English *whelmen* (turn upside down; cover)
- suffix: none
- family: overwhelm, whelm, underwhelm, overwhelming

### Etymology Narrative

### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: over-（盖过）+ whelm（翻转、蒙住）→ 一个浪头从头顶扣下来，把人整个罩住、按进水里。

### Semantic Chain
倾覆 -> 蒙头盖住 -> 没入水中 -> 淹没

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/overwhelm；mid-14c. *overwhelmen* < *over-* + Middle English *whelmen*；submerge early 15c.; figurative ruin 1520s.
- Treat *whelm* as the historical lexical base; do not invent a modern English suffix or equate `-whelm` with *helm* “helmet.”
- *underwhelm* is a later contrastive formation; *overwhelming* and *overwhelmed* are related or inflectional forms, not aliases.
- confidence: source-backed
