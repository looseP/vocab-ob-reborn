## take
### Identity
- lemma: take
- title: take
- pos: v/n
- ipa: /teɪk/
- cefr: A1
- aliases: taken

### Short Definition
拿；带走；接受；采取

### Core Definitions
1. 把某物拿到手中
   - en: to get or hold something with the hands
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 带走或运送
   - en: to move a person or thing from one place to another
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 接受、选择或采取
   - en: to accept, choose, or adopt something
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B

### Prototype
take = 他伸手从架上抽出一本，夹在腋下就走

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: tak-/take- / Old English *tacan* (take, grasp)
- suffix: none
- family: take, taken, taking, takeaway, retake

### Etymology Narrative

本词是日耳曼语动词，经古 Norse 语进入英语，在许多用法上取代了古英语的旧形。核心意象是伸手抓住某物、把它置于自己掌控之下；画面是一把攥住、拿走。接受、选择、运送、采取行动等义，都由这一框架发展而来。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 经古诺尔斯语进入英语 → 一把攥住、拢到自己手底下；收下、挑拣、运走，都打这儿来。

### Semantic Chain
抓紧 -> 据为己控 -> 取走他移 -> 拿、取走

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/take；Germanic/Old Norse *taka* and Old English replacement history.
- The entry has many historical branches; this L1 note keeps the recognition-sized grasp/control prototype.
- `taken` is a lookup form, not a self-reference alias.
- confidence: uncertain

### Short Definition
天赋；才能；人才；才能展示

### Core Definitions
1. 自然的能力或天赋
   - en: a natural ability or aptitude
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 有特殊能力的人
   - en: a person or group with notable ability
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 演艺或专业能力的总称
   - en: ability considered as a resource in performance or work
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C

### Prototype
talent = 原本是可计量的重量和货币单位，后来转指一个人所拥有的可贵能力。

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: talent- / Greek *talanton* through Latin *talentum* (weight, sum)
- suffix: none
- family: talent, talented, talentless

### Etymology Narrative

经拉丁语溯自希腊语 talanton（一个重量单位、一笔钱）。画面是称银子的那个秤砣与它所代表的数目。圣经中的比喻用法及其后来的引申，把这个贵重单位转为一个受托付之人的才能，遂生出现代“天赋、才能”与“有才之士”诸义。
### Mnemonic
- type: story
- text: 希腊语 talanton 本是称银子的一个重量单位 → 主人按各人能耐托付银子，这单位便转指天生的禀赋。

### Semantic Chain
分量 -> 一笔钱款 -> 受托之才 -> 天赋

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/talent；Greek *talanton* through Latin *talentum*.
- The money-to-ability metaphor is retained; religious and literary mediation is summarized rather than overclaimed.
- No aliases; `talented` and `talentless` remain family derivatives.
- confidence: uncertain

### Core Definitions

   - en: the quality of food or drink perceived by the tongue
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 品尝或尝试
   - en: to sample food or experience something

   - priority: 3

- suffix: none

源自古法语 taster，出自俗拉丁语 *tastare（taxtare 的变体），与拉丁语 taxare（评定、触摸）相关。画面是先用手指碰一碰、试试看，再送入口中尝一尝。由实际的触碰与试测，发展为品尝，进而引申为体验一番、以及评判艺术或社交上的品位。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 古法语 taster 上溯俗拉丁语 *tastare（与 taxare“触摸、评定”相关）→ 先伸手碰一碰，再送进嘴里。

### Semantic Chain
触碰 -> 伸舌尝味 -> 短暂体验 -> 味道

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/taste；Old French *taster* < Vulgar Latin *\*tastare*, via *taxtare/taxare* “evaluate, handle.”
- The unsupported *tangere/tact-* link is rejected; the sensory-to-judgment development is retained conservatively.
- `tasted` is a lookup form and not a self-reference alias.
- confidence: uncertain

### Short Definition

### Core Definitions

   - en: relating to technology, machines, or practical techniques
   - priority: 1

   - priority: 3

### Prototype

### Morphology
- prefix: none
- root: technic- / English *technic* < Latin *technicus* < Greek *tekhnikos* < *tekhnē* (art, skill)
- suffix: -al (English adjectival formation)
- family: tech, technique, technology, technical, technician

### Etymology Narrative

在英语中由 technic 加 -al 构成，部分受到一个拉丁化希腊语形式的影响；technic 最终出自希腊语 tekhnē（技艺、技能、手艺）。画面是匠人手上那套吃饭的本事。其义由实用的技艺，发展为专门知识、机械装置与精确的程序。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: 同出于希腊语 tekhnē（手艺）→ 非得懂那一行门道才弄得明白、做得下来的事。

### Semantic Chain
工艺 -> 实作之技 -> 专精之知 -> 技术

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/technical；Greek *tekhnē* through the technical word family.
- Direct formation is `technic + -al`; the surface ending is not analyzed as a single historical `-ical` suffix.
- `technically` is a lookup derivative and not a self-reference alias.
- confidence: uncertain
