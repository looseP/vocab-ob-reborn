# S组 · 子组S19（L1 生产词条）

## suppress
### Identity
- lemma: suppress
- title: suppress
- pos: v
- ipa: /səˈpres/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: suppresss, suppressed, suppressing

### Short Definition
压制；抑制；隐瞒

### Core Definitions
1. 阻止某事表达或发生
   - en: to prevent something from being expressed or occurring
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 压制人或群体
   - en: to use force or authority to hold back a person or group
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 隐瞒信息或情绪
   - en: to keep information or a feeling from being revealed
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B

### Prototype
suppress = 把东西向下压住，使它不能向外表达或继续发展。

### Morphology
- prefix: sub-/sup- (under, down)
- root: press- / Latin *premere* (press)
- suffix: none
- family: suppress, suppression, suppressive, suppressant

### Etymology Narrative

源自拉丁语 suppressus（supprimere“压下”的过去分词），由 sub-（在下）加 premere（压）构成。画面是一只手把东西死死按下去、不让它起来。由物理上的按压，发展出压制言论、行动、团体、消息或情感。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: sub-（在下）+ premere（压）→ 一只手死死按住，不叫它起来，也不叫它出声。

### Semantic Chain
下压 -> 按住不放 -> 禁其发声 -> 压制

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/suppress；Latin *suppressus* < *supprimere* < *sub-* + *premere*.
- The prefix form is recorded as historical assimilation; it is not treated as a modern free split.
- The physical, political, informational, and emotional branches share the hold-down frame.
- confidence: uncertain

## surpass
### Identity
- lemma: surpass
- title: surpass
- pos: v
- ipa: /sərˈpæs/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: surpasss, surpassed, surpassing

### Short Definition
超过；胜过；优于

### Core Definitions
1. 在数量、程度或质量上超过
   - en: to be greater than something in amount, degree, or quality
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 胜过某人或某标准
   - en: to perform better than a person or standard
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
surpass = 越过原有界线或水平，达到更高的位置。

### Morphology
- prefix: sur-/super- (over, beyond)
- root: pass- / Old French *passer* (pass, go by)
- suffix: none
- family: surpass, surpassed, surpassing, surpassable

### Etymology Narrative

经法语 surpasser 进入英语，由 sur-（越过）加 passer（经过）构成。画面是跨过一道界线、走到它那边去。基本意象是越过某个边界或水平，遂发展为超过某个数量、标准或对手。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: sur-（越过）+ passer（经过）→ 一脚跨过那道界线，落到它那一边去了。

### Semantic Chain
前行而过 -> 越其界限 -> 压倒同侪 -> 超过

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/surpass；French *surpasser* < *sur-* + *passer*.
- The French compound is retained as the historical route; modern English morphology is not asserted beyond the source line.
- Quantitative, qualitative, and competitive senses remain one exceedance family.
- confidence: uncertain

## surplus
### Identity
- lemma: surplus
- title: surplus
- pos: n/adj
- ipa: /ˈsɜːrpləs/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: surpluses

### Short Definition
过剩；剩余；多余的

### Core Definitions
1. 超出需要的数量
   - en: an amount that remains after more than enough has been supplied
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 多余的或额外的
   - en: more than what is needed or used
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
surplus = 在满足需要之后仍然留下来的部分。

### Morphology
- prefix: super- (above, beyond)
- root: plus / Latin *plus* (more)
- suffix: none
- family: surplus, superplus, surplusage

### Etymology Narrative

源自盎格鲁-法语 surplus，出自拉丁语 superplus（超出其上的），由 super（在上、超过）加 plus（更多）构成。画面是斗里的粮食漫出来、多过所需。本词指超过所需数量的部分，后来兼作名词与形容词。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: sur-（超出）+ plus（更多）→ 用度都满了之后，还剩下的那一部分。

### Semantic Chain
有余 -> 多出所需 -> 余下之数 -> 盈余

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/surplus；Anglo-French *surplus* < Latin *superplus*.
- The Latin components are historical evidence, not a claim that modern English freely builds this word from `super + plus`.
- Noun and adjective uses are parallel grammatical branches of excess.
- confidence: uncertain

## surprise
### Identity
- lemma: surprise
- title: surprise
- pos: n/v
- ipa: /sərˈpraɪz/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: surprises, surprises, surprised, surprising

### Short Definition
惊讶；意外；使惊讶

### Core Definitions
1. 未预料的事件
   - en: an unexpected event or feeling
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 突然使某人感到意外
   - en: to cause someone to feel sudden astonishment
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 突袭或出其不意地取得优势
   - en: to attack or encounter someone unexpectedly
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C

### Prototype
surprise = 门后忽然跳出一个人，他手里的杯子掉了

### Morphology
- prefix: sur-/super- (over, unexpectedly)
- root: prise-/prehend- / Old French *surprendre*, Latin *prehendere* (seize)
- suffix: none
- family: surprise, surprised, surprising, surprisingly

### Etymology Narrative

经古法语 surprendre（赶上、制服）而来，由 sur-（在上、越过）加拉丁语 prehendere（抓住）构成。画面是一只手从背后出其不意地把你抓住。被出其不意地抓住，遂发展出惊愕、意外的事件与突袭诸义。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 古法语 surprendre = sur-（越过）+ prehendere（抓住）→ 一只手从背后出其不意把你攥住。

### Semantic Chain
自上方抓下 -> 猝然袭来 -> 毫无防备 -> 意外

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/surprise；Old French *surprendre* < Latin *super-* / *prehendere*.
- The exact French and Latin intermediate spellings are simplified here; the seize-unexpectedly frame is retained.
- Event, emotion, verb, and tactical senses are related branches rather than separate roots.
- confidence: uncertain

## surveillance
### Identity
- lemma: surveillance
- title: surveillance
- pos: n
- ipa: /sərˈveɪləns/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: surveillances

### Short Definition
监视；监督；监控

### Core Definitions
1. 对人或地点的持续观察
   - en: close and continuous observation of a person or place
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 对活动或系统的监控
   - en: systematic monitoring of an activity or system
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
surveillance = 从上方保持警觉，持续观察某人或某事是否发生变化。

### Morphology
- prefix: sur- (over, above)
- root: veil-/vigil- / French *veiller* from Latin *vigilare* (watch)
- suffix: -ance (state or act noun layer)
- family: surveillance, surveil, surveillant

### Etymology Narrative

源自法语 surveillance，出自动词 surveiller（监视、看管），由 sur-（在上）加 veiller（守夜、注视）构成，最终与拉丁语 vigilare（保持警醒）相关。画面是站在高处、彻夜盯着下面的人与物。本词保留着对人物、处所或系统加以监视之意。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: sur-（在上）+ veiller（守夜、注视）→ 站在高处彻夜盯着下头，一刻也不挪眼。

### Semantic Chain
张望 -> 守望在侧 -> 连绵观察 -> 监视

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/surveillance；French *surveillance* < *surveiller* < *sur-* + *veiller*.
- The Latin depth is kept as a cautious related line; the French route is the direct source path.
- Physical observation and institutional monitoring are branches of watch-over.
- confidence: uncertain
