## objection

### Identity
- lemma: objection
- title: objection
- pos: n
- ipa: /əbˈdʒekʃən/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: objections

### Short Definition
反对；异议；反对理由

### Core Definitions
1. 反对意见或理由
   - en: a reason or argument given against something
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 正式异议
   - en: a formal statement of opposition, especially in a legal or public discussion
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 反对的行为
   - en: the act of objecting to a proposal or action
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B

### Prototype
objection = 把某个主张或行动挡在面前，并说明为什么不能接受它。

### Morphology
- prefix: ob- (in front of, against)
- root: ject- / Latin *iacere* (to throw)
- suffix: -ion (action/result noun layer)
- family: object, objection, objective, objector

### Etymology Narrative

约14世纪末出现，源自古法语 objeccion（答复、反驳），并直接出自晚期拉丁语 obiectionem（投掷或放置于前），是拉丁语 obicere（反对）的动作名词。该拉丁词可析为 ob-“在前、相抗”加 iacere（投掷），画面是把东西扔到对方面前挡回去。这是拉丁语内部的历史分析，并非说现代英语可自由拼合这两部分。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: ob-（在前、相抗）+ iacere（投掷）→ 把一块石头掷到议案跟前，横在路上拦它一拦。

### Semantic Chain
抛到面前 -> 横加阻挡 -> 据理反驳 -> 反对

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/objection；late 14c. < Old French *objeccion* < Late Latin *obiectionem* < Latin *obicere*.
- `ob-` and `-ject-` describe the historical Latin route through *obicere*; they are not asserted as a productive English decomposition.
- No aliases: object, objective, and objector are related family members, not aliases.
- confidence: source-backed

## observation

### Identity
- lemma: observation
- title: observation
- pos: n
- ipa: /ɒbzəˈveɪʃən/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: observations

### Short Definition
观察；观测；观察结果或评论

### Core Definitions
1. 观察行为
   - en: the act of watching, noticing, or paying attention
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 科学观测
   - en: the process of gathering information by watching or measuring
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 观察到的评论或事实
   - en: a remark or fact based on something observed
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B

### Prototype
observation = 把注意力放在对象上并记录由此看到、测量或注意到的内容。

### Morphology
- prefix: ob- (in front of, before)
- root: serv- / Latin *servare* (watch, keep safe)
- suffix: -ation (action/result noun layer)
- family: observe, observer, observation, observant

### Etymology Narrative

约14世纪末出现，最初指“举行宗教仪式”。源自古法语 observation，并直接出自拉丁语 observationem（看守、遵守、考察），来自动词 observare（看守、留意、注意、护卫）。observare 可析为 ob-“在前”加 servare（看守、保全），画面是站在前面盯着、守护不放。“注意的行为或事实”一义始于1550年代，“就所见事物发表的评论”始于1590年代。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: observare = ob-（对着）+ servare（看守）→ 眼睛盯住不放，看得够久，把细节一一记下来。

### Semantic Chain
看守 -> 留意 -> 细加考察 -> 观察

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/observation；late 14c. < Old French *observation* < Latin *observationem* < *observare*.
- The historical *ob-* + *servare* analysis explains the Latin source; it is not a free modern English prefix rule.
- No aliases: observe and observer are family members; they are not spelling variants of the noun.
- confidence: source-backed

## obsession

- title: obsession
- pos: n
- ipa: /əbˈseʃən/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: obsessions

### Short Definition
痴迷；执念；强迫性念头

### Core Definitions
1. 对某事的强烈执着
   - en: an idea or interest that continually occupies the mind
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 心理上的侵入性念头
   - en: an unwanted idea or image that repeatedly intrudes on the mind
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C
3. 压倒性的关注对象
   - en: a person or thing that engrosses someone completely
   - priority: 3
   - tags: C

### Prototype
obsession = 一个念头像围城一样反复占据心智，使注意力难以转向别处。

### Morphology
- prefix: ob- (against, in front of)
- root: sess- / Latin *sedere* (to sit)
- suffix: -ion (state/action noun layer)
- family: obsess, obsessed, obsession, obsessive

### Etymology Narrative

约1510年代出现，原义为“围困的行为”。源自法语 obsession，并直接出自拉丁语 obsessionem（围困、封锁、堵塞），来自 obsidere（围困）的过去分词词干。相关动词 obsess 出自拉丁语 obsessus，字面“坐在对面”，由 ob-“对着、相抗”加 sedere（坐）构成。画面是敌兵扎营坐在城对面不走——死死围住。“某事占据心神”的心理义始于1670年代，“侵入性念头或意象”的心理学义始于1901年。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: obsidere 本义是“围困、封锁”→ 一个念头像敌兵般扎营不走，把心神围得水泄不通。

### Semantic Chain
坐镇对面 -> 围困缠住 -> 占满心神 -> 对某事的强烈执着

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/obsession；1510s < French *obsession* < Latin *obsessionem*; psychological sense 1901.
- The “siege” origin and later mental sense must remain distinct; do not reduce the modern noun to a literal military meaning.
- No aliases: obsess and obsessive are related forms, not aliases.
- confidence: source-backed

2. 被新事物取代的

### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: ob-（离去）+ solere（习用）→ 渐渐没人再使唤它，搁在墙角蒙了尘、也失了光泽。

### Semantic Chain
曾常用 -> 废置不用 -> 陈旧落伍 -> 不再使用

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/obsolete；1570s < Latin *obsoletus* < *obsolescere*; deeper *ob-* + *solere* analysis stated as probable.
- Do not present `ob- + sol- + -ete` as a certain modern English construction; the source explicitly limits the deeper etymology.
- No aliases: obsolesce and obsolescence are derivatives, not aliases.
- confidence: uncertain

occupy = 把空间、时间、领土、职位或注意力抓住并持续占着。

- prefix: ob- (over, toward)
- root: cup- / Latin *capere* (grasp, seize)

- family: occupy, occupant, occupation, preoccupy

### Etymology Narrative

约14世纪中叶，出自古法语 ocuper / occuper，或直接源自拉丁语 occupare（接管、夺取、占为己有、占据）。occupare 可析为 ob-“在上、覆盖”加上 capere（抓、夺）的强化形式。画面是伸手把某物一把抓住、据为己有。末尾音节的拼法难以解释，但其由来与记录本身同样古老。16至17世纪曾有过委婉的性含义，今已避用；该历史义不属于本词的核心释义。
### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: ob-（覆盖）+ capere（抓）→ 伸手一把抓住、攥着不放：地方、位子、心思，都这么被占住了。

### Semantic Chain
抓住 -> 夺取在手 -> 据有处所 -> 占据

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline: https://www.etymonline.com/word/occupy；mid-14c. < Old French *ocuper/occuper* or Latin *occupare* < *ob-* + intensive *capere*.
- Do not invent a suffix analysis for the final syllable; Etymonline says its explanation is difficult.
- The historical euphemistic sense is recorded but excluded from the L1 core sense set.
- No aliases: occupant and occupation are related derivatives, not aliases.
- confidence: source-backed
