## representative

### Identity
- lemma: representative
- title: representative
- pos: n/adj
- ipa: /reprɪˈzentətɪv/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: representatives

### Short Definition
代表，代理人；代表性的，典型的

### Core Definitions
1. 代表，代理人
   - en: a person chosen or appointed to act or speak for others
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 代表性的，典型的
   - en: typical of a class or group; serving as an example
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
representative = 被派出来"再次呈现"某群体的人

### Morphology
- prefix: re- (back, again)
- root: present (L praesentare "to present, exhibit" < praesens "at hand")
- suffix: -ative
- family: represent, representation

### Etymology Narrative
 由 represent＋-ative 构成。represent（14世纪晚期）出自拉丁语 repraesentare（呈现、展示、陈列），由 re-“回、再”＋praesentare（呈现，源自 praesens“在场的、当下的”）构成。故 representative 即“代他人再次呈现者”。名词义“代表、代理人”自1640年代有载。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: re-（back/again）+ present（呈现）+ -ative → 会场里他举手，身后二十个人的意见都由他开口

### Semantic Chain
再次呈现 -> 代为出面 -> 众人所推 -> 代表；代表性的

### Reviewer Notes
- Keep.
- representative = represent + -ative ← L repraesentare (re- + praesentare)；root `present (L praesentare "to present, exhibit" < praesens "at hand")`，prefix `re- (back, again)`，suffix `-ative`。family: represent/representation 同根 present-。Etymonline 直核。
