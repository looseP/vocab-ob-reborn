## wagon

### Identity

- lemma: wagon
- title: wagon
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈwæɡən/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: wagons

### Short Definition

四轮运货车；铁路货车车厢

### Core Definitions
1. 四轮运货车；四轮马车
   - en: a four-wheeled vehicle used for carrying goods or people
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

2. 铁路货车车厢
   - en: a railway vehicle used for carrying goods
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype

wagon = 一辆用四个轮子承载并运送人或货物的车

### Morphology

- prefix: 
- root: wagon (whole borrowing)
- suffix: 
- family: wagoner, wain (doublet)

### Etymology Narrative
 15世纪晚期，出自中古荷兰语 wagen 或 waghen（四轮货车），是日耳曼语的车辆词。英语中的同源异形词 wain 经古英语而来，wagon 则较晚经荷兰语进入。 

### Mnemonic

- type: image
- text: 想象一辆四轮 wagon 满载货物缓缓前行；wain 是它较古老的同源双生词。

### Semantic Chain
轮载具 -> 四轮货车 -> 铁路货车 -> 四轮运货车

### Reviewer Notes

- Keep.
- vehicle 只是释义上位词，不列入近 family。
- confidence: source-backed

## wake

### Identity

- lemma: wake
- title: wake
- pos: v/n
- ipa: /weɪk/
- cefr: A2
- aliases: wakes, woke, woken, waking

### Short Definition

醒来；唤醒；守夜；船只尾流

### Core Definitions
1. 醒来；唤醒
   - en: to stop sleeping or make someone stop sleeping
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

2. 守夜；守灵
   - en: a vigil, especially one held beside a dead person
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

3. 船只尾流；航迹
   - en: the track of disturbed water left behind a moving ship
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B

### Prototype

wake = 从睡眠进入清醒；另有一条独立名词线表示船后留下的水迹

### Morphology

- prefix: 
- root: wake₁ (awake/vigil line) / wake₂ (ship-trail line)
- suffix: 
- family: waken, waking, awake, awaken (wake₁ line only)

### Etymology Narrative
 “醒来”与“守夜”诸义承自古英语中表“起身、醒着”的动词，以及守夜不眠的习俗。指船行尾迹的名词另出一源，或出自斯堪的纳维亚语中表船后水道或航迹的词；这一路径尚不确定。 

### Mnemonic

- type: contrast
- text: wake₁ 是“人醒着、守着”；wake₂ 是“船开过后留下的水路”，两条来源分开记。

### Semantic Chain
睡 -> 醒转 -> 守夜或船迹 -> 醒来

### Reviewer Notes

- Keep.
- 尾流来源只写 perhaps，不并入醒来词族。
- confidence: source-backed

水；浇水；供水

   - en: the clear liquid essential for life

## waterproof

### Identity

- lemma: waterproof
- title: waterproof
- pos: adj/v/n
- ipa: /ˈwɔːtəpruːf/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: waterproofs, waterproofed, waterproofing

### Short Definition

防水的；使防水；防水材料或衣物

### Core Definitions
1. 防水的
   - en: not allowing water to pass through
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

2. 使防水
   - en: to treat something so that water cannot pass through it
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

3. 防水材料或衣物
   - en: waterproof material or a waterproof garment
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B

### Prototype

waterproof = 经得住水的检验，因此能阻止水穿透

### Morphology

- prefix: 
- root: water + proof
- suffix: 
- family: waterproofed, waterproofing

### Etymology Narrative
 1725年有载，由 water＋proof 构成。此处 proof 由“检验、证据”转为“能够抵挡、抵御”，故这个复合词义为能阻住水的通过。 

### Mnemonic

- type: root
- text: water + proof：经得住水的检验，就能抵抗水穿透，也就是 waterproof。

### Semantic Chain
证验 -> 能抵挡 -> 拒水 -> 防水的

- Keep.
- water 是复合首成分而非 prefix；proof 的桥梁是 test/evidence -> resistant。
