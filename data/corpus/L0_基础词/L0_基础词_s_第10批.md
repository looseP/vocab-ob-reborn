## surge

### Identity

- lemma: surge
- title: surge
- pos: v/n
- ipa: /sɜːrdʒ/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: surges, surged, surging

### Short Definition

汹涌；急剧上升；猛增

### Core Definitions
1. （水）汹涌；起伏翻腾
   - en: to rise and move in a powerful forward or upward motion
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

2. （数量/情绪）急剧上升；猛涨
   - en: to increase suddenly and powerfully
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype

surge = 从下面往上猛冲

### Morphology

- prefix: sub- (proposed/probable historical analysis: up from below)
- root: reg (proposed/probable L *regere* "to guide, rule")
- suffix:
- family:

### Etymology Narrative
 来源不明，大概是经法语 sourge- 出自拉丁语 surgere／surrigere（升起、起立）。有一种历史分析把 surrigere 解作同化了的 sub-“自下而上”＋regere（引导、统治），但这一拆分并不确定。 

### Mnemonic

- type: root
- text: 可按 proposed/probable 分析联想 sub-（从下往上）+ reg（引导）→ 往上猛冲；这是助记，不把不确定拆分当定论。

### Semantic Chain
自下涌起 -> 翻滚而上 -> 排空而来 -> 汹涌

### Reviewer Notes

- Keep.
- 来源不确定，probably French *sourge-* < L *surgere/surrigere*；sub- + reg 的历史拆分为 proposed/probable，保留 prefix 标注但不作定论。与 surgery（Gk 手工作）互不为 family。

- confidence: uncertain
