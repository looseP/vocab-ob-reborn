## stagger

- title: stagger
- pos: v

- cefr: B1
- aliases: staggers, staggered, staggering

### Short Definition

蹒跚；摇晃地走；使震惊

### Core Definitions
1. 蹒跚；摇摇晃晃地走
   - en: to walk unsteadily, reeling from side to side
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

2. 使震惊；使错愕
   - en: to shock, bewilder, or amaze
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype

stagger = 走不稳、左右晃

### Morphology

- prefix:
- root: stagger
- suffix:
- family:

### Etymology Narrative
 出自斯堪的纳维亚语来源（比较古 Norse 语 stakra“推、使蹒跚”）；15世纪中叶，由更早的 stakeren（走得不稳）改形而来。画面是左右摇晃着前行。 

### Mnemonic

- type: image
- text: st-ag-ger 像醉汉两边晃 → 一手扶墙一手拎鞋，走到第三步就打了个趔趄；消息传来，他站着愣了半分钟

### Semantic Chain
摇晃 -> 不稳而行 -> 蹒跚 -> 蹒跚

### Reviewer Notes

- Keep.
- confidence: source-backed
