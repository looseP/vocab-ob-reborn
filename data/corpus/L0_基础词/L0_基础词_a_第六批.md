## attendant

### Identity

- lemma: attendant
- title: attendant
- pos: n / adj
- ipa: /əˈtendənt/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: attendants

### Short Definition

服务员；随从；伴随的

### Core Definitions
1. 服务员；随从
   - en: a person who attends to or waits upon others
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

2. 伴随的；随之而来的
   - en: accompanying, related
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype

attendant = 把心思伸向（照顾）的人

### Morphology


- prefix: ad- (to)
- root: tend (Latin *tendere* "to stretch", PIE *ten- "to stretch")
- suffix: -ant (noun / adjective)
- family: attend, tend, extend, intend, attention

### Etymology Narrative

14世纪晚期作形容词义为“留意的”，源自古法语 atendant，即 atendre（留心）的现在分词，出自拉丁语 attendere“朝……伸展”＝ad-“向”＋tendere“伸”。15世纪初起作名词指“侍从、服务员”。画面是把心思朝某人伸过去、随时照应。
### Mnemonic

- type: root
- text: ad-（to）+ tend 伸（同 attend/extend/intend，PIE *ten-）→ 病房门口的护工随时探身进来添水

### Semantic Chain
倾身相向 -> 悉心照应 -> 随侍在侧 -> 服务员

### Reviewer Notes



- *tendere* "stretch" (PIE *ten-), same root as *attend / extend / intend*.
- Keep.
- confidence: source-backed
