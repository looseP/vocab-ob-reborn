   - priority: 2

## bypass

### Identity

- lemma: bypass
- title: bypass
- pos: n/v
- ipa: /ˈbaɪpæs/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: bypasses, bypassed, bypassing

### Short Definition

绕过；旁路；避开

### Core Definitions
1. 绕过；避开
   - en: to go around something instead of through it
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

2. 旁路；支路
   - en: a road or route around a place
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
bypass = 主路堵死了，司机一打方向盘拐进了村道

### Morphology

- prefix:
- root: by + pass
- suffix:
- family: bypassing, bypassed

### Etymology Narrative
 日耳曼语本族复合词，由 by（在旁、绕开）＋pass 构成。本义（1830年代）是绕城而过的辅路，20世纪后引申为“绕开、避开（问题）”。 

### Mnemonic

- type: image
- text: by（在旁边）+ pass（通过）→ 高架堵死，导航把车引到城外那条辅路

### Semantic Chain
闪到一旁 -> 绕道而行 -> 避而不走 -> 绕过

### Reviewer Notes

- Keep.
- Germanic 复合词，按 by + pass 拆，不伪拆拉丁词根。
- confidence: source-backed
