## bloody

- lemma: bloody
- title: bloody
- pos: adj
- ipa: /ˈblʌdi/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: bloodier, bloodiest

### Short Definition

血腥的；流血的；带血的

### Core Definitions
1. 血腥的；残忍的
   - en: covered with blood, or involving violence and killing
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

2. （英式口语，加强语气）该死的
   - en: used as a strong intensifier in British informal speech
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype

bloody = 沾了血的

### Morphology

- prefix:
- root: blood
- suffix: -y (-ig)
- family: blood, bloodily, bloody-minded

### Etymology Narrative
 源自古英语 blōdig（沾血的），由 blōd（血）加形容词后缀 -ig 构成。日耳曼语固有词。画面是被血沾染、浸透的东西。 

### Mnemonic

- type: root
- text: blood（血）+ -y（…的）→ 拳击手退场，毛巾按在眉骨上已经洇红

### Semantic Chain
沾了血 -> 血迹斑斑 -> 杀气腾腾 -> 血腥的

### Reviewer Notes

- Keep.
