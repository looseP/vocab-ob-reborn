## shame

### Identity

- lemma: shame
- title: shame
- pos: n/v
- ipa: /ʃeɪm/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: shames, shamed, shaming, ashamed

### Short Definition

羞耻；羞愧；使感到羞耻

### Core Definitions
1. 羞耻；羞愧
   - en: the painful feeling of having lost esteem or having done something wrong
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

2. 使感到羞耻；使蒙羞
   - en: to cause someone to feel ashamed
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype

shame = 丢了脸面、心中刺痛

### Morphology

- prefix:
- root: shame
- suffix:
- family:

### Etymology Narrative
 出自古英语 scamu（羞耻、耻辱），源自原始日耳曼语 *skamo。本条有用的意象是：失去了他人的尊重或声誉时，那种痛切的感受。 

### Mnemonic

- type: image
- text: 羞耻时人会**捂脸** → 被点名那一下，他恨不得找个地缝钻进去

### Semantic Chain
蒙羞 -> 痛楚之感 -> 自尊失落 -> 羞耻

### Reviewer Notes

- Keep.
- Germanic 原生词，无词根可拆，用 image 锚点。PGmc *skamo 更远处源 uncertain，但 OE 形已确证。
- confidence: source-backed
