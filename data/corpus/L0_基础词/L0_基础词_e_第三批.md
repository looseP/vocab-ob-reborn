## evade

### Identity

- lemma: evade
- title: evade
- pos: v
- ipa: /ɪˈveɪd/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: evades, evaded, evading

### Short Definition

逃避；回避；躲避

### Core Definitions
1. 逃避；规避
   - en: to escape or avoid, especially by cunning
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

2. 回避
   - en: to avoid dealing with something
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype

evade = 走开躲掉

### Morphology

- prefix: ex- (out)
- root: vad (go)
- suffix:
- family: evaded, evasion

### Etymology Narrative
 源自拉丁语 evadere（逃脱），由同化后的 ex-（离开）＋vadere（走、行）构成。画面是走开以避开。 

### Mnemonic

- type: root
- text: ex- = 出/离开 + vad = 走（invade 入侵也是 vad「走」）→ 记者递话筒，他侧身从侧门溜走

### Semantic Chain
抽身退去 -> 避而不接 -> 脱身溜走 -> 逃避
