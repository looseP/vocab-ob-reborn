## denounce

### Identity

- lemma: denounce
- title: denounce
- pos: v
- ipa: /dɪˈnaʊns/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: denounces, denounced, denouncing

### Short Definition

公开谴责；告发；指责

### Core Definitions
1. 公开谴责；指责
   - en: to publicly declare something or someone to be wrong or evil
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

2. 告发；检举
   - en: to formally accuse or inform against someone
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype

denounce = 大声宣告其罪

### Morphology


- prefix: de- (down, from)
- root: nunt (announce, from L nuntiare "proclaim", nuntius "messenger")
- suffix:
- family: denunciation, announce, pronounce, renounce

### Etymology Narrative
 源自拉丁语 denuntiare（宣告、公布；告发），由 de-（向下、从）＋nuntiare（宣告，出自 nuntius“信使”）构成。画面是正式地大声宣告，后转为公开的指责与告发。 

### Mnemonic

- type: root
- text: de（宣告/彻底）+ nunt（宣告，同源 announce 宣布 / pronounce 发音）→ 广场布告栏前，点名那人的罪状人人可读

### Semantic Chain
出言宣示 -> 当众指摘 -> 斥其不是 -> 公开谴责

### Reviewer Notes
