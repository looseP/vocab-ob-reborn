## apple

### Identity

- lemma: apple
- title: apple
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈæpl/
- cefr: A1
- aliases: apples

### Short Definition

苹果

### Core Definitions
1. 苹果
   - en: a round fruit with red or green skin
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

### Prototype

apple = 最常见的果子

### Morphology


- prefix:
- root: (Middle English *appel*, Old English *æppel*; PGmc *ap(a)laz, from PIE *ab(e)l- "apple" — connection to other IE forms uncertain)
- suffix:
- family: apple, apple-tree

### Etymology Narrative

中古英语 appel、古英语 æppel 义为“苹果；任何一种果子”，源自原始日耳曼语 *ap(a)laz，再溯至原始印欧语 *ab(e)l-（苹果；另见高卢语 avallo、古爱尔兰语 ubull）。各形式之间的确切关系尚不清楚。语义演变：泛指一切果实（中古英语仍如此）→专指那种熟悉的圆果苹果。
### Mnemonic

- type: image
- text: apple 从古就是"果子"统称，到现代专指苹果。Germanic 原生，PIE *ab(e)l-，无需与 Latin malum 强联。

### Semantic Chain
一种果实 -> 甘美之果 -> 苹果

### Reviewer Notes



- Germanic native; PIE *ab(e)l- "apple" but exact connection to Latin *malum* / Greek *melon* is disputed — do NOT force a Latin link.
- Keep.

- Confidence detail: source-backed (PIE *ab(e)l-; link to Latin *malum*/Greek *melon* disputed)

   - en: an act of setting a value or price on something
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

2. 评价（文学等）
   - en: a critical assessment
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype

- text: a-(to) + pret 价（同 price/praise/appreciate）→ 鉴定师戴着白手套，围着古董转了三圈

### Semantic Chain
定出价码 -> 估量价值 -> 估价

### Reviewer Notes

- Keep.
