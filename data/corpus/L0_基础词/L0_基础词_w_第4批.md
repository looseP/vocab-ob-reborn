## withhold

### Identity

- lemma: withhold
- title: withhold
- pos: v
- ipa: /wɪðˈhəʊld/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: withholds, withheld, withholding

### Short Definition

扣留；拒给；抑制

### Core Definitions
1. 扣留；拒不给予
   - en: to refuse to give something or keep it back
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 抑制；忍住
   - en: to hold back or restrain (an emotion, reaction)
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype

withhold = 把东西“往回/对抗地”握住不放

### Morphology

- prefix:
- root: with + hold
- suffix:
- family: hold, withdraw, withstand

### Etymology Narrative
 由 with＋hold 复合而成，其中 with 保留着古英语较古的“相对、反向”之义（源出 wið“对着、相反”），而非现代的“与某物一起”。画面是把某物按住不给，或推得远远的。 

### Mnemonic

- type: contrast
- text: 这里的 with 是古义“对抗/往回”（如 withdraw、withstand）：往回握住不给，就是 withhold。

### Semantic Chain
持与回 -> 留存不付 -> 拒给 -> 扣留

### Reviewer Notes

- Keep.
- with 取古义“against/back”，与 withdraw/withstand 同类；两成分归 root。
- confidence: source-backed
