## disturbance

### Identity

- lemma: disturbance
- title: disturbance
- pos: n
- ipa: /dɪˈstɜːrbəns/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: disturbances

### Short Definition

扰乱；骚乱；不安

### Core Definitions
1. 扰乱；骚乱
   - en: an interruption of calm or order, a public disturbance
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

2. 心神不安；困扰
   - en: a feeling of worry or mental distress
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype

disturbance = 被搅乱、不在原位

### Morphology


- prefix: dis- (apart)
- root: turb (stir, disorder)
- suffix: -ance
- family: disturb, turbid, trouble

### Etymology Narrative
 源自古法语 destorbance，出自拉丁语 disturbare（搅乱），由 dis-（散开）＋turbare（使混乱，出自 turba“骚乱”）构成。画面是事物被从原本安定的秩序中抛散开来。 

### Mnemonic

- type: root
- text: dis-（分开/彻底）+ turb（搅动，如 turbid 浑浊的）→ 深夜一声玻璃碎响，整栋楼亮起灯

### Semantic Chain
搅得离散 -> 秩序大乱 -> 人心浮动 -> 骚乱
