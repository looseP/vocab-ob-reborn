### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline absorb：Late 14c., from Latin absorbere 'to swallow up, devour,' from ab- 'away, off' + sorbere 'suck in,' from PIE *srebh- 'to suck, absorb.'

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline catalyst：1900, formed in English (on analogy of analyst) from catalysis. Catalysis 1650s, 'dissolution,' from Latinized Greek katalysis, from katalyein 'to dissolve' (kata 'down' + lyein 'to loosen,' PIE *leu-). Chemical sense from 1836.
