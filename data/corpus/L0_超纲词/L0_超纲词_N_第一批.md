   - priority: 3

### Prototype

### Morphology
- prefix:

### Semantic Chain

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline narrowly：Old English nearolice "with little breadth or extent, closely; strictly; carefully;" see narrow (adj.) + -ly (2). Meaning "only by a little, by a small distance" attested from 1550s. 透明派生，采用基础词 narrow 源页；root=narrow，suffix=-ly，无 prefix。

### Short Definition
神经的；神经系统的

### Core Definitions
1. 与神经或神经系统有关的
   - en: pertaining to a nerve or nerves; to the nervous system generally
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

### Prototype
neural = 指尖一碰热锅，信号瞬间传回了手臂

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: neuron (Greek "nerve, sinew")
- suffix: -al
- family: neuron, neurology, neuro-

### Etymology Narrative
neural 1830 年意为“与神经或神经系统有关的”，由希腊语 neuron“神经”加形容词后缀 -al 构成；可比较 nervous。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: neuron（神经，希腊语）+ -al（…的）= neural：神经的。

### Semantic Chain
筋脉 -> 周身传信的线 -> 与这些线相关 -> 神经的

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline neural (adj.) "pertaining to a nerve or nerves, pertaining to the nervous system generally," 1830, from Greek neuron "nerve" (see neuro-) + adjectival suffix -al (1). 透明派生 neural=neuron + -al，采用基础词 neuron / neuro- 源页；root=neuron，suffix=-al，无 prefix。

## neurology

### Identity
- lemma: neurology
- title: neurology
- pos: n
- ipa: /njʊəˈrɒlədʒi/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: neurologia, neurological

### Short Definition
神经学；神经系统研究

### Core Definitions
1. 研究神经系统形态与功能的科学
   - en: scientific study of the form and function of the nervous system
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

### Prototype
neurology = 门诊里他让病人闭眼，用小锤敲了敲膝盖下面

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: neuro (Greek "nerve", combining form of neuron)
- suffix: -logy (study)
- family: neurology, neurological, neuron

### Etymology Narrative
neurology 1680年代 意为“神经系统形态与功能的科学研究”，源自现代拉丁语 neurologia，来自现代希腊语 neurologia（1660年代），由 neuro-“神经”加 -logia“研究、学问”构成。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: neuro-（神经，来自 neuron）+ -logy（研究，如 biology）= neurology：神经学。

### Semantic Chain
筋脉 -> 传信的线 -> 专研这套的学问 -> 神经学

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline neurology (n.) "scientific study of the form and function of the nervous system," 1680s, from Modern Latin neurologia, from Modern Greek neurologia (1660s), from neuro- "nerves, the nervous system" (see neuro-) + -logia "study" (see -logy). 透明派生 neurology=neuro- + -logy，采用基础词 neuron / neuro- 源页；root=neuro，suffix=-logy，无 prefix。
