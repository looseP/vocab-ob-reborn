## wade

### Identity
- lemma: wade
- title: wade
- pos: v
- ipa: /weɪd/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: waded, wading

### Short Definition
涉水；费力地走/通过

### Core Definitions
1. 涉（水）；跋涉
   - en: walk into or through water or any impeding substance
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. （比喻）费力对付、艰难推进
   - en: move with difficulty and labor (battle, reading)
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
wade = 在水或阻碍物中迈步前行。

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: wadan / wadanan (OE "go forward"; PIE *wadh- "to go")
- suffix:
- family: waded, wading

### Etymology Narrative
 wade 源自中古英语 waden，上承古英语 wadan“前行、迈步”，来自原始日耳曼语 *wadanan（PIE *wadh-"去、行"，亦见拉丁语 vadere）。原为强动词，16 世纪后弱化为规则动词。w- 非前缀。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: wade 即“wading through water”，古英语 wadan“前行”，与 go 同族；w- 不是前缀。

### Semantic Chain
行走 -> 在水里走 -> 蹚水而过 -> 涉水、艰难跋涉

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline wade：Middle English waden, "go, advance ... walk into or through water," from Old English wadan, from Proto-Germanic *wadanan, from PIE *wadh- "to go"。w- 非前缀，不拆 prefix。
- confidence: source-backed
