## patronize

### Identity
- lemma: patronize
- title: patronize
- pos: v
- ipa: /ˈpætrənaɪz/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: patron, patronage, patronizing

### Short Definition
赞助；惠顾；以恩赐态度对待

### Core Definitions
1. 赞助；光顾
   - en: to give support or be a customer of
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 以居高临下的态度对待
   - en: to treat someone condescendingly
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
patronize = 他把钱往桌上一放，说这店他包了；说话时下巴一直是抬着的

### Morphology
- prefix: 
- root: patron (from Latin patronus 'protector')
- suffix: -ize
- family: patron, patronage, patronizing

### Etymology Narrative
 由 patron（庇护者、赞助人）加动词后缀 -ize 构成，指赞助、惠顾，也引申为“以恩赐态度对待”。patron 源自拉丁语 patronus（保护者），由 pater（父亲）构成。画面是位高者如父辈般庇护、提携弱者，故含“光顾、施恩”之意。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: patron（赞助人）+ -ize（使…）；patronize 即“赞助 / 光顾”，贬义为“以恩赐态度对待”。

### Semantic Chain
庇护的人 -> 照拂底下的人 -> 出钱捧场 -> 赞助、光顾

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline patronize：(n.) c. 1300, patroun , "a lord-master, one who protects, supports, or encourages,"
- confidence: source-backed
