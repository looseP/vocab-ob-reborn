## facial

### Identity
- lemma: facial
- title: facial
- pos: adj/n
- ipa: /ˈfeɪʃl/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: face

### Short Definition
面部的；脸的；面部美容护理

### Core Definitions
1. 面部的；与脸有关的
   - en: relating to the face
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. （名词）面部美容护理
   - en: a beauty treatment for the face
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
facial = 热毛巾敷上脸，蒸汽里一股草药味

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: faci (face, from Latin facies)
- suffix: -al
- family: face, facially

### Etymology Narrative
 经法语 facial 进入英语，源自中世纪拉丁语 facialis（面部的），来自拉丁语 facies（脸）；-al 为形容词后缀。名词「面部美容」义1914年起见于美式英语。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: faci 直指 face（脸），-al 是形容词后缀，facial 就是“面部的”。

### Semantic Chain
脸 -> 相貌 -> 长在脸上的 -> 面部的、与脸有关的

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline facial：c. 1600, "face to face," from French facial, from Medieval Latin facialis "of the face," from facies (see face (n.)); 英语 "pertaining to the face" 义 1786 年，"beauty treatment" 名词义 1914 年美式英语。-al 为形容词后缀，整体借词链经法语，不拆 prefix。
- confidence: source-backed

## factoid

### Identity
- lemma: factoid
- title: factoid
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈfæktɔɪd/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: fact, factual

### Short Definition
看似事实的虚构信息；小事实

### Core Definitions
1. 看似事实的虚构信息
   - en: a published statement taken to be a fact because of its appearance in print
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 零散的小事实
   - en: a small, isolated bit of true factual information
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
factoid = 传得人人都会说，一查却找不到出处

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: fact (thing done, from Latin factum)
- suffix: -oid (like, resembling)
- family: fact, factual, factually

### Etymology Narrative
factoid 由 Norman Mailer 于 1973 年提出，由 fact 加后缀 -oid“似…的”构成，原指“因印成文字而被当成事实的陈述”；到 1988 年也指“零散的真实小事实”。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: fact（事实）+ -oid（像…的）= factoid：像事实一样的“事实”。

### Semantic Chain
确凿的事 -> 看着像那么回事 -> 其实并无出处 -> 看似事实的虚构信息

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline factoid：1973, "published statement taken to be a fact because of its appearance in print," from fact + -oid, first explained, if not coined, by Norman Mailer. 注意 Mailer 是“阐释/或创造”者，叙事不夸大其词源确定性。
- confidence: source-backed
