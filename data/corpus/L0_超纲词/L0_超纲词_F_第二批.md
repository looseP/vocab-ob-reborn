   - en: a state of excited agitation

### Morphology

## folklore

### Identity
- lemma: folklore
- title: folklore
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈfəʊklɔː/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: folk, lore

### Short Definition
民间传说；民俗

### Core Definitions
1. 民间传说；传统习俗
   - en: the traditional beliefs, customs, and stories of ordinary people
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

### Prototype
folklore = 村口老树下那段故事，爷爷讲给孙子，一句没改

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: folk + lore
- suffix:
- family: folk, lore, folklorist

### Etymology Narrative
folklore 由古物学家 William J. Thoms 于 1846 年创造，仿德语 Volk- 复合词，由 folk“民众”与 lore“知识、传说”组合，用以取代 popular antiquities；该词复兴了 folk 的现代“民间”义。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: folk（民间）+ lore（传说/学问）= folklore：民间代代相传的传说。

### Semantic Chain
民众 -> 口耳相传的旧学问 -> 民间传说与传统习俗

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline folklore：1846, coined by antiquarian William J. Thoms as an Anglo-Saxonism (replacing popular antiquities) in imitation of German compounds in Volk-; see folk + lore. Thoms 是创造者（coined），叙事如实写 coined，不改成 popularized。
- confidence: source-backed
