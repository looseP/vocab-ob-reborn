## granite

### Identity
- lemma: granite
- title: granite
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈɡrænɪt/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: grain, granulate

### Short Definition
花岗岩；坚如磐石

### Core Definitions
1. 花岗岩
   - en: a coarse-grained igneous rock
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. （比喻）坚硬、顽固
   - en: figuratively, something hard or unyielding
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
granite = 因“颗粒/纹理”得名。

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: granite (from Italian granito "grained" < grano "grain" < Latin granum "grain")
- suffix:
- family: grain, granulate, granular

### Etymology Narrative
 1640年代来自法语 granit(e)，或直接源自意大利语 granito（花岗岩），本义「有颗粒纹理的」，是 granire（使成粒状）的过去分词形容词，源自 grano（颗粒），出自拉丁语 granum（谷粒），原始印欧语 *gre-no-（谷粒）。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: granite 与 grain（颗粒）同源——花岗岩肉眼可见的“颗粒感”就是它名字的由来。

### Semantic Chain
一颗谷粒 -> 粒粒紧挨 -> 结成硬石 -> 花岗岩

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline granite (n.) 1640s, from French granit(e) or directly from Italian granito "granite," originally "grained," past-participle adjective from granire "granulate, make grainy," from grano "grain," from Latin granum "grain" (PIE *gre-no-). 整体借词，不拆前缀。
- confidence: source-backed
