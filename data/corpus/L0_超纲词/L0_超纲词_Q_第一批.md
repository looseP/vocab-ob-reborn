## questionable

### Identity
- lemma: questionable
- title: questionable
- pos: adj
- ipa: /ˈkwestʃənəbl/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: question

### Short Definition
可疑的；有疑问的；值得怀疑的

### Core Definitions
1. 可疑的；有问题的、值得怀疑的
   - en: doubtful; open to dispute, suspicion, or challenge
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 品格可疑的；名声有问题的
   - en: of dubious character or reputation
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
questionable = 单据上的日期改过，谁也说不清是几号

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: question (from Latin quaestio "a seeking, inquiry," from quaerere "to ask, seek")
- suffix: -able
- family: question, questionably

### Etymology Narrative
questionable 于 1580 年代意为“可被审问的”（现义已废），约 1600 年起用于事实、主张等“可争议、可疑的”，由 question（动词）加形容词后缀 -able 构成；其贬义“品格可疑”自 1806 年有记录。question 源自拉丁语 quaestio“追问、查问”，来自动词 quaerere“询问、寻求”。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: question（问题 / 质疑）+ -able（可…的）= questionable：可被质疑的、可疑的。

### Semantic Chain
追问 -> 查究 -> 提出疑议 -> 说不大通 -> 可疑的、有问题的

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline questionable (adj.) 1580s, "that may be interrogated" (obsolete sense); c. 1600, of facts/claims, "open to dispute, doubtful," from question (v.) + -able. Deprecatory "dubious in character" attested 1806. base question：early 13c. from Anglo-French questiun / Old French question < Latin quaestionem (nominative quaestio) "a seeking, questioning, inquiry," noun of action from quaerere "ask, seek". q- 非前缀；root=question，suffix=-able，无字符重叠。
- confidence: source-backed

## quick-witted

### Identity
- lemma: quick-witted
- title: quick-witted
- pos: adj
- ipa: /kwɪk ˈwɪtɪd/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: quick, wit, witty

### Short Definition
机敏的；反应敏捷的；聪慧的

### Core Definitions
1. 机敏的；反应快的、思维敏捷的
   - en: having a nimble and ready intellect; mentally sharp and quick to respond
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

### Prototype
quick-witted = 话音刚落，他已经接了下一句，还接得恰到好处

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: quick + wit (free-morpheme compound)
- suffix: -ed
- family: quick, wit, witty

### Etymology Narrative
 透明英语复合派生词，由 quick（敏捷的、机敏的）与 wit（才智、心智）加形容词后缀 -ed 构成，字面即「才智敏捷的、反应机灵的」。quick 出自古英语 cwic（活的、有生命的），约1300年起引申为「活泼、迅速」，15世纪末用于人「思维敏捷、反应快」；wit 出自古英语 wit（理解力、理智），出自原始日耳曼语 *wit-，源自原始印欧语词根 *weid-（看、认知）。Etymonline 无独立词条（直达页仅有导航文本），故此处依据 quick 与 wit 的词源。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: quick（敏捷的）+ wit（机智）+ -ed = quick-witted：思维敏捷、反应机灵的。

### Semantic Chain
灵动 -> 脑筋转得快 -> 心思来得快 -> 机敏的、思维敏捷

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline quick-witted 直达页为占位页（仅返回导航文本，无 Origin and history of 正文），按透明派生词以基础词源页补证。quick (adj.) Middle English quik, from Old English cwic "living, alive" < Proto-Germanic *kwikwaz < PIE *gwei- "to live"; sense "lively, active, swift, speedy" by c. 1300; of persons "mentally active, prompt to perceive or respond" from late 15c. wit (n.) Old English wit "understanding, intellect" < Proto-Germanic *wit- < PIE *weid- "to see, to know". 复合为自由语素 quick + wit，-ed 为形容词后缀，root 整体入 quick+wit，不拆 prefix；标注 transparent derivative。
