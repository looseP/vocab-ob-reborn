## biophilia

### Identity
- lemma: biophilia
- title: biophilia
- pos: n
- ipa: /baɪəʊˈfɪliə/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: biophilic

### Short Definition
亲生命性；对生命与自然的喜爱

### Core Definitions
1. 亲生命性
   - en: a tendency to value or feel drawn to living things and nature
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 对自然生命的喜爱
   - en: love or affinity for living organisms and natural life
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
biophilia = bio（生命）与 -philia（喜爱）合在一起。

### Morphology
- prefix: bio-（Greek bios, life；combining form）
- root: philia（Greek love, affection）
- suffix: 
- family: biophilic

### Etymology Narrative

英语内部复合而成，由 bio-（生命）与 -philia（对…的亲近、爱好）构成。画面是对活的东西天生感到亲切，故指人类亲近自然的天性；此现代义项由弗洛姆提出、威尔逊推广，并被词典收录。
### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: bio = life，philia = love：对生命有亲近和喜爱。

### Semantic Chain
生命 -> 对生命的喜爱 -> 亲近活物的天性 -> 亲生命性

### Reviewer Notes
- OED source: https://www.oed.com/dictionary/biophilia_n；Merriam-Webster source: https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/biophilias
- OED confirms English compounding; Merriam-Webster confirms component meanings and modern sense.
- confidence: source-backed

### Reviewer Notes
- 与 biomedical 的现代 bio- + medical 区分；本条来自 Greek biotikos，按 biot- + -ic 识别。

   - en: something that brings benefit or relief
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
blessing = 老人把手按在孩子头顶，念了一句吉祥话

### Morphology
- prefix:

### Mnemonic
- type: story

### Semantic Chain
祝圣护佑 -> 说好话祈福 -> 祈福这件事 -> 上天给的恩典 -> 祝福
