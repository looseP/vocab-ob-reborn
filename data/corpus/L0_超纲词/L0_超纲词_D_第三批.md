## dissatisfaction

### Identity
- lemma: dissatisfaction
- title: dissatisfaction
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˌdɪsˌsætɪsˈfækʃən/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: satisfy, satisfaction, dissatisfied, dissatisfy

### Short Definition
不满；不满意

### Core Definitions
1. 不满；不满意
   - en: a feeling that something is not good enough or does not meet expectations
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

### Prototype
dissatisfaction = 原本应满足的期待没有被填满。

### Morphology
- prefix: dis- (not, opposite)
- root: satis (enough) + fac (make)
- suffix: -tion
- family: satisfy, satisfaction, dissatisfied, dissatisfaction

### Etymology Narrative
透明派生链 dis- + satisfaction；satisfaction 源自拉丁 satis“足够”和 facere“做、使”，dis- 将满足状态反转为不满足。 

### Mnemonic
- type: contrast
- text: satisfaction 是“够满足”，dis-satisfaction 就是满足感被撤回：不满。

### Semantic Chain
够了 -> 觉得可以 -> 本该满意 -> 偏不如意 -> 不满
