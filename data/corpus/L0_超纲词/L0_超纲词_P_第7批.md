## puritan

### Identity
- lemma: puritan
- title: puritan
- pos: n/adj
- ipa: /ˈpjʊərɪtən/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: puritanical, pure

### Short Definition
清教徒；禁欲拘谨者

### Core Definitions
1. （史）清教徒
   - en: a member of the strict Protestant group
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 形容词：禁欲拘谨的
   - en: strictly moral or prudish
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
puritan = 酒不许喝，牌不许打，连衣裳的颜色都得是灰的

### Morphology
- prefix: 
- root: purit (from Latin puritas 'purity' < purus 'pure')
- suffix: -an
- family: pure, puritan, puritanical

### Etymology Narrative
 1560年代始见，指16世纪英格兰兴起的一派新教徒，起初泛指反对国教等级制者，后带贬义指国教会内要求进一步改革的人。与 pure 同源，画面是追求信仰上的纯净无杂，后引申为禁欲拘谨者。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: purit = 纯净（puritas，purus“纯”），-an（…者）；puritan 即“追求纯净者”——清教徒。

### Semantic Chain
洁净 -> 一尘不染的信仰 -> 事事都要干净的人 -> 清教徒

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline puritan：Origin and history of Puritan Puritan (n.) 1560s, in reference to a class of Protestants that arose in 16th century England, originally generally, "opponent of Anglican hierarchy," later applied opprobriously to "person in the Church of England who seeks further reformation"
- confidence: source-backed


## purport

### Identity
- lemma: purport
- title: purport
- pos: n/v
- ipa: /pəˈpɔːt/
- cefr: C2
- aliases: purported

### Short Definition
主旨；声称；大意

### Core Definitions
1. 主旨；大意
   - en: the intended meaning or tenor
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 声称；伪装
   - en: to claim or profess to be
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
purport = 讲了半天，听下来就是这事他不干

### Morphology
- prefix: 
- root: purport (from Anglo-French purport, Old French porport 'contents' < por- 'forth' + port 'carry')
- suffix: 
- family: purport, purport

### Etymology Narrative
 15世纪初的名词，义为含义、要旨、文件的表面意思。源自英法语 purport（13世纪末）与古法语 porport（内容、宗旨）。画面是某段话所承载、所传达出来的东西，故指主旨、大意与声称的意图。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: purport 来自 porport（por-“向前”+ port“携带 / 表达”），指“所表达的内容 / 主旨”；动词作“声称”。

### Semantic Chain
带着走 -> 一路承载 -> 话里要说的 -> 主旨、大意

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline purport：(n.) early 15c., "meaning, tenor, the surface or expressed meaning of a document, etc.; that which is conveyed or expressed," from Anglo-French purport (late 13c.), Old French porport "contents, tenor,"
- confidence: source-backed


## pushback

### Identity
- lemma: pushback
- title: pushback
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈpʊʃbæk/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: push, back

### Short Definition
抵制；反弹；推回

### Core Definitions
1. 抵制；反对
   - en: resistance or opposition to a policy
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 推回；反弹
   - en: the act of pushing back
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
pushback = 通知一贴出来，第二天就有人把牌子砸了

### Morphology
- prefix: 
- root: push + back
- suffix: 
- family: push, back

### Etymology Narrative
 复合词 push（推）＋ back（回、后）。push 约1300年指“推、猛力顶撞以推进”，源自古法语 pousser；back 源自古英语 bæc，出自原始日耳曼语 *bakam，词源仅限日耳曼语系。画面是用手把对方或物件朝原方向顶回去，故指抵制、反弹，亦指（飞机）返航、推迟。 

### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: push（推）+ back（回）——被推回来就是 pushback，引申为对政策的“抵制 / 反弹”。

### Semantic Chain
推 -> 反推回去 -> 顶了回去 -> 抵制、反对

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline pushback：(v.) c. 1300, pushen , "to shove, move onward, strike with a thrusting motion, thrust forcibly against for the purpose of impelling,"
- confidence: source-backed
