- title: inferiority

- cefr: C2
- aliases: inferior

### Short Definition
劣势；低劣；下级

### Core Definitions
1. 低劣；劣势
   - en: state of being inferior
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 自卑感
   - en: inferiority complex
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
inferiority = 同样的活他做得又快又好，自己怎么赶都差一截

### Morphology
- prefix: 
- root: inferior (from Latin inferior)
- suffix: -ity
- family: inferior, inferiority

### Etymology Narrative

### Semantic Chain
