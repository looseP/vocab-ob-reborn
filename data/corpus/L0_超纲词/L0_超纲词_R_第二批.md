## relic

### Identity
- lemma: relic
- title: relic
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈrelɪk/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: relics

### Short Definition
圣物；遗物；遗迹

### Core Definitions
1. 圣物、圣骸
   - en: an object held in reverence due to connection with a holy person
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 遗迹、遗存
   - en: a surviving trace or remnant of the past
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
relic = 与圣人相关、被敬奉的遗存。

### Morphology
- prefix: 
- root: relic (from Old French relique, from Late Latin reliquiæ (plural) "remains of a martyr", in classical Latin "remains, remnants", from reliquus "remaining"; from re- "back" + linquere "to leave", PIE *linkw- "to leave")
- suffix: 
- family: relics, reliquary, relique

### Etymology Narrative
relic 约1200年作 relik“因与圣者关联而被敬奉的遗体或圣物”，源自古法语 relique 及晚期拉丁语 reliquiæ“殉道者遗骸”（古典拉丁语指“遗存”），为 reliquus“剩余的”的阴性复数名词用法，源自 re-（回）+ linquere“留下”（PIE *linkw-“离开”）。14世纪初起泛指“遗存、残迹”，约1600年起指“作为纪念品的遗物”。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: relic 来自拉丁语 reliquus“剩余的”，re-（回）+ linquere（留下）——留下来的就是 relic 圣物/遗迹。

### Semantic Chain
留下 -> 遗留下来 -> 前人的遗存 -> 圣物、圣骸

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline relic (n.) c. 1200, relik, "a body part or other object held in reverence ... due to its connection with a holy person," from Old French relique, from Late Latin reliquiæ (plural) "the remains of a martyr," in classical Latin "remains, remnants," noun use of fem. plural of reliquus "remaining, that which remains." Related to relinquere, from re- "back" + linquere "to leave" (PIE *linkw- "to leave"). r- 非前缀；re- 在此深嵌于 reliquus 内，整体借词，不拆 prefix。
- confidence: source-backed

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: re-（反复）+ nomin（命名，nominare）→ renown（声名）→ 巷子深处的那家店，外地人都专程找来

### Semantic Chain
名字 -> 传扬出去 -> 人人皆知 -> 著名的、声誉卓著的

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline renowned (adj.) "celebrated, famous," late 14c., past-participle adjective from renounen "make (a name) famous ...", c. 1400, from renown (n.). renown (n.) c. 1300, "fame or glory ...", from Anglo-French renoun, Old French renon, from renomer "make famous," from re- "repeatedly" + nomer "to name," from Latin nominare "to name". 注意 renowned 与 renege 词源无关（renegare vs renominare）；re- 真实拉丁前缀。
