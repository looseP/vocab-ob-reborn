   - tags: A

### Prototype

### Semantic Chain

- Etymonline meteorological：1560s, from French météorologique or Latinized Greek meteōrologikos, from meteōrologia (see meteorology). -al 为形容词后缀；transparent derivative，base (meteorology) etymology used；confidence: source-backed。
