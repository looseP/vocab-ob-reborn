## agile

### Identity
- lemma: agile
- title: agile
- pos: adj
- ipa: /ˈædʒaɪl/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: agility

### Short Definition
敏捷的；灵活的；机敏的

### Core Definitions
1. 敏捷的；动作灵活快速的
   - en: able to move quickly and easily
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. （思维）机敏的；反应快的
   - en: able to think and understand quickly
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
agile = 动得快而且灵活

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: ag-/agere (drive, move, do)
- suffix: -ile (adjectival ending; whole Latin borrowing)
- family: act, action, active, agent, agitate, agility

### Etymology Narrative
 源自法语 agile，或直接出自拉丁语 agilis（灵巧的、迅捷的），由 agere（使动起来、保持运动）而来。画面是身体或心思一直被驱着快速而灵活地转动；其词根是拉丁语的行动、运动词根，并非英语的 age。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: ag-/agere = drive or move；一直被推动、快速移动，就是 agile（敏捷的）。

### Semantic Chain
动起来 -> 不停在动 -> 又快又灵 -> 身手或脑子都灵 -> 敏捷的

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline source: https://www.etymonline.com/word/agile
- Do not analyze the initial letters as `a-` plus `gile`, and do not merge agile with age; the source gives Latin *agilis* < *agere* “to set in motion, keep in movement.”
- confidence: source-backed
