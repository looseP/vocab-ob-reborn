## redirect

### Identity
- lemma: redirect
- title: redirect
- pos: v
- ipa: /riːdəˈrekt/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: direct, redirected

### Short Definition
使改道；重新导向

### Core Definitions
1. 使改道；重新指引
   - en: direct again or anew
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. （邮件/流量）转寄、转发
   - en: send in a new direction (mail, traffic)
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
redirect = 再次指定方向。

### Morphology
- prefix: re-
- root: direct (from Latin directus < dirigere < dis- "apart" + regere "guide, keep straight")
- suffix:
- family: direct, direction, redirected

### Etymology Narrative
 1805年（由 redirected 一词暗示）由 re-（再、又）＋ direct 构成。direct 源自拉丁语 directus（dirigere「使直、指引」的过去分词），由 dis-（分开）＋ regere（引导、使直，原始印欧语 *reg-「沿直线移动」）组成。re- 为真实前缀，词根取 direct。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: re-（再）+ direct（指向）→ redirect 重新指向/改道。

### Semantic Chain
引直 -> 引向一处 -> 换个方向引 -> 使改道、重新指引
