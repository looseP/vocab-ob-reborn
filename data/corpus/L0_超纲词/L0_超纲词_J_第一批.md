## jobless

### Identity
- lemma: jobless
- title: jobless
- pos: adj/n
- ipa: /ˈdʒɒbləs/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: job

### Short Definition
失业的；无工作的；（名词）失业者

### Core Definitions
1. 失业的；没有工作的
   - en: out of work; unemployed
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. （名词）失业的人（总称）
   - en: a jobless person or persons (collective)
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
jobless = 早上不再赶公交，口袋里只剩一沓投出去的简历

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: job (from phrase "jobbe of worke," 1550s, "task, piece of work"; ultimate origin uncertain, perhaps variant of gobbe "mass, lump")
- suffix: -less
- family: job, joblessness

### Etymology Narrative
jobless 于 1892 年作为形容词出现，意为“失业的、无工作的”，由 job（名词“一份工作”）加否定后缀 -less 构成；作名词“失业者（总称）”自 1909 年起使用。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: job（工作）+ -less（没有）= jobless：没有工作的、失业的。

### Semantic Chain
一件活计 -> 手上没活 -> 无事可做 -> 失业的

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline jobless：adj. "out of work, unemployed," 1892, from job (n.) + -less. As a noun, "jobless person or persons," from 1909. Related: Joblessness. -less 为真实英语否定后缀；job 自身词源 uncertain（或源自 gobbe“团块”），但不影响 jobless = job + -less 这一透明派生，按来源支持标记 source-backed。

## journalistic

- title: journalistic
- pos: adj
- ipa: /dʒɜːnəˈlɪstɪk/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: journal, journalist, journalism

### Short Definition
新闻业的；新闻工作者的；新闻风格的

### Core Definitions
1. 新闻业的；与新闻媒体有关的
   - en: relating to journalism or journalists
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 新闻报道风格的
   - en: characteristic of journalism or news writing
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
journalistic = 稿子第二天就见了报，配图占了半个版面

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: journal (from Anglo-French jurnal, Old French jornel "a day," from Late Latin diurnalis "daily" < Latin dies "day," PIE *dyeu- "to shine")
- suffix: -istic
- family: journal, journalism, journalist, journalistic

### Etymology Narrative
journalistic 由 journalism（新闻业）加形容词后缀 -istic 构成，意为“与新闻业或新闻写作有关的”；journal 本义“每日的（记录）”，源自拉丁语 dies“日”，经古法语 jornel“一天”进入英语，后引申出“日记、日报、新闻刊物”等义。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: journal（日记/日报）+ -istic（…的）= journalistic：与新闻业相关的。

### Semantic Chain
每日 -> 逐日记下 -> 记事的册子 -> 以报道为业 -> 新闻业的

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline journalistic 直达页为占位（仅返回站点默认描述），故采用基础词源页补证：journal (n.) mid-14c., from Anglo-French jurnal, Old French jornel "a day," from Late Latin diurnalis "daily," from Latin dies "day." journalistic = journalism + -istic（经由 journalist）。-istic 为形容词后缀（journalist + -ic 的合并识别层）；基础词 journal 经拉丁语 dies 可核验，confidence: source-backed。

## judiciary

### Identity
- lemma: judiciary
- title: judiciary
- pos: adj/n
- ipa: /dʒuˈdɪʃəri/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: judge, judicial, judicature

### Short Definition
司法的；法院的；（名词）司法部；法官团体

### Core Definitions
1. 司法的；与法院/审判有关的
   - en: relating to courts of law or the administration of justice
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. （名词）司法机关；法官（总称）
   - en: the branch of government that administers justice; judges collectively
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
judiciary = 法庭上那排高背椅，坐着的都穿着黑袍

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: iudic- (from Latin iudicium "judgment, court of justice," from iudex "a judge")
- suffix: -ary
- family: judge, judicial, judicature, judiciary

### Etymology Narrative
judiciary 作形容词约 15 世纪初出现，意为“与法院有关的”，直接借自拉丁语 iudiciarius“属于法庭的、司法的”，源自 iudicium“判决、法庭”，其根 iudex“法官”由 ius“法律、权利”+ dicere“说、宣布”构成；名词“法官团体、司法机关”义自 1788 年起（此前 judicature 自 1590 年代用此义）。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: iudic-（来自 iudex“法官”、iudicium“判决”）+ -ary（…的）= judiciary：司法的、法院的。

### Semantic Chain
说法断理 -> 断案的人 -> 定下判决 -> 司法的、与法院有关的

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline judiciary：adj. "relating to courts," early 15c., from Latin iudiciarius "of or belonging to a court of justice," from iudicium "judgment, court of justice," from iudicem "a judge" (see judge (n.)). The noun "a body of judges" is from 1788 (judicature used in this sense from 1590s). 整体拉丁借词 iudiciarius，-ary 为识别层后缀；judge/judicial 同源（ius + dicere），confidence: source-backed。
- confidence: source-backed

## juggle

### Identity
- lemma: juggle
- title: juggle
- pos: v
- ipa: /ˈdʒʌɡl/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: juggler, juggling

### Short Definition
玩杂耍；同时应付（多件事）；歪曲

### Core Definitions
1. 玩杂耍；（用手）连续抛接
   - en: to entertain by throwing and catching objects skilfully
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 同时应付；兼顾
   - en: to deal with several things at once
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B
3. 歪曲；篡改
   - en: to manipulate or present falsely
   - priority: 3
   - tags: B

### Prototype
juggle = 三个球轮流往上一抛，落下时手已经接住了下一个；他一手接电话一手签单，还腾出工夫回邮件

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: juggle (back-formation from juggler; from Latin ioculari "to jest," from iocus "jest")
- suffix:
- family: juggler, juggling, jocular

### Etymology Narrative
juggle 约 14 世纪末作 jogelen，意为“以滑稽表演或戏法娱人”，是 juggler 的逆成词；juggler 源自古法语 jogler“玩把戏、唱小曲”，来自晚期拉丁语 ioculare，最终源于拉丁语 ioculari“开玩笑、戏谑”（参见 jocular）。15 世纪中叶起专指手技杂耍，1940 年起比喻“兼顾多项事务”。 

### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 想象一个杂耍艺人（juggler）同时把几个球抛接在空中——juggle 就是“玩杂耍”，也引申为“同时应付好几件事”。（image 助记，非词源）

### Semantic Chain
戏耍 -> 耍把戏的手艺人 -> 倒推出来的说法 -> 玩杂耍、连续抛接

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline juggle (v.) late 14c., jogelen, "entertain by clowning or doing conjuring tricks," back-formation from juggler, and in part from Old French jogler, from Late Latin ioculare, from Latin ioculari "to jest" (see jocular). "deceive" sense from c. 1400; manual-dexterity sense mid-15c.; figurative "careers, husbands" by 1940. juggle 为 juggler 逆成词，非 jugg- + -le，故 suffix 留空；root 整体标注，confidence: source-backed。
- confidence: source-backed
