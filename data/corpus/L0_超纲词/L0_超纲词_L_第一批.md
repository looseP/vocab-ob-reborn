## laminated

### Identity
- lemma: laminated
- title: laminated
- pos: adj
- ipa: /ˈlæmɪneɪtɪd/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: laminate, laminar

### Short Definition
分层的；由薄层/薄片构成的

### Core Definitions
1. 由薄层或鳞片叠合而成的；分层的
   - en: disposed in or having laminae or scales; made of thin layers
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

### Prototype
laminated = 桌板侧面一道道压痕，撕开能揭下一片来

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: lamin- (from Latin lamina "thin plate, scale")
- suffix: -ated (past-participle adjective; < laminate)
- family: laminate, lamination, laminar

### Etymology Narrative
laminated 是 laminate（动词“覆以薄层”）的过去分词形容词，1660 年代起意为“由薄片/鳞片构成的”；laminate 源自拉丁语 lamina“薄板、鳞片”。同源形容词形式 laminate 亦见于 1660 年代。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: lamin- 来自拉丁语 lamina“薄板、鳞片”，-ated 是分词形容词；laminated 就是“一层层薄片叠成的”。

### Semantic Chain
薄薄一片 -> 一层层压上 -> 叠合而成 -> 分层的、由薄片叠成的

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline laminated (adj.) "disposed in or having laminae or scales," 1660s, past-participle adjective from laminate (v.); 另一形容词形式 laminate 亦 1660s，"having the form of a thin plate," as if from Latin *laminatus. -ated 为 laminate 加 -ed 的分词层，整体不拆 prefix。
- confidence: source-backed

## lineage

血统；世系；家族传承

### Core Definitions
1. 家族世系；血统传承
   - en: line of descent; ancestry; family line
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

### Prototype
lineage = 族谱从第一页排到最后一页，全是一脉下来的

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: line- (from Latin linea "line of descent, string")
- suffix: -age (noun-forming)
- family: line, lineal, lineally

### Etymology Narrative
lineage 晚期 17 世纪，源自中古英语 linage“世系、祖先”（约 1300 年），来自古法语 lignage“血统、种族”（11 世纪），由 ligne“线”而来，后者源自拉丁语 linea“血统线”（字面义“绳、线、线thread”）。早期现代英语中拼写/发音受 line 与 lineal 影响而变。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: line-（线，来自拉丁 linea“血统线”）+ -age（名词）= lineage：家族那条传承的线、血统。

### Semantic Chain
一条线 -> 一线相承 -> 父子相继 -> 家族世系、血统

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline lineage (n.) late 17c., from Middle English linage "line of descent; an ancestor" (c. 1300), from Old French lignage, from ligne "line," from Latin linea "line of descent." Altered by influence of line and lineal in early Modern English. 后缀 -age 为名词。
