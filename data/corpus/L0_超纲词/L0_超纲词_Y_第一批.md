## yearning

### Identity
- lemma: yearning
- title: yearning
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈjɜːnɪŋ/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: yearn, yearned

### Short Definition
渴望；向往；思念

### Core Definitions
1. 强烈的渴望、向往
   - en: feeling of one who yearns; strong desire or longing
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. （宗教/文学）渴慕
   - en: longing, covetousness
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
yearning = 隔着车窗挥手，直到站台缩成一个点

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: yearn (OE giernan "desire, strive"; PGmc *gernjan; PIE *gher- "to like, want")
- suffix: -ing
- family: yearn, yearned, yearningly

### Etymology Narrative
 yearning 为中古英语 yerning，是 yearn 的动名词，源自古英语 gierning，来自动词 yearn（西撒克逊 giernan，渴望、追求），出自原始日耳曼语 *gernjan，上接原始印欧语 *gher- (2)（喜爱、想要）。-ing 为名词化后缀。y- 非前缀。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: yearn（渴望，古英语 giernan）+ -ing（名词化）→ 隔着车窗挥手，直到站台缩成一个点

### Semantic Chain
想要 -> 心里惦着 -> 长久地惦念 -> 强烈的渴望、向往
