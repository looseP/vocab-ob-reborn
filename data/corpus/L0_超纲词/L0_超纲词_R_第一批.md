## raisin

### Identity
- lemma: raisin
- title: raisin
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈreɪzn/
- cefr: B1
- aliases: raisins

### Short Definition
葡萄干

### Core Definitions
1. 晒干的甜葡萄
   - en: a dried sweet grape
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

### Prototype
raisin = 葡萄晒了半个月，咬一口甜得发黏

### Morphology
- prefix: 
- root: raisin (from Anglo-French raycin, Old French raisin "grape", from Vulgar Latin *racimus, alteration of Latin racemus "cluster of grapes", probably from a lost Mediterranean substrate; cognate with Greek rhax "grape")
- suffix: 
- family: raisins, raceme, racemic

### Etymology Narrative
raisin 约1300年自盎格鲁-法语 raycin、古法语 raisin“葡萄”进入英语，源自通俗拉丁语 *racimus（拉丁语 racemus“葡萄串”的变体），可能借自某种已消亡的地中海底层语言（希腊语 rhax“葡萄”同源）；中古英语亦可用指葡萄本身。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: raisin 来自拉丁语 racemus“葡萄串”，raisin 就是晒干的葡萄。

### Semantic Chain
一串葡萄 -> 摘下晾晒 -> 晒得皱缩甘甜 -> 葡萄干

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline raisin: c. 1300, "dried sweet grape," from Anglo-French raycin (late 13c.), Old French raisin "grape; raisin," from Vulgar Latin *racimus, alteration of Latin racemus "cluster of grapes or berries" (also source of Spanish racimo, Italian racemo), probably a loan-word from the same ancient lost Mediterranean language that gave Greek rhax "grape, berry." In Middle English the word also could be used of grapes themselves.
- confidence: source-backed

Sense of "a rare thing or event ..." is from 1590s.

### Etymology Narrative

### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 想象煎锅里“刺啦”一片薄培根——rasher 就是那薄薄的一片火腿/培根。

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline rasher (n.) in cookery, "thin slice of bacon or ham," 1590s, a word of unknown origin. Perhaps from Middle English rash "to cut," variant of rase "to rub, scrape out, erase."
