## gape

### Identity
- lemma: gape
- title: gape
- pos: v
- ipa: /ɡeɪp/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: gape at, gaping

### Short Definition
张口；张大嘴；张口呆看

### Core Definitions
1. 张口；把嘴/眼大大张开
   - en: to open the mouth wide; to stare with open mouth
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 凝视；张口呆看
   - en: to stare in wonder or idle curiosity
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
gape = 把嘴/眼大大张开。

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: gape (from Old Norse gapa "to open wide"; related to gap)
- suffix:
- family: gap, gaped, gaping

### Etymology Narrative
gape 约 13 世纪初意为“张大嘴”，源自未记录的古英语词，或来自古诺斯语 gapa“张大嘴”（参见 gap 名词）；1530 年代起作名词“张口动作”。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: gape 与 gap（张口/缝隙）同源——嘴张成一个大 gap 就是 gape。

### Semantic Chain
张开口 -> 嘴合不拢 -> 呆呆地看着 -> 张口、瞠视

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline gape (v.) early 13c., from an unrecorded Old English word or else from Old Norse gapa "to open the mouth wide, gape" (see gap (n.)). As a noun "act of opening the mouth" from 1530s. 整体借自斯堪的纳维亚语或本土，无英语前缀。

   - en: a person who guards or controls a gate
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. （比喻）把关者；审查者
   - en: a person or thing that controls access or passage
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: gate + keeper
- suffix:

### Etymology Narrative

### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 想象站在大门（gate）前、手握钥匙的看守者（keeper）——gatekeeper 就是守门人。

### Semantic Chain
门口 -> 守在门边 -> 管着进出的人 -> 守门人、把关者

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline gatekeeper 直达页正文占位，采用基础词源页补证：gate (Old English geat "gate, door, opening"); keeper (c. 1300, agent noun from keep (v.)). 透明复合词，root 合并两自由语素，无 prefix/suffix。

   - en: having the power to produce or create
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 生殖的；繁殖的
   - en: relating to reproduction
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype

### Morphology

### Etymology Narrative

### Mnemonic

- text: generat（生育/产生，如 generate）+ -ive（…的）= generative：有生成能力的。

### Semantic Chain
族类 -> 生生不息 -> 产出新东西 -> 生成的、产生的

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline generative (adj.) late 14c., "reproductive, pertaining to propagation," from generate + -ive. generate (v.) c. 1500, from Latin generatus, past participle of generare "to beget, produce," from genus "race, kind" (PIE *gene-). -ive 为形容词后缀；generat 词基，不拆前缀。

   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: genetic（基因的）+ -ally（地）= genetically：从基因上看。

### Semantic Chain
来历开端 -> 由血统而来 -> 从遗传这一层说 -> 在基因上

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline genetically 直达页正文占位，采用基础词源页补证：genetic (adj.) 1831, coined by Carlyle as if from Greek genetikos from genesis "origin"; modern sense "pertaining to genetics or genes" from 1908 (see gene). gene (n.) 1911, from German Gen, from Greek genea "generation, race" (PIE *gene-). -ally 为副词后缀。

## gospel

- title: gospel
- pos: n

- cefr: B2

### Short Definition
福音；信条；绝对真理

### Core Definitions
1. 福音
   - en: the teachings of Jesus; one of the four Gospels
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 被视为真理的准则/信条
   - en: a doctrine held as of exclusive importance
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
gospel = 牧羊人报的那句话，一路传到了各个村子

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: god + spel (good spell: god "good" + spel "story, message")
- suffix:
- family: god, spell, evangel

### Etymology Narrative
gospel 源自古英语 godspel“耶稣宣布的喜讯；四福音书之一”，字面义“好消息”（good spell），由 god“好”与 spel“故事、消息”复合；是拉丁语 bona adnuntiatio 与希腊语 euangelion 的翻译。首音节原长元音“o”，后误与 God 关联成“上帝的故事”。 

### Mnemonic
- type: story
- text: god（好）+ spell（消息）= godspel，字面“好消息”，即福音；虽后来误会成 God's spell，但本义是 good spell。

### Semantic Chain
好消息 -> 口耳相传的佳音 -> 记下来的那一部 -> 福音

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline gospel (n.) Old English godspel "glad tidings announced by Jesus; one of the four gospels," literally "good spell," from god "good" + spel "story, message" (see spell (n.1)). Translation of Latin bona adnuntiatio / Greek euangelion. 透明复合词 god + spel，整体入 root。
- confidence: source-backed
