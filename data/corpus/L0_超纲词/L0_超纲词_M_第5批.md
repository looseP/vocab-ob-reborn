## multiplicity

### Identity
- lemma: multiplicity
- title: multiplicity
- pos: n
- ipa: /mʌltɪˈplɪsəti/
- cefr: C2
- aliases: multiplicitous

### Short Definition
多样性；多重性；大量

### Core Definitions
1. 多样性；多重性
   - en: the state of being manifold or various
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 大量；众多
   - en: a great number or variety
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
multiplicity = 摊子上摆了几十种花样，看花了眼

### Morphology
- prefix: multi-
- root: plic
- suffix: -ity
- family: multiple, multiply, plexus

### Etymology Narrative
multiplicity 于 15 世纪中叶进入英语，指“繁复、多样的状态”，源自古法语 multiplicité 或直译自晚期拉丁语 multiplicitas“多重性”，来自拉丁语 multiplic-（multiplex“多倍的”的词干，见 multiple）。其根 plic- 来自 plicare“折叠”。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: multi-（多）+ plic（折叠，Latin plicare）+ -ity（性质）= multiplicity：多重性。

### Semantic Chain
折叠 -> 一层又一层 -> 花样繁多 -> 多样性、多重性

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline multiplicity：mid-15c., from Late Latin multiplicitas, from Latin multiplic- (see multiple). multi- 为真实前缀（many）；root=plic（fold, from plicare）；-ity 为名词后缀；confidence: source-backed。
- confidence: source-backed

## mutability

### Identity
- lemma: mutability
- title: mutability
- pos: n
- ipa: /mjuːtəˈbɪləti/
- cefr: C2
- aliases: mutable

### Short Definition
易变；无常；可变性

### Core Definitions
1. 易变性；无常
   - en: the tendency to change; inconstancy
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A

### Prototype
mutability = 山里的天，早上出太阳晌午就飘雪

### Morphology
- prefix: 
- root: mutabil
- suffix: -ity
- family: mutable, mutate, mutation

### Etymology Narrative
mutability 于 14 世纪末进入英语，指“变化的倾向、无常”，源自古法语 mutabilité 及拉丁语 mutabilitas，来自动词 mutabilis“可变的”（见 mutable）。雪莱在《Mutability》（1816）中写道“万事皆流，唯有变易长存”。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: mutabil（可变的，Latin mutabilis < mutare ‘to change’）+ -ity = mutability：易变。

### Semantic Chain
改变 -> 说变就变 -> 没有定准 -> 易变性、无常

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline mutability：late 14c., from Latin mutabilitas, from mutabilis (see mutable). -ity 为名词后缀；root=mutabil（< L mutare）；confidence: source-backed。

## mutually

### Identity
- lemma: mutually
- title: mutually
- pos: adv
- ipa: /ˈmjuːtʃuəli/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: mutual, mutuality

### Short Definition
互相地；共同地

### Core Definitions
1. 互相地；互惠地
   - en: reciprocally; in a way of giving and receiving
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 共同地
   - en: conjointly, in common (held erroneous by some)
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
mutually = 你替我担一程，我替你扛一段

### Morphology
- prefix: 
- root: mutual
- suffix: -ly
- family: mutual, mutuality, mutually

### Etymology Narrative
mutually 于 1530 年代指“互惠地、有来有往地”，由 mutual（相互的）加 -ly 构成；“共同地、一起”（1590 年代起）曾被《世纪词典》等视为误用。mutually exclusive（互斥）一词自 1650 年代起有记录。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: mutual（相互的，Latin mutuus ‘reciprocal’）+ -ly = mutually：互相地。

### Semantic Chain
互换 -> 你来我往 -> 彼此相对 -> 互相地、交互地

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline mutually：1530s, from mutual + -ly (2); 'conjointly' sense (1590s) held erroneous by Century Dictionary. mutual < Latin mutuus 'reciprocal' (PIE *mei- 'to change'). -ly 为副词后缀；transparent derivative，base (mutual) etymology used；confidence: source-backed。

## mythical

### Identity
- lemma: mythical
- title: mythical
- pos: adj
- ipa: /ˈmɪθɪkl/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: mythic

### Short Definition
神话的；虚构的；只存在于传说中的

### Core Definitions
1. 神话的；传说中的
   - en: of, relating to, or characteristic of myths
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 虚构的；不存在的
   - en: existing only in myth; fictitious
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
mythical = 书里画着九条尾巴的狐狸，谁也没在林子里见过

### Morphology
- prefix: 
- root: myth
- suffix: -ical
- family: myth, mythic, mythology

### Etymology Narrative
mythical 于 1670 年代出现，与 mythic 同义，由 mythic（神话的）加 -al 构成；mythic 源自晚期拉丁语 mythicus，来自希腊语 mythikos，其根 mythos 即“神话、故事”（见 myth）。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: myth（神话，Greek mythos）+ -ical（…的）= mythical：神话的/虚构的。

### Semantic Chain
口传的故事 -> 古时的传说 -> 只见于传说的 -> 神话的、传说中的

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline mythical：1670s, same as mythic; see mythic + -al (1). mythic < Late Latin mythicus < Greek mythikos (mythos). -ical 为形容词后缀；transparent derivative，base (mythic/mythos) etymology used；confidence: source-backed。
- confidence: source-backed
