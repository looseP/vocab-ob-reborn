## keyword

### Identity
- lemma: keyword
- title: keyword
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈkiːwɜːd/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: key word, keywords

### Short Definition
关键词；用于检索或概括内容的核心词

### Core Definitions
1. （检索/索引用的）关键词
   - en: a word used as a key to retrieve or index information
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. 概括主题的核心词
   - en: a word that serves as a guide to the subject or contents
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
keyword = 在框里敲进这个词，整堆资料就翻了出来

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: key + word
- suffix:
- family: key, word, keywords

### Etymology Narrative
keyword 于 1807 年出现，由 key 的比喻义“开启/解释之键”与 word 复合而成，最初用于密码与密码本；1967 年起指信息检索系统中标示文档内容的核心词。key 源自古英语 cæg，word 源自古英语 word。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: key（关键的）+ word（词）= keyword：开启或索引内容的核心词。

### Semantic Chain
开锁的物件 -> 一句话 -> 用来检索的那个词 -> 关键词

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline keyword：1807, "word which serves as a guide to other words or matters," from key (n.1) in the figurative sense + word (n.). Originally in reference to codes and ciphers; information-retrieval sense 1967. 透明复合词，root 合并两自由语素 key + word，无 prefix/suffix。
- confidence: source-backed

## kinship

### Identity
- lemma: kinship
- title: kinship
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈkɪnʃɪp/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: kin, kinsman, kinfolk

### Short Definition
亲属关系；血缘/家族联系

### Core Definitions
1. 亲属关系；家族纽带
   - en: the relationship between family members; blood or family ties
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. （广义）同类事物的联系
   - en: a sharing of characteristics or origins; affinity
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
kinship = 两人一报家门，往上数竟是同一个太爷爷

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: kin (from Old English cynn "family, race")
- suffix: -ship
- family: kin, kinsman, kinfolk

### Etymology Narrative
kinship 约 1764 年由 kin 加名词后缀 -ship 构成，意为“亲属关系”；kin 源自古英语 cynn“家族、种族”，来自原始日耳曼语 *kunja-“家族”（PIE *gene- “生育、产生”，同 genus/genos）。relationship 同义但为 hybrid 词（拉丁+日耳曼），故 kinship 为纯日耳曼派生。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: kin（亲属）+ -ship（关系/状态）= kinship：亲属关系。

### Semantic Chain
同族 -> 血脉相连 -> 一家人的情分 -> 亲属关系、家族纽带

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline kinship：by 1764, from kin + -ship. Relationship covers the same sense but is a hybrid. kin 源自古英语 cynn "family, race," < PGmc *kunja- < PIE *gene- (cognate genus, genos). -ship 为本土名词后缀，整体派生，无 prefix。
- confidence: source-backed

## know-it-all

### Identity
- lemma: know-it-all
- title: know-it-all
- pos: n
- ipa: /ˈnəʊɪtɔːl/
- cefr: C1
- aliases: know-all, smart aleck

### Short Definition
自以为无所不知的人；自以为是者

### Core Definitions
1. 自认（或被认为）什么都懂的人
   - en: a person deemed (over)full of information or correct answers
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. （作定语）自以为是的态度
   - en: (attributive) an attitude of pretending to know everything
   - priority: 2
   - tags: B

### Prototype
know-it-all = 别人话没说完，他就把结论抢着说了

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: know + it + all
- suffix:
- family: know, all, know-all

### Etymology Narrative
know-it-all 于 1895 年出现，来自动词短语 know it all，由 know（知道）+ it + all（全部）复合而成；更早同义形式为 know-all（1862），而 Mr. Know-All 一称已见于班扬《圣战》（1682）。know 源自古英语 cnawan，all 源自古英语 eall。 

### Mnemonic
- type: image
- text: 想象一个人拍着胸脯说“这个我知道、那个我也全知道”——know-it-all 就是那种自认无所不知的人。

### Semantic Chain
全都知道 -> 样样都懂 -> 自以为没有不懂的 -> 自认什么都懂的人

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline know-it-all：1895, "one deemed (over)full of information or correct answers," from verbal phrase; see know (v.). Earlier know-all (1862); Mr. Know-All in Bunyan's The Holy War (1682). 透明复合，root 合并三自由语素 know + it + all，无 prefix/suffix。
- confidence: source-backed

## knuckle

### Identity
- lemma: knuckle
- title: knuckle
- pos: n/v
- ipa: /ˈnʌkl/
- cefr: B2
- aliases: knuckles, knuckle down, knuckle under

### Short Definition
指节；关节；（喻）屈服/认真投入

### Core Definitions
1. （手）指节；指关节
   - en: a finger joint, especially the rounded joint at the base of a finger
   - priority: 1
   - tags: A
2. （喻）knuckle down 认真去做；knuckle under 屈服
   - en: (figurative) to apply oneself earnestly; to submit or give in
   - priority: 2
   - tags: C

### Prototype
knuckle = 攥起拳头，手背上鼓出四个圆圆的凸起

### Morphology
- prefix:
- root: knuck (from Proto-Germanic *knuk- "bone, knobby joint")
- suffix: -le (diminutive)
- family: knuckle, knuckles, knuckleball

### Etymology Narrative
knuckle 约 14 世纪中叶作 knokel“指关节”出现，属日耳曼共同词（比较中古低地德语 knökel、中古荷兰语 cnockel、德语 Knöchel），字面义“小骨头”，是原始日耳曼语词根 *knuk-“骨”的指小形式（英语中 *knuk- 的简单形式不存，参见德语 Knochen“骨”）；与 knock（古英语 cnocian“敲”）同出一拟声词根。动词义 1740 年起（源自弹珠戏中将指节抵地），knuckle down“认真投入”1864 年美式英语，knuckle under“屈服”1740 年。 

### Mnemonic
- type: root
- text: knuck（*knuk- “骨”）+ -le（小）= knuckle：手指上那块“小骨头”——指节。

### Semantic Chain
骨头 -> 鼓出的骨节 -> 手指的关节 -> 指节、指关节

### Reviewer Notes
- Etymonline knuckle (n.) mid-14c., knokel "finger joint ... morbid lump"; Common Germanic (Middle Low German knökel, Middle Dutch cnockel, German Knöchel), literally "little bone," diminutive of PGmc *knuk- "bone" (not in simple English form; cf. German Knochen). Related to knock (OE cnocian, imitative). 词根 *knuk- 与 knock 同源；-le 为指小后缀，root 与 suffix 无字符重叠；k- 非前缀。
- confidence: source-backed
